# The Thorn-Crowned Stag

## Role

An ancient fey hunter and keeper of the Unpaid Hunt. The Stag holds the Shadow Fangs' old bargain and rules the [[Gloamfold Chase]], a fey domain where Moonpaw's wife and child remain trapped.

## Nature

The Thorn-Crowned Stag is an immortal fey shapeshifter, not a beast that happens to be magical. Its towering stag shape is a ceremonial hunting form, not its true body. It can appear as a lost traveler, a rain-soaked child, a white wolf, a raven with thorny antlers, a familiar voice in the mist, or an elegant hunter in worn green. Each form is a role in the story it wants someone else to enter.

In its true nature, the Stag is far beyond the party's ability to defeat. The fey character has heard tales of beings like it: ancient immortals who have outlived courts, wars, and ordinary ambition, then become obsessed with games, stories, bargains, or beautiful moments of danger because novelty is the only thing that still feels alive. They are not to be trifled with, threatened, or treated as a normal monster encounter.

The fey character recognizes the danger but does not know this being's true name, full history, loyalties, or every rule. That knowledge must be earned in play.

## Motivation

The Stag believes promises define reality. It learned of Moonpaw only when Tabaxi survivors he freed from the Shadow Fangs reached Dapplefern and told the village his story. The Stag now wants to test whether Moonpaw is a worthy runner, not conquest of the Verdant Expanse. Its cruelty comes from treating names, memories, and people as terms in a game rather than recognizing that no raider had the moral right to sell them.

It does not seek slaughter. Immortality has made effortless violence dull. It wants surprise, risk, courage, reversals, and a chase it can remember. A fair race is not mercy in the Stag's mind; it is the only kind of hunt worth having.

## Methods and Limits

- Hunts only quarry that is named, marked, invited, or follows its trail.
- Is not all-knowing; it learned Moonpaw existed only by overhearing freed Tabaxi survivors speak of him inside its realm.
- Holds Dapplefern inside its forest-realm, where every exit loops back until it recognizes a formally won race and opens a true path out.
- Sends velvet moths, white-antler tracks, false familiar voices, and recovered tokens to lure quarry into the Gloamfold.
- Is bound by witnessed bargains, formal challenges, and fey hospitality when they are correctly invoked.
- Captures rather than kills. Its living trophies are held in dark-amber pendants on its antler necklace.
- Chooses a limited shape for a declared hunt and is bound to that form's stated limits until the hunt ends. Its self-restraint is a matter of pride and entertainment, not weakness.
