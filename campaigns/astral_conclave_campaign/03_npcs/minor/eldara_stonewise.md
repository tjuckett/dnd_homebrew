# Eldara Stonewise

## Role
High chieftain of [[Stonehearth]] and primary leader of its earth elemental community.

## Goals
- Preserve the long-term safety and continuity of [[Stonehearth]]
- Guide the village through threats without sacrificing its traditions
- Seek trustworthy allies against spreading corruption and other destabilizing forces

## Personality
Ancient, deliberate, and wise. Eldara carries the authority of someone who has endured many eras and prefers measured action over reckless force.

## Secrets
She likely understands that Stonehearth cannot remain entirely isolated if corruption continues to spread into elemental lands.

## The Resonance Trammel

Eldara carries a Conclave-made **resonance trammel**: a long crystal-and-metal calibration rod designed to draw, measure, and store elemental power. It is currently bonded to her earth resonance and slowly draining her strength.

- Its drain is visible as amber light leaving the runes in Eldara's stone body and threading into the trammel's cracked crystal core.
- It cannot safely be torn away. A careless removal risks a violent resonance backlash that could injure Eldara, damage the Heartstone Altar, or release the stored energy unpredictably.
- The device is not merely harming Eldara; it is collecting a precise earth-elemental resonance profile. That makes it direct evidence of the same Conclave research that used genasi captives to build imperfect elemental-control systems.
- Eldara has endured the drain to keep the device contained and out of Conclave hands, but she is visibly weakened and cannot sustain it indefinitely.

## Immediate Need

The trammel needs a careful extraction, grounding ritual, or technical countermeasure. [[Crystalmender]] can stabilize Eldara temporarily, but the party will need to decide whether to disable the device at Stonehearth, trace its stored resonance back to the Conclave, or seek Kira's technical help.

## Hooks
- Can serve as a diplomatic patron or cautious ally for the players
- May ask outsiders to investigate threats earth elementals cannot ignore but do not fully understand
