# Sable Reedwhisper

## Role

Elected chief of [[Dapplefern Refuge]] and former Tabaxi scout.

## Goals

- Keep Dapplefern hidden and its people safe from the Unpaid Hunt of [[The Thorn-Crowned Stag]].
- Recover captives and preserve survivor testimony without turning the refuge into a battlefield.
- Decide whether limited support for an attack on [[Aetheris Island]] can protect people rather than sacrifice them.

## Personality

Patient, observant, and direct. Sable listens before speaking, asks for practical plans rather than heroic promises, and will accept hard choices only when the people bearing the cost have been heard.
