# Astral Conclave War-Artificer

## Role

An elite Conclave engineer trained to keep airships, portal machinery, and crystal weapons functioning under direct assault. A War-Artificer is almost as dangerous as a Sky-Captain, but controls the battlefield through lightning weapons, defensive emitters, and emergency repairs rather than command authority.

## Typical Use

- Replace an operations mage when the encounter needs a powerful technological officer
- Defend an airship engine, crystal housing, portal console, or siege device
- Force the party to choose between attacking the artificer and disabling the machinery being repaired

## Session 14

For the Wintervale Air Dock battle, replace the Rift Magister and one Field Technician with one War-Artificer. Do not add it to the entire existing roster unless the party has substantial allied support or the fight is deliberately being escalated.
