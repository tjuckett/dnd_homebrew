# Astral Conclave Sky-Captain

## Role

Senior Conclave field commander trusted with an airship, its crew, and cargo too valuable or unstable for routine portal transit. A Sky-Captain is not merely a pilot: they decide what escapes, what is sacrificed, and when the vessel's weapons are brought to bear.

## Wintervale Tactics

- Begins on the upper deck, between the helm and the deck batteries.
- Directs the ship system, uses legendary actions to reposition, and keeps the party divided between the ship and its objectives.
- Does not order indiscriminate fire into Wintervale; the air dock and town are useful Conclave infrastructure.
- If the ship cannot launch and the escape portal is gone, may surrender rather than destroy the vessel and its crew for pride.
