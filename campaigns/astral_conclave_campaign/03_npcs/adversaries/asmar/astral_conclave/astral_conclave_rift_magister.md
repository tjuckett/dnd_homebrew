# Astral Conclave Rift Magister

## Role
- Senior [[Astral Conclave]] extraction mage who shapes gravity and planar boundaries around active portals.
- Rift Magisters willingly support the harvesting operation because they believe forced evacuation is the only way to preserve Heliox's population and irreplaceable knowledge.
- They are not connected to the Heliox civic-control network and are unaffected by the Local Directive Projector.

## Portal Tactics
- Uses `Gravitic Fold` to drag characters away from the projector or into portal hazards.
- Uses `Banishment` on a projector carrier only when another defender can seize the unattended device.
- Repositions loyal defenders with `Vector Step` after the controlled Heliox shut down.
- Avoids indiscriminate area attacks when they would destroy the anchor or kill large numbers of citizens.
