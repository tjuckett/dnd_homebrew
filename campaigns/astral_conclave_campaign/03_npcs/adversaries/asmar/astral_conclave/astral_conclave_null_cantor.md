# Astral Conclave Null Cantor

## Role
- Loyal [[Astral Conclave]] battle-mage assigned to suppress hostile magic and keep extraction machinery under Conclave control.
- Null Cantors regard involuntary preservation as preferable to allowing an entire people to perish.
- They are not connected to the Heliox civic-control network and are unaffected by the Local Directive Projector.

## Portal Tactics
- Opens with `Slow` or `Signal Fracture` against characters carrying the projector.
- Preserves `Counterspell` for portal sabotage, mass movement, and attempts to protect the projector.
- Uses `Dispel Magic` against barriers or magical effects shielding an anchor.
- Retreats through the portal only when the assigned anchor system becomes unrecoverable.
