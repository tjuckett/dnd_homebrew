# Primal Engine

## Known Function

The Primal Engine is intended to open a portal to the elemental planes. It requires a rod entrusted to [[Eldara Stonewise]] of [[Stonehearth]] as a power source or key component.

## Current Status

An attempt to draw energy through the rod incapacitated Eldara. She is recovering, and it is not yet known whether the engine can be powered safely or whether the attempt caused further instability.

## Open Questions

- Where is the Primal Engine located?
- What exact energy and calibration does it require?
- Can it open a stable portal without harming its operator or the surrounding elemental community?
