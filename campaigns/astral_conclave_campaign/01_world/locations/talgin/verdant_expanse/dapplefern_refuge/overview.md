# Dapplefern Refuge

## Summary

[[Dapplefern Refuge]] is a concealed Tabaxi settlement in the [[Verdant Expanse]], founded by escapees, scattered survivors, and families who avoided the Shadow Fangs' raids. It is not the original village destroyed in the Tabaxi player character's backstory. Its people protect one another by keeping the refuge hidden, moving often enough to defeat old maps, and refusing to let outsiders turn survivors into resources.

## Location

Dapplefern lies within the **Veilroot Basin**, a bowl-shaped ravine beneath enormous, interwoven canopy trees. Homes are built among high root-bridges and concealed platforms while food stores, healing rooms, and evacuation tunnels sit within hollowed buttress roots below. From the forest floor, dense hanging vines, mist, and false game trails make the basin look like impassable marsh.

A visitor who arrives without an invitation finds abandoned decoy camps, flood channels that erase tracks, and the feeling of being watched. The refuge's exact location is never recorded in a portable ledger. Its people can collapse or reroute root bridges, move by canopy paths that leave no ground trail, and evacuate through water-worn tunnels if discovered.

## Current Situation

- The Shadow Fangs' old bargain with [[The Thorn-Crowned Stag]] trapped Dapplefern inside the Stag's forest-realm.
- No one can leave the realm. Every road, tunnel, and creek loops back unless the Stag recognizes a race victory.
- Moonpaw's wife and child are alive but held separately as living trophies in the Stag's [[Gloamfold Chase]].
- The refuge may become an ally against [[Aetheris Island]], but it will not sacrifice its people for a symbolic victory.

## Key Figures

- [[Sable Reedwhisper]]: Former scout and the refuge's elected chief. Patient but unsentimental, Sable refuses any request that risks the children, elders, or location of Dapplefern without a concrete plan for their safety.
- Other leaders, relatives, and survivors should be named with player input as the quest develops.
