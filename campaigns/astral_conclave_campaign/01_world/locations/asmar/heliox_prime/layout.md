# Heliox Prime Layout

## Summary
[[Heliox Prime]] is a circular industrial survivor-city built around efficiency, continuity, and energy discipline. Its layout reflects the Preservation Doctrine completely: no wasted plazas, no decorative districts without function, and no building allowed to exist unless it captures, stores, routes, defends, repairs, or preserves something the city needs.

## Core Design Logic
- Circular, concentric city plan.
- Every ring has a defined civic or industrial purpose.
- Major radial spokes carry freight, patrols, emergency routing, and maintenance access.
- Buildings are dense, shared-wall structures with little wasted volume.
- Heat, light, and signal are managed as carefully as food or ammunition would be in another city.
- If a structure exists, it must justify its energy cost.

## City Rings
### 1. Central Spire
- Seat of [[Arch-Calculant Veyrix]].
- Highest archive vaults.
- Deep command chambers.
- Restricted Lattice access.
- Administrative and strategic center of Heliox Prime.

### 2. Directive Ring
- Ministries.
- Dispatch authorities.
- Records offices.
- Security command.
- Dormancy scheduling and preservation policy offices.
- This is where Heliox procedure becomes civic law.

### 3. Lattice Ring
- Relay towers.
- Signal-smoothing stations.
- Broadcast nodes.
- Maintenance shafts.
- Energy-balancing hardware.
- Much of Heliox's hidden command architecture and obedience infrastructure runs through this ring.

### 4. Refinement Ring
- Crystal processing.
- Energy-core refinement.
- Charge regulation.
- Heat-recovery plants.
- Salvage and recycling for damaged construct parts.
- Loud, hot, and heavily controlled.

### 5. Logistics Ring
- Warehouses.
- Freight cranes.
- Transit sorting yards.
- Ration depots.
- Sealed preservation storage.
- The most plausible ring for suspicious warehouses, hidden broadcast hardware, and covert Conclave logistics.

### 6. Civic Habitation Ring
- Worker blocks.
- Shared recharge bays.
- Dormancy halls.
- Repair alcoves.
- Dense communal living infrastructure with minimal wasted private space.

### 7. Solar Mirror Ring
- Outer industrial band of mirrored arrays.
- Light capture towers.
- Heat redirection vanes.
- Solar focusing systems.
- Defensive and power-generation infrastructure at once.

### 8. Outer Service Rim
- Intake checkpoints.
- Waste reclamation.
- Vehicle berths.
- Maintenance yards.
- Failed infrastructure projects and obsolete utility sites.
- Best place for covert approaches, abandoned service works, or hidden meetings like the old solar farm.

## Radial Spokes
- Major spoke-lines cut across all rings.
- These carry freight, patrols, emergency traffic, and sealed maintenance access.
- In lockdown, the spokes can be segmented and isolated.
- This lets Heliox trap movement district by district without needing to shut the whole city at once.

## City Feel
- Brass, black iron, mirrored glass, and heat haze.
- Narrow streets where there is no need for broad transit.
- Bigger open spaces exist only when they serve routing, thermal regulation, or industrial handling.
- Little ornament unless it also reflects heat, stores energy, or channels light.
- The city feels like a machine that became architecture.

## District Logic
If someone asks why a district or building exists in Heliox, the answer should usually be one of these:

- energy capture
- energy storage
- routing
- labor
- repair
- control
- preservation

## Use In Play
- The outer service rim is best for covert entry, secret meetings, or abandoned infrastructure.
- The logistics ring is best for warehouse investigation, hidden freight movement, and trap sites disguised as civic storage.
- The Lattice ring is best for broadcast nodes, signal mysteries, and obedience-architecture reveals.
- The directive ring is best for procedure, bureaucracy, and the feeling that paperwork itself has become a weapon.
