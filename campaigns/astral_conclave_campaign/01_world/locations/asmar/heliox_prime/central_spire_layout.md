# Central Spire

## Summary
The [[Central Spire]] is the administrative, archival, and command heart of [[Heliox Prime]]. Its upper twenty floors form a deliberate tapering tower of square halls and increasingly restricted rooms. Beneath the upper security checkpoint, the spire incorporates older structures built in different eras, producing an irregular descent through ministries, archives, utilities, ceremonial spaces, and the throne complex. The lowest floors have been converted into the current population-intake and portal operation.

## Established And Added Details
### Established In Play
- The party landed on the top of the spire.
- They descended roughly `20` flights through restricted square floors.
- They encountered a staffed security checkpoint after that descent.
- Beyond the checkpoint, they continued downward on a spiral staircase.
- They eventually reached the throne-room level, bypassed its visible guards, and entered the halls behind it.
- They defeated two heavy guards there without raising the wider alarm.
- The throne room is above the active portal chamber.
- Heliox's populace is being funneled into the spire through its base.

### Added Layout Decision
- The notes do not establish the number of flights between the checkpoint and throne room.
- This layout sets that distance at `15` flights:
  - security checkpoint: Level `20`,
  - throne room: Level `5`.
- The portal chamber is on Level `2`, only `3` floors beneath the throne room.
- The public intake concourse is on Level `1`.
- One foundation level beneath the city supports the portal and spire power systems.

## Floor Numbering
- `Roof`: exterior crown platform where the party landed.
- Levels `40-21`: tapering square upper spire.
- Level `20`: upper security checkpoint and architectural transition.
- Levels `19-6`: older, irregular inner spire.
- Level `5`: throne and continuity-audit complex; current party position.
- Levels `4-1`: portal support, portal chamber, and public intake.
- Level `B1`: foundation machinery and portal-anchor roots.

## Upper-Spire Geometry: Levels 40-21
- These twenty floors are stacked squares that become larger toward the bottom.
- Approximate width:
  - Level `40`: `45 feet` across.
  - Each descending floor adds roughly `5 feet` of usable width.
  - Level `21`: roughly `140 feet` across.
- A paired central staircase descends through the middle of every upper floor.
- A square loop hallway surrounds the staircase core.
- On the narrowest floors, the loop nearly touches the central stairs.
- On broader floors, rooms exist both:
  - outside the loop along the four exterior faces,
  - and inside the loop around the staircase core.
- The top floor has only four shallow rooms: one centered on each side of the spire.
- Each lower floor adds subdivisions, storage cells, workrooms, and inner offices without changing the basic square circulation pattern.
- Exterior windows become wider and more numerous as the spire broadens, but most are mirrored or shuttered from within.

## Vertical Circulation
### Crown Stair: Levels 40-20
- A paired central stair descending through the square upper floors.
- Landings alternate orientation so someone looking down cannot see more than one floor at a time.
- Emergency shutters can seal each landing independently.

### Sovereign Spiral: Levels 20-5
- Begins behind the security checkpoint.
- Older than the upper tower and visibly rebuilt several times.
- Sometimes hugs an outer wall, sometimes winds around utility shafts, and occasionally crosses a floor through an exposed gallery.
- Its changing placement explains the irregular lower-floor layouts.

### Throne-To-Portal Routes: Levels 5-1
- **Ceremonial descent:** broad processional stairs connecting the throne approach to the public intake concourse.
- **Command stair:** guarded stair from the rear throne complex to the portal-control gallery.
- **Service lift:** concealed freight platform linking Levels `5`, `3`, `2`, and `1`; large enough to transport the Local Directive Projector.
- **Anchor ladders:** narrow maintenance access from Level `3` to `B1`.

## Complete Floor Guide

Printable tactical schematics for Levels `1-5` are available in [Central Spire Lower-Floor Maps](floors_01_05.md).

### Roof - Crown Landing
- Flat mirrored landing platform ringed by four lightning vanes.
- Contains tie-down points for small flying craft, maintenance harnesses, and a sealed hatch to Level `40`.
- No permanent guard post; security relies on altitude, alarms, and the restricted floors below.
- This is where the party entered the spire.

### Level 40 - Crown Watch
- Narrowest occupied floor.
- One shallow room along each of the four exterior sides:
  - north: long-range optical watch,
  - east: wind and atmospheric instruments,
  - south: solar-load monitor,
  - west: roof-access and emergency equipment.
- The square hallway is little more than a tight circuit around the central stairs.
- No inner rooms beyond the staircase enclosure.

### Level 39 - Astral Survey
- Four exterior observation rooms track stars, planar disturbances, and high-altitude traffic.
- A small inner timing room synchronizes the instruments.
- The square hall contains brass markers for celestial bearings.

### Level 38 - Solar Alignment
- Controls calibration data for Heliox's outer solar mirrors.
- Exterior rooms contain sighting scopes facing the cardinal directions.
- Two cramped inner rooms store alignment plates and emergency shutter controls.

### Level 37 - Atmospheric Calculation
- Tracks sandstorms, thermal shifts, lightning, and visibility over Asmar.
- Outer rooms contain weather lenses and barometric engines.
- Inner rooms hold prediction drums that click continuously in changing rhythms.

### Level 36 - Aerial Defense Observation
- Watches approach corridors and designates targets for distant mirror defenses.
- Contains no large weapons; it is an observation and authorization floor.
- Four outer tracking rooms surround two inner authentication booths.

### Level 35 - High Dock Control
- Coordinates maintenance skiffs, courier constructs, and emergency rooftop arrivals.
- Outer rooms contain traffic maps and docking signals.
- Inner rooms include a compact rescue-gear locker and damaged-flight triage alcove.

### Level 34 - Restricted Signal Laboratory
- Tests secure civic transmissions and interference resistance.
- Outer rooms are shielded signal cells.
- Inner rooms contain tuning benches, frequency records, and removable relay crystals.
- Recent Conclave work may have copied Aetherbind wrist-device handshake data here.

### Level 33 - Long-Range Communications
- Handles transmissions beyond Heliox and across Asmar.
- Contains four directional communication rooms and a ring of inner cipher booths.
- One booth has been quietly redirected to Conclave traffic.

### Level 32 - Continuity Backup Vault
- Stores current copies of civic law, population counts, energy budgets, and emergency succession rules.
- Outer rooms are sealed record vaults.
- Inner rooms house comparison engines that detect altered or missing records.

### Level 31 - Executive Refuge
- Emergency shelter for senior civic leadership during attack or system collapse.
- Contains four compact private dormancy rooms, shared repair equipment, and an independent air and charge supply.
- Inner rooms include a crisis table and sealed escape-control console.

### Level 30 - Diplomatic Overlook
- Receives highly trusted foreign envoys before they are allowed deeper into the spire.
- Four restrained audience rooms face the city.
- Inner rooms allow observers and translators to monitor conversations unseen.

### Level 29 - Strategic Projection Hall
- Holds citywide tactical maps, convoy routes, mine reports, and defense projections.
- The outer square is divided into four regional theaters.
- Inner rooms contain map engines and scenario archives.

### Level 28 - Forecasting Engines
- Runs long-term calculations for crystal depletion, dormancy needs, and population survival.
- Outer rooms contain calculation banks.
- Inner rooms house the assumptions, weighting crystals, and manual overrides that determine the forecasts.
- Veyrix's harshest preservation projections originate here.

### Level 27 - Archive Index
- Does not hold the most sensitive records; it tells authorized users where those records are stored.
- Outer rooms contain era-specific catalogues.
- Inner rooms hold access keys and record-transfer lifts.
- A Sovereign-era query here could point toward the memory vault below.

### Level 26 - Memory Restoration Offices
- Used to repair damaged civic memories and verify identity continuity.
- Exterior rooms are quiet diagnostic chambers.
- Inner rooms store temporary memory prisms and consent records.
- Conclave personnel have searched this floor for command-resistance cases.

### Level 25 - Pre-Partition Records
- Contains copies of laws and decisions from before Heliox and Calyxis separated.
- Outer vaults are arranged by historical period.
- Inner reading rooms include obsolete interfaces that may recognize the construct PC.

### Level 24 - Directive Drafting
- Civic instructions are written, tested, and checked for contradiction here before broadcast.
- Exterior rooms belong to separate ministries.
- Inner rooms contain language-testing engines and legal review stations.
- The distinction between legitimate civic instruction and coercive command is visible in the floor's records.

### Level 23 - Emergency Command
- Coordinates city response to attack, fire, energy collapse, or mass dormancy.
- Outer rooms correspond to the major city rings.
- Inner rooms contain sealed emergency-authority consoles.
- Several consoles now carry unauthorized Conclave priority layers.

### Level 22 - Crown Guard Readiness
- Barracks, recharge alcoves, equipment lockers, and briefing rooms for elite spire guards.
- Outer rooms provide quick access to four sides of the floor.
- Inner rooms surround the stairs and allow guards to intercept anyone descending.
- The party passed while staffing was reduced by the portal operation below.

### Level 21 - Screening Approach
- Last conventional square floor before the checkpoint.
- Contains waiting squares, identity scan arches, weapon-seal lockers, and interview booths.
- Its larger footprint allows visitors to be separated into four lanes.
- All lanes converge on the checkpoint below.

### Level 20 - Upper Security Checkpoint
- Broad square floor marking the base of the modern upper tower.
- Central security desk monitors the Crown Stair, elevators, shutters, and access logs.
- Side rooms include:
  - guard office,
  - detention room,
  - evidence locker,
  - alarm relay,
  - and staff recharge alcove.
- The party silently neutralized the lone person staffing the desk.
- Beyond the desk, circulation changes from the central Crown Stair to the irregular Sovereign Spiral.

## Irregular Inner Spire: Levels 19-6
- These floors incorporate several generations of construction.
- Walls do not align cleanly from one floor to the next.
- Some corridors end at sealed former doorways or descend half a level before rising again.
- Old square foundations remain visible beneath later curved, triangular, and wedge-shaped additions.
- The Sovereign Spiral changes position repeatedly as it passes around older machinery and sealed chambers.

### Level 19 - Transfer Vestibule
- Architectural seam between the modern upper tower and older inner spire.
- Contains decontamination arches, visitor rerouting gates, and redundant identity checks.
- A half-circle corridor feeds into the Sovereign Spiral on the western side.
- Several doors appear functional but open only into old sealed wall cavities.

### Level 18 - Senior Staff Residences
- Living quarters for senior clerks, policy calculators, archive supervisors, and overnight command staff.
- Corridors bend around preexisting support columns, producing dead ends and narrow diagonal passages.
- Contains:
  - twelve private dormancy rooms,
  - two shared recharge salons,
  - personal repair alcoves,
  - a communal strategy-game room,
  - and a small duty office handling energy exemptions and emergency staffing.
- Mostly empty during the current crisis because residents have been reassigned, controlled, or sent toward intake.

### Level 17 - Active Registry Stacks
- Stores recent civic orders, transit permissions, repair assignments, and citizen-location records.
- Tall rotating shelves create shifting lanes.
- A document lift connects to Levels `27`, `16`, and `9` but is too small for most creatures.
- Current records show the population being reassigned toward the spire base.

### Level 16 - Deep Memory Stacks
- Holds older personal memory deposits, testimony, and identity-verification records.
- Rooms are arranged radially around a cylindrical cold vault that does not align with the stair.
- Several narrow bridges cross the open archive cylinder.
- The most restricted Sovereign material has been removed or copied elsewhere.

### Level 15 - Tribunal And Review Chambers
- Former site of civic hearings, command-abuse reviews, and disputes over construct autonomy.
- Contains one large irregular chamber and six wedge-shaped consultation rooms.
- Old inscriptions emphasize that emergency authority must remain reviewable.
- Useful place to foreshadow the Local Directive Projector's moral danger.

### Level 14 - General Staff Living Quarters
- Primary residential floor for technicians, archivists, attendants, and rotating administrative staff assigned to the spire.
- Built as two offset residential blocks joined by a narrow diagonal common hall.
- Contains:
  - shared dormancy alcoves,
  - regulated recharge bays,
  - communal repair benches,
  - cleaning and plating-maintenance stations,
  - personal lockers,
  - a quiet memory-journal room,
  - and a small social chamber overlooking the city.
- Several unused alcoves are sealed to conserve power.
- A few controlled residents may still be waiting motionless for reassignment or instructions.

### Level 13 - Thermal Exchange Floor
- Large pipes carry recovered heat downward and cooled conduits upward.
- Catwalks and maintenance platforms replace normal halls.
- The Sovereign Spiral crosses the main chamber on an exposed iron bridge.
- Noise and heat make this a good place to evade pursuit or stage an ambush.

### Level 12 - Service Junction
- Connects freight lifts, waste chutes, message tubes, and maintenance passages from multiple eras.
- No two walls meet at a clean right angle.
- Contains the first concealed access to the service lift that reaches the portal complex.
- Conclave technicians have marked a route downward with small violet maintenance sigils.

### Level 11 - Old War Command Bunker
- Reinforced chamber dating to the conflict that preceded the current civic division.
- Contains outdated tactical tables, sealed armories, and hardened communication booths.
- A circular command room sits inside a much older square shell.
- Some obsolete systems may still recognize Sovereign-era authority.

### Level 10 - Accord History Gallery
- Ceremonial exhibits describing the creation of construct civic independence and the old Accord.
- The intended procession doubles back through several angled galleries.
- Later Heliox additions reinterpret older unity through the Preservation Doctrine.
- Displays involving the lost Sovereign have been covered, edited, or moved.

### Level 9 - Ministry Annex Maze
- Overflow offices for preservation policy, labor allocation, and infrastructure denial.
- Repeated renovation created short stairs, mismatched ceiling heights, and rooms accessible only through other offices.
- Contains operational evidence of covert sabotage against Calyxis.
- Also contains forged work orders used by the Conclave to redirect citizens.

### Level 8 - Executive Repair And Dormancy
- Secure repair suites and dormancy chambers for Veyrix and other high officials.
- Built around an off-center circular core with four uneven wings.
- Includes private living chambers, personal archives, medical tools, replacement plating, protected memory backups, and an emergency isolation room.
- Veyrix's quarters are austere: one dormancy frame, one calculation desk, one wall of civic projections, and no purely decorative objects.
- A hidden stair connects directly to the rear throne complex.

### Level 7 - Inner Guard Barracks
- Houses the guards assigned to the throne, archives, and portal-control approaches.
- Contains armories, shared dormancy alcoves, recharge frames, personal lockers, repair stations, briefing pits, a small common room, and a narrow training court.
- The floor is divided by defensible choke points rather than convenient circulation.
- Many guards have been reassigned to population intake and portal security.

### Level 6 - Throne Vestibules
- Formal waiting chambers, petition rooms, honor-guard posts, and controlled approaches to the throne.
- A broad ceremonial corridor curves downward despite the surrounding spire remaining square.
- Side passages lead to servant-equivalent maintenance rooms and guard galleries.
- The party used the less-visible approach to bypass the main throne guards.

## Throne And Portal Complex: Levels 5-B1

### Level 5 - Throne And Continuity Audit
- Closest thing Heliox has to a royal or executive floor.
- Main spaces:
  - public throne chamber,
  - side guard galleries,
  - petition and strategy alcoves,
  - rear executive hallway,
  - Continuity Audit Chamber,
  - and command-stair access toward the portal complex.
- The throne room is severe and functional rather than lavish:
  - Veyrix's seat faces a city-status display,
  - petitioners stand on marked calculation positions,
  - and every decorative surface also serves as a map, heat sink, acoustic panel, or signal conductor.
- The Continuity Audit Chamber behind it contains:
  - the live model of Heliox,
  - incoming traces from the five remote control devices,
  - portal-intake counts,
  - the Local Directive Projector in its charging cradle,
  - and access to the concealed service lift.
- The party is currently in the rear complex after defeating two heavy guards under silence.

### Level 4 - Withdrawal Stores And Portal Security
- Recently converted into the Conclave's final withdrawal staging floor.
- Contains:
  - packed records and stolen components,
  - portable power-core cradles,
  - portal-security equipment,
  - ammunition and repair supplies,
  - temporary holding cells,
  - and a muster room for the last personnel scheduled to leave.
- Original Heliox function was secure civic continuity storage.
- Corridors are short and heavily barricaded because this is the last defensible floor above the portal.
- The command stair from Level `5` and service lift both stop here.

### Level 3 - Portal Control And Anchor Gallery
- Wraparound mechanical gallery overlooking parts of the portal chamber below.
- Contains:
  - three portal-anchor control stations,
  - Null-Key Shroud controls,
  - phase-key authentication pedestal,
  - destination and transit logs,
  - emergency closure hardware,
  - and maintenance access to the portal frame.
- Any `2` of the `3` anchors are sufficient to maintain the portal; the third is a failsafe.
- Disabling one station transfers load to the other two.
- Disabling a second breaks the `Null-Key Shroud` and makes the portal closable by an Aetherbind wrist device.
- The portal is not the source of the population-control signal.
- The Shroud provides the shared timing reference that makes the five remote signals overlap coherently at the intake.
- Disabling the Shroud allows the party's wrist devices to attempt a safe closure.
- Narrow bridges and balconies make this the best location for technicians, ranged defenders, and the phase-key handler.

### Level 2 - Grand Portal Chamber
- Vast, high-ceilinged room occupying most of the spire's footprint.
- The active portal stands against the north foundation wall inside a reinforced arch.
- Three physical anchors form a triangle around it:
  - west floor anchor,
  - east floor anchor,
  - and upper anchor connected to the Level `3` gallery.
- The portal needs only `2` functioning anchors, making the third redundant rather than additive.
- The party can expose the portal by disabling any `2` anchors; they never need to reach all three.
- Once exposed, an Aetherbind wrist device can perform the actual closure.
- Two broad intake ramps rise from Level `1` and feed citizens toward the portal.
- Side lanes allow Conclave personnel and equipment to bypass the main population queue.
- Floor markings divide the room into:
  - intake lanes,
  - inspection areas,
  - portal threshold,
  - emergency exclusion zone,
  - and loading corridor.
- This is the likely climax location for the Local Directive Projector encounter.

### Level 1 - Grand Intake Concourse
- Ground-level base of the spire and only floor designed to receive large numbers of ordinary citizens.
- Four civic entrances correspond to the city's major radial approaches.
- Current Conclave use:
  - citizens enter through separated lanes,
  - pass identity and core scans,
  - surrender tools or weapons,
  - and are directed up the two ramps to the portal chamber.
- Original Heliox use:
  - public petitions,
  - emergency shelter processing,
  - mass dormancy registration,
  - and civic reassignment during infrastructure crises.
- Shutters can divide the concourse into four independent crowd pens.
- Public exits can be opened from Level `3` or manually from guarded mechanisms on this floor.

### Level B1 - Foundation And Anchor Roots
- Subterranean machinery level beneath the portal chamber.
- Contains:
  - portal-anchor roots,
  - crystal power buses,
  - heat sinks,
  - structural counterweights,
  - emergency discharge channels,
  - and old foundation passages predating the modern spire.
- Extremely hot, loud, and hazardous while the portal is active.
- Destroying machinery here can collapse the portal physically but risks uncontrolled discharge and harm to creatures in transit.
- One old Sovereign-era conduit continues deeper beneath Heliox, providing a possible escape or future route toward buried Accord infrastructure.

## Fast Routes From The Party To The Portal
### Concealed Service Lift
- Starts in the Continuity Audit Chamber on Level `5`.
- Stops at Levels `4`, `3`, `2`, and `1`.
- Best route for transporting the Local Directive Projector.
- Using it is fast but may notify the portal-control gallery unless the party spoofs the destination signal.

### Command Stair
- Descends from the rear throne hall through Level `4` to Level `3`.
- More defensible and quieter than the lift.
- Too narrow to carry the deployed projector, though the folded device fits.

### Ceremonial Route
- Exits through the throne room and follows broad stairs toward the public intake concourse.
- Longest route and most heavily watched.
- Gives the best view of the population funnel and the scale of the crisis.

### Anchor Maintenance Route
- Accessible from the Level `3` gallery.
- Descends into `B1` and reaches the portal's physical power roots.
- Avoids the controlled crowd but bypasses the portal floor, phase-key handler, and captives.

## Living Quarters
- The Central Spire is primarily a government and command structure, not a general residential tower.
- It nevertheless houses officials, guards, technicians, archivists, and emergency personnel who must remain on site through long duty cycles.
- Construct living quarters usually contain:
  - private or shared dormancy alcoves,
  - regulated recharge connections,
  - repair and cleaning stations,
  - locked personal-storage compartments,
  - memory-journal terminals,
  - and communal rooms for conversation, games, study, or quiet activity.
- The major residential floors are:
  - Level `31`: emergency executive refuge,
  - Level `18`: senior administrative living quarters,
  - Level `14`: general staff living quarters,
  - Level `8`: Veyrix and high-official quarters,
  - Level `7`: inner guard barracks.
- Most ordinary Heliox citizens live in the Civic Habitation Ring rather than inside the spire.
