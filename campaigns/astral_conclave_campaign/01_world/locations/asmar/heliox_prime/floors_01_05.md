# Central Spire Lower-Floor Maps

## Map Conventions
- Each minor grid square represents `5 feet`.
- North is at the top.
- Brass lines are structural walls.
- Orange breaks are doors, gates, or shutters.
- Cyan areas are stairs, ramps, or lifts.
- Violet features are active Conclave or portal systems.
- Dashed lines indicate balconies, overhead structures, or controlled crowd lanes rather than full walls.
- Furniture and machinery show tactical position, not every minor object in the room.

## Vertical Alignment
- The concealed service lift occupies the east side of every mapped floor.
- The command stair connects Levels `5`, `4`, and `3`.
- The Level `3` anchor gallery overlooks the Level `2` portal chamber.
- Two intake ramps connect the Level `1` concourse to the south side of Level `2`.
- Anchor-maintenance ladders connect Level `3` to the machinery on Level `B1`.

## Simplified Anchor Rule
- The portal has `3` anchors: west, east, and upper.
- It requires `2` functioning anchors.
- The third anchor is a failsafe in case one is disabled or damaged.
- First anchor disabled:
  - the portal remains open,
  - the other two flare and take the additional load,
  - and the Conclave immediately knows the redundancy has been lost.
- Second anchor disabled:
  - only one remains,
  - the anchor network can no longer sustain the `Null-Key Shroud`,
  - and the portal becomes closable by the party's Aetherbind wrist devices.
- The party may disable anchors through the Level `3` control stations or destroy the physical machinery on Level `2`.
- They can mix methods and choose whichever two positions are easiest to reach.
- After the second anchor goes offline, one party member must maintain a wrist-device connection for one full round to close the portal.
- The six possible interaction points and their encounter plans are detailed in [Portal-Anchor Encounters](portal_anchor_encounters.md).

## Current Tactical Route
- The party begins on Level `5`, immediately outside the Continuity Audit Chamber.
- The fastest route for moving the folded Local Directive Projector is the concealed service lift:
  - Level `5` audit chamber,
  - Level `4` withdrawal stores,
  - Level `3` control gallery,
  - Level `2` portal chamber,
  - Level `1` intake concourse.
- The quieter route is the command stair from Level `5` to Level `3`, followed by a gallery stair into Level `2`.
- The service lift can be stopped or ambushed at Level `4`.
- Reaching Level `3` first gives the party a chance to disable the Null-Key Shroud before deploying onto the portal floor.
