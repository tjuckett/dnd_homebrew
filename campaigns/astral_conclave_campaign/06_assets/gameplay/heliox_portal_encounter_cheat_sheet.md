# Heliox Portal Encounter Cheat Sheet

Use with [Portal Encounter Stat Blocks](heliox_portal_encounter_statblocks.md) and [Portal-Anchor Encounter Plans](../../01_world/locations/asmar/heliox_prime/portal_anchor_encounters.md).

## Field Rule

- **Controlled Heliox:** Shut down immediately when caught in the deployed Local Directive Projector field. Remove them from initiative but leave their tokens on the map.
- **Loyal defenders:** Astral Conclave personnel remain active and continue fighting.
- **Second target:** Add the pursuit package after the party disables its first anchor system.
