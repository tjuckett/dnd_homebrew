# Annex Veil Escort

*Medium humanoid (any race), lawful neutral · CR 2 (`450 XP`)*

| AC | HP | Speed |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `15` | `44` (`8d8 + 8`) | `30 ft.` |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `13` (`+1`) | `16` (`+3`) | `12` (`+1`) | `12` (`+1`) | `12` (`+1`) | `10` (`+0`) |

- **Saves** Dex `+5`; **Skills** Acrobatics `+5`, Perception `+3`, Stealth `+5`
- **Senses** passive `13`; **Languages** Common

## Traits

**Extraction Discipline.** While within `60 feet` of the Annex Relay Handler, the escort can Dash or Disengage as a bonus action.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Two Veil Saber attacks.

**Veil Saber.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+5` to hit, reach `5 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `6` (`1d6 + 3`) slashing plus `3` (`1d6`) force.

**Signal Bolt.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+5` to hit, range `60 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `10` (`2d6 + 3`) force.

**Veil Screen (Recharge 5-6).** Create a `15-foot-radius` cloud of silver-black static within `30 feet`. It is heavily obscured until the end of the escort's next turn; Conclave creatures see through it as lightly obscured.

## Reaction

**Cover the Handler.** When the Annex Relay Handler within `5 feet` is hit, the escort can be hit instead.
