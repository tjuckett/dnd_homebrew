# Annex Purge Technician

*Medium humanoid (any race), neutral evil · CR 1 (`200 XP`)*

| AC | HP | Speed |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `13` | `27` (`6d8`) | `30 ft.` |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `9` (`-1`) | `14` (`+2`) | `10` (`+0`) | `16` (`+3`) | `12` (`+1`) | `11` (`+0`) |

- **Saves** Int `+5`; **Skills** Arcana `+5`, Investigation `+5`, Sleight of Hand `+4`
- **Senses** passive `11`; **Languages** Common, Primordial

## Trait

**Extraction Crew.** The technician can use an action to advance extraction one step by assisting at the gate or moving the restraint cradle. Extraction can advance only once per round from any source.

## Actions

**Signal Prod.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+4` to hit, reach `5 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `5` (`1d6 + 2`) piercing plus `3` (`1d6`) lightning.

**Arc Spark.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+5` to hit, range `60 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `7` (`2d6`) lightning.

**Purge Crystal (1/Day).** Target an unattended document bank, evidence case, or console within `5 feet`. A creature can stop the purge with an action and DC `13` Sleight of Hand, Arcana, or relevant tools check. Otherwise, at the start of the technician's next turn mundane documents are destroyed and a device loses one recoverable clue.

## Bonus Action

**Pack Component.** Pack one Tiny or Small relay component within `5 feet`; it is unavailable as evidence unless the technician is stopped and searched.
