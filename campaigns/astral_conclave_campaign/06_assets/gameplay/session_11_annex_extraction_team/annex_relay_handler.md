# Annex Relay Handler

*Medium humanoid (any race), lawful evil · CR 3 (`700 XP`)*

| AC | HP | Speed |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `14` | `52` (`8d8 + 16`) | `30 ft.` |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `9` (`-1`) | `14` (`+2`) | `14` (`+2`) | `17` (`+3`) | `13` (`+1`) | `14` (`+2`) |

- **Saves** Int `+5`, Wis `+3`; **Skills** Arcana `+5`, Deception `+4`, Investigation `+5`
- **Senses** passive `11`; **Languages** Common, Primordial, telepathy `30 ft.` through relay devices

## Traits

**Breach-Gate Operator.** While adjacent to the breach-gate console, the handler can use an action to advance extraction one step. Extraction can advance only once per round from any source. If the handler is incapacitated, another creature must operate the console for extraction to continue.

**Mission Preservation.** Advantage on saves against charmed or frightened while within `30 feet` of a relay device or breach-gate console.

## Actions

**Relay Dagger.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+4` to hit, reach `5 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `5` (`1d6 + 2`) piercing plus `7` (`2d6`) psychic.

**Command Static.** One creature within `60 feet` makes a DC `13` Wisdom save, taking `10` (`3d6`) psychic on a failure, or half on a success. On a failure, it cannot take reactions until the handler's next turn.

**Signal Snare (Recharge 5-6).** Creatures of the handler's choice within `10 feet` of a point within `60 feet` make DC `13` Strength saves or are restrained by hard-light bands until the handler's next turn. A restrained creature can use an action to repeat the save.

## Bonus Action

**Scrub Trace.** One console, document bank, or evidence case within `30 feet` gives disadvantage on checks to recover evidence until the handler's next turn.

## Reaction

**Redirect Pulse.** When hit while within `30 feet` of a relay device, gain `+2` AC against that attack.
