# High Curator Malrec Voss, Leader of the Astral Conclave
*Medium humanoid, lawful evil*

High Curator Malrec Voss is the supreme strategist of the [[Astral Conclave]]: part archmage, part technologist, part cult administrator. He does not think of himself as a tyrant. He believes reality is unstable, mortal choice is inefficient, and the only moral future is one where dangerous power is gathered, measured, and controlled by minds strong enough to use it.

- **Armor Class:** `20` astral ward mantle, reactive force geometry
- **Hit Points:** `210` (`20d8 + 120`)
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`, fly `30 ft.` hover while his Astral Mantle is active
- **STR** `10` **DEX** `16` **CON** `22` **INT** `22` **WIS** `18` **CHA** `18`
- **Saving Throws:** Con `+12`, Int `+12`, Wis `+10`, Cha `+10`
- **Skills:** Arcana `+18`, Deception `+10`, History `+12`, Insight `+10`, Investigation `+12`, Persuasion `+10`, Religion `+12`
- **Damage Resistances:** force, lightning, necrotic, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, frightened
- **Senses:** truesight `60 ft.`, passive Perception `14`
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic, Primordial, Deep Speech, Celestial, telepathy `120 ft.`
- **Proficiency Bonus:** `+6`
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `20`

## Traits
- **Level 20 Conclave Savant.** Malrec is a 20th-level spellcaster. Intelligence is his spellcasting ability. Spell save DC `20`, `+12` to hit with spell attacks.
- **Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Malrec fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
- **Astral Mantle.** Malrec's armor projects old Conclave force geometry. He can hover, cannot be knocked prone while conscious, and ignores difficult terrain created by magical or technological effects.
- **Calculated Sacrifice.** When Malrec takes damage, he can transfer up to `20` of that damage to one allied construct, summoned creature, controlled creature, or active Conclave device within `60` feet that he can see.
- **Master of Extraction.** Whenever a creature within `60` feet fails a saving throw against one of Malrec's spells or abilities, Malrec can gain `10` temporary hit points. He can gain temporary hit points this way once per turn.
- **Control Is Mercy.** Malrec has advantage on saving throws against spells and effects that would charm, frighten, possess, dominate, or magically compel him.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** Malrec makes two Astral Scepter attacks, or he makes one Astral Scepter attack and uses Command Extraction.
- **Astral Scepter.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+12` to hit, range `120 ft.`, one target. Hit: `27` (`5d8 + 5`) force damage, and the target cannot take reactions until the start of Malrec's next turn.
- **Command Extraction.** One creature Malrec can see within `60` feet must make a DC `20` Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the target takes `31` (`7d8`) psychic damage and must use its reaction, if available, to move up to half its speed in a direction Malrec chooses. A construct, warforged, or creature carrying Conclave control hardware has disadvantage on the save.
- **Void-Comet Lance (Recharge 5-6).** Malrec fires a line of star-black force `90` feet long and `10` feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a DC `20` Dexterity saving throw, taking `45` (`10d8`) force damage plus `22` (`4d10`) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. A creature reduced to `0` hit points by this damage has its essence partially crystallized.
- **Seal The Field (Recharge 6).** Malrec chooses a point he can see within `120` feet. A `30-foot-radius` field appears there until the end of his next turn. Creatures of Malrec's choice in the area cannot teleport, cannot benefit from planar travel, and have disadvantage on concentration checks. The area is difficult terrain for enemies.

## Bonus Actions
- **Directive Step.** Malrec teleports up to `30` feet to an unoccupied space he can see. One allied construct or controlled creature within `30` feet of his destination can move up to `15` feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Harvest Calculation.** Malrec marks one creature he can see within `90` feet until the end of his next turn. The first time Malrec or an ally hits the marked target, the attack deals an extra `13` (`3d8`) force damage.
- **Activate Device.** Malrec activates one Conclave device, ward, control node, portal anchor, or extraction apparatus within `60` feet.

## Reactions
- **Counterspell.** Malrec casts `counterspell`.
- **Reactive Ward.** When Malrec is hit by an attack, he gains a `+5` bonus to AC against that attack, potentially causing it to miss.
- **Punish Defiance.** When a creature Malrec can see within `60` feet succeeds on a saving throw against one of his spells or abilities, that creature takes `10` (`3d6`) psychic damage.

## Legendary Actions
Malrec can take `3` legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.

- **Scepter Pulse.** Malrec makes one Astral Scepter attack.
- **Reposition Asset.** One allied construct, controlled creature, or Conclave agent within `60` feet moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Disrupt Pattern.** One creature Malrec can see within `60` feet must succeed on a DC `20` Intelligence saving throw or have disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes before the end of its next turn.
- **Cast A Spell (Costs 2 Actions).** Malrec casts a spell of 3rd level or lower.
- **Extraction Surge (Costs 3 Actions).** Each enemy within `20` feet of Malrec must make a DC `20` Constitution saving throw, taking `27` (`6d8`) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. Malrec gains temporary hit points equal to half the highest damage dealt to one creature.

## Mythic Trait
- **The Conclave Endures.** When Malrec is reduced to `0` hit points, he does not die if at least one active Conclave engine, portal anchor, extraction core, or prepared phylactery-like research vessel is within `300` feet. Instead, he regains `105` hit points, all conditions on him end, and the device overloads or is destroyed. For the rest of the fight, Malrec's Void-Comet Lance recharges on `4-6`, and his Astral Scepter deals an extra `9` (`2d8`) necrotic damage.

## Mythic Actions
If Malrec's mythic trait has activated, he can use the options below as legendary actions.

- **Break The Boundary.** Malrec tears open a small planar fracture at a point he can see within `90` feet. Each creature within `10` feet of that point must make a DC `20` Dexterity saving throw, taking `18` (`4d8`) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
- **Rewrite Obedience (Costs 2 Actions).** One construct, warforged, or controlled creature Malrec can see within `60` feet must make a DC `20` Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it is stunned until the end of its next turn. If the target is already under Conclave control, it instead immediately makes one attack or moves up to its speed, Malrec's choice.
- **Meteoric Recall (Costs 3 Actions).** Malrec recharges Void-Comet Lance and immediately uses it.

## Ancient Magic Items
Malrec's equipment is cleaner and better maintained than Kira's, but much of it is still old age-of-technology hardware kept alive through replacement parts, stolen relics, and harvested cores.

### Astral Scepter Of Total Custody
- **Rarity:** Legendary
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Effects:**
  - Functions as Malrec's spellcasting focus and primary weapon.
  - Grants `+2` to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs, included above.
  - Once per day, can cast `dominate monster` without spending a spell slot.
- **Tell:** A target struck by the scepter briefly sees a perfect geometric cage around their own thoughts.

### Mantle Of Controlled Horizons
- **Rarity:** Legendary
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Effects:**
  - Grants hovering flight `30 ft.`
  - Grants resistance to force and psychic damage.
  - Prevents Malrec from being knocked prone while conscious.
- **Tell:** The air around him folds into faint star-map lines whenever he moves.

### Null-Comet Heart Shard
- **Rarity:** Artifact fragment
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Effects:**
  - Powers Void-Comet Lance.
  - Lets Malrec survive once through The Conclave Endures if a suitable device is nearby.
  - Makes him detectable to void-sensitive instruments, Kira's tuning rod, and planar breach scanners.
- **Drawback:** The shard is slowly making him less mortal and less capable of empathy.

### Curator's Sixfold Rings
- **Rarity:** Very rare
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Effects:**
  - Stores six prepared countermeasures.
  - Explains his Reactive Ward and several defensive redundancies.
  - If all six rings are destroyed or removed, Malrec loses Reactive Ward and his AC drops by `2`.

## Running Malrec
- Malrec should not fight like a lone wizard waiting to be surrounded.
- He uses terrain, devices, constructs, hostages, portals, and extraction engines as part of his body.
- His first goal is always control:
  - separate the party,
  - move enemies into bad positions,
  - punish casters,
  - activate machinery,
  - and make the battlefield itself feel owned.
- Against the warforged PC, he should be fascinated and hostile.
- He sees true autonomy as a dangerous design flaw.
- If the party defeats him, it should feel like breaking the Conclave's central philosophy, not just reducing a hit point total.
