# Session 10 High-Level Elementals

Use these as major elemental threats for five level `11` characters. Each one is intended to be a hard encounter by itself, especially with terrain, objectives, or minions. Running two at once is likely deadly unless the party has strong preparation or NPC support.

## Cinderstorm Primordial
*Huge elemental, chaotic neutral*

An ancient fire elemental swollen with furnace-pressure and old breach energy. Its body is a cyclone of flame, slag, and burning crystal dust.

- **Armor Class:** `18`
- **Hit Points:** `210` (`20d12 + 80`)
- **Speed:** `50 ft.`
- **STR** `22` **DEX** `20` **CON** `18` **INT** `8` **WIS** `14` **CHA** `12`
- **Saving Throws:** Dex `+10`, Con `+9`, Wis `+7`
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** cold
- **Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision `120 ft.`, passive Perception `12`
- **Languages:** Ignan, Primordial
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `13`

### Traits
- **Fire Form.** The primordial can move through a space as narrow as `1` inch wide without squeezing. A creature that touches it or hits it with a melee attack while within `5` feet takes `10` (`3d6`) fire damage.
- **Ignite.** A creature hit by the primordial's Flame Lash catches fire. Until a creature uses an action to douse the fire, the target takes `7` (`2d6`) fire damage at the start of each of its turns.
- **Smoke Crown.** Ranged attacks against the primordial from more than `30` feet away are made with disadvantage unless the attacker can see through smoke or magical obscurement.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The primordial makes three Flame Lash attacks.
- **Flame Lash.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+11` to hit, reach `15 ft.`, one target. Hit: `18` (`2d10 + 7`) fire damage plus `7` (`2d6`) force damage, and Ignite applies.
- **Cinderburst (Recharge 5-6).** Each creature of the primordial's choice within `20` feet must make a DC `18` Dexterity saving throw, taking `45` (`10d8`) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. On a failed save, the creature is also blinded until the end of its next turn.
- **Molten Trail.** The primordial moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Spaces it moves through become burning ground until the end of its next turn. A creature that enters burning ground for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes `10` (`3d6`) fire damage.

### Bonus Actions
- **Flare Step.** The primordial teleports up to `30` feet to an unoccupied space it can see that is within `5` feet of fire, lava, a furnace, or a burning creature.

### Reactions
- **Heat Backlash.** When a creature within `15` feet deals cold damage to the primordial, the primordial makes one Flame Lash attack against that creature.

### Terrain Ideas
- Furnace vents, burning intake lanes, glass floors cracking from heat, smoke that limits sight, citizens or captives trapped behind flame curtains.

## Abyssal Tide Sovereign
*Huge elemental, chaotic neutral*

A water elemental with the mass of a moving sea wall. Its shape holds drowned faces, pressure eddies, and fragments of old containment glyphs.

- **Armor Class:** `17`
- **Hit Points:** `225` (`18d12 + 108`)
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`, swim `90 ft.`
- **STR** `24` **DEX** `16` **CON** `22` **INT** `10` **WIS** `16` **CHA** `12`
- **Saving Throws:** Str `+12`, Con `+11`, Wis `+8`
- **Damage Resistances:** acid, cold; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** blindsight `60 ft.`, passive Perception `13`
- **Languages:** Aquan, Primordial
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `13`

### Traits
- **Water Form.** The sovereign can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as `1` inch wide without squeezing.
- **Crushing Pressure.** A creature grappled by the sovereign cannot speak and has disadvantage on Strength checks made to escape the grapple.
- **Drown The Spark.** Creatures that start their turn grappled by the sovereign take `14` (`4d6`) bludgeoning damage plus `14` (`4d6`) cold damage.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The sovereign makes two Slam attacks and uses Grasping Current if available.
- **Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+12` to hit, reach `10 ft.`, one target. Hit: `20` (`3d8 + 7`) bludgeoning damage plus `7` (`2d6`) cold damage.
- **Grasping Current.** One Large or smaller creature within `10` feet must succeed on a DC `19` Strength saving throw or be grappled escape DC `19`. Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained. The sovereign can grapple up to three creatures this way.
- **Tidal Crush (Recharge 5-6).** The sovereign unleashes a wave in a `60-foot` cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC `19` Strength saving throw, taking `36` (`8d8`) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. On a failed save, a creature is pushed `30` feet and knocked prone.
- **Undertow Relocation (Recharge 6).** Each creature grappled by the sovereign is moved up to `40` feet to an unoccupied space in water or on wet ground that the sovereign can see.

### Bonus Actions
- **Flood The Floor.** The sovereign creates a `20-foot-radius` area of ankle-to-knee-deep water centered on a point it can see within `60` feet. The area is difficult terrain for enemies for `1 minute`.

### Reactions
- **Flow Around The Blow.** When hit by a melee attack, the sovereign halves the attack's damage and moves up to `10` feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Terrain Ideas
- Flooded portal chamber, broken coolant canals, pressure pipes, citizens trapped in intake sluices, platforms sinking or rotating in water.

## Sky-Razor Tempest
*Huge elemental, chaotic neutral*

An air elemental compressed into a living stormfront. Its body is nearly invisible except for razor arcs, pressure rings, and debris orbiting its core.

- **Armor Class:** `19`
- **Hit Points:** `195` (`23d12 + 46`)
- **Speed:** `0 ft.`, fly `100 ft.` hover
- **STR** `18` **DEX** `24` **CON** `15` **INT** `10` **WIS** `16` **CHA** `14`
- **Saving Throws:** Dex `+12`, Wis `+8`, Cha `+7`
- **Damage Resistances:** lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision `120 ft.`, passive Perception `13`
- **Languages:** Auran, Primordial
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `13`

### Traits
- **Air Form.** The tempest can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as `1` inch wide without squeezing.
- **Flyby.** The tempest does not provoke opportunity attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.
- **Storm Shear.** Ranged weapon attacks against the tempest have disadvantage.
- **Unsteady Air.** A creature that starts its turn flying within `30` feet of the tempest must succeed on a DC `18` Strength saving throw or descend `30` feet and fall prone if it lands.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The tempest makes three Razor Gust attacks.
- **Razor Gust.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* `+12` to hit, reach `10 ft.` or range `90 ft.`, one target. Hit: `17` (`3d6 + 7`) slashing damage plus `9` (`2d8`) lightning damage.
- **Cyclone Snatch (Recharge 5-6).** Each creature in a `20-foot-radius`, `60-foot-high` cylinder centered on a point the tempest can see within `90` feet must make a DC `18` Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes `31` (`7d8`) bludgeoning damage, is lifted `30` feet into the air, and is restrained until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and is not lifted.
- **Thunderline Dash.** The tempest flies up to `80` feet in a straight line. Each creature whose space it passes through must make a DC `18` Dexterity saving throw, taking `27` (`6d8`) thunder damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

### Bonus Actions
- **Pressure Slip.** The tempest becomes invisible until the start of its next turn or until it makes an attack.

### Reactions
- **Deflect Projectile.** When hit by a ranged weapon attack, the tempest reduces the damage by `18` (`4d8`). If this reduces the damage to `0`, the projectile is redirected at a creature within `30` feet of the tempest; use the original attack roll against the new target.

### Terrain Ideas
- Catwalks, open spire shafts, floating debris, vertical lift wells, lightning rods, exposed portal rings, civilians hanging from wind-torn platforms.

## Worldroot Cataclysm
*Huge elemental, chaotic neutral*

An earth elemental whose body is layered stone, crystal, old root-like mineral veins, and buried war-era metal. It is slow until the ground itself decides to move with it.

- **Armor Class:** `20`
- **Hit Points:** `245` (`19d12 + 114`)
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`, burrow `40 ft.`
- **STR** `26` **DEX** `8` **CON** `22` **INT** `8` **WIS** `16` **CHA** `10`
- **Saving Throws:** Str `+13`, Con `+11`, Wis `+8`
- **Damage Resistances:** force; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, unconscious
- **Senses:** tremorsense `120 ft.`, passive Perception `13`
- **Languages:** Terran, Primordial
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `14`

### Traits
- **Earth Glide.** The cataclysm can burrow through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone without disturbing the material it moves through.
- **Immutable Mass.** The cataclysm has advantage on saving throws against being moved, knocked prone, stunned, or restrained.
- **Crystal Spine.** When a creature hits the cataclysm with a melee attack while within `10` feet, the attacker takes `7` (`2d6`) piercing damage.
- **Siege Monster.** The cataclysm deals double damage to objects and structures.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The cataclysm makes two Stone Fist attacks.
- **Stone Fist.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+13` to hit, reach `15 ft.`, one target. Hit: `25` (`3d10 + 9`) bludgeoning damage plus `9` (`2d8`) force damage.
- **Faultline Break (Recharge 5-6).** The cataclysm tears a line in the ground `80` feet long and `10` feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a DC `19` Dexterity saving throw, taking `44` (`8d10`) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. On a failed save, a creature also falls prone and is restrained by stone until the end of its next turn.
- **Crystal Prison (Recharge 6).** One creature the cataclysm can sense within `60` feet must make a DC `19` Strength saving throw. On a failure, the creature is encased in jagged crystal and restrained for `1 minute`. While restrained this way, it has total cover against effects outside the crystal but takes `14` (`4d6`) force damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or another creature can use an action to make a DC `19` Strength check, breaking the crystal on a success.

### Bonus Actions
- **Ground Claim.** The cataclysm chooses a `20-foot-radius` area of ground it can sense within `60` feet. Until the end of its next turn, that area is difficult terrain, and enemies in it cannot take reactions.

### Reactions
- **Stone Intercept.** When a creature the cataclysm can sense within `60` feet is hit by an attack, the cataclysm raises a slab of stone. The target gains `+5` AC against the triggering attack, potentially causing it to miss. The slab then crumbles.

### Terrain Ideas
- Collapsing halls, crystal mines, broken spire foundations, portal anchors sunk into bedrock, unstable columns, civilians trapped behind stonefall.

## Encounter Tuning
- **Hard solo:** one elemental plus dangerous terrain or an active objective.
- **Very hard:** one elemental plus `2` to `4` CR `2-4` minions, hazards, or lair pulses.
- **Deadly:** two elementals at once.
- **Arc finale:** all four should not fight simultaneously unless the goal is survival, ritual disruption, or staged waves rather than a normal combat.

## Shared Lair Pulse Option
If these elementals are tied to the same planar breach, use one pulse at initiative count `20`.

- **Fire Pulse:** one `15-foot` radius area ignites; creatures there make DC `16` Dex saves or take `14` (`4d6`) fire.
- **Water Pulse:** a `30-foot` line becomes rushing water; creatures make DC `16` Str saves or are pushed `20` feet.
- **Air Pulse:** one creature must make a DC `16` Str save or be lifted `20` feet.
- **Earth Pulse:** jagged stone erupts in a `10-foot` radius; creatures make DC `16` Dex saves or take `14` (`4d6`) piercing and fall prone.
