# Level 12 Enemy Stat Blocks

## Shadow Operative
*Medium humanoid, any alignment*

Rogue-style assassin, scout, or spire infiltrator. Use when you want a mobile enemy who hits hard, vanishes, and punishes isolated targets.

- **Armor Class:** `17` studded leather
- **Hit Points:** `110`
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`
- **STR** `10` **DEX** `20` **CON** `16` **INT** `14` **WIS** `14` **CHA** `12`
- **Saving Throws:** Dex `+9`, Int `+6`
- **Skills:** Acrobatics `+9`, Athletics `+4`, Deception `+5`, Insight `+6`, Perception `+6`, Sleight of Hand `+9`, Stealth `+13`
- **Senses:** passive Perception `16`
- **Languages:** Common plus two others
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `8`

### Traits
- **Sneak Attack 1/Turn.** The operative deals an extra `21` (`6d6`) damage when it hits with a weapon attack and has advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within `5` feet of an enemy of the target that is not incapacitated.
- **Cunning Action.** The operative can Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action.
- **Evasion.** If the operative is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, it instead takes no damage on a success and half damage on a failure.
- **Reliable Killer.** The operative has advantage on initiative rolls.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The operative makes two Shortsword attacks or two Hand Crossbow attacks.
- **Shortsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+9` to hit, reach `5 ft.`, one target. Hit: `8` (`1d6 + 5`) piercing damage. Once per turn, add Sneak Attack if applicable.
- **Hand Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* `+9` to hit, range `30/120 ft.`, one target. Hit: `8` (`1d6 + 5`) piercing damage. Once per turn, add Sneak Attack if applicable.
- **Poisoned Blade (3/Day).** The operative makes one Shortsword attack. On a hit, the target takes the attack's normal damage plus `14` (`4d6`) poison damage and must succeed on a DC `15` Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.
- **Smoke Bomb (1/Day).** The operative creates a `20-foot-radius` cloud of smoke centered on itself. The area is heavily obscured for `1 minute` or until dispersed by strong wind. The operative can immediately move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Bonus Actions
- **Dead Angle.** The operative moves up to `15` feet. If it ends this movement hidden or behind cover, it has advantage on its next attack before the end of the turn.

### Reactions
- **Uncanny Dodge.** When an attacker the operative can see hits it with an attack, the operative halves the attack's damage against it.

### Tactics
- Open from stealth and spend Sneak Attack early.
- Use Cunning Action to hide, break line of sight, or force the party to split attention.
- Smoke Bomb is best used to escape a bad position, protect an objective, or isolate a wounded target.

## Blinkknife Operative
*Medium humanoid, any alignment*

Swashbuckler-style assassin equipped with an old dagger-anchored teleportation device. Use when you want the Shadow Operative feel, but nastier: throw a blade, vanish to it, reappear behind the healer, and make the party start watching where every dagger lands.

- **Armor Class:** `17` studded leather
- **Hit Points:** `104`
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`
- **STR** `10` **DEX** `20` **CON** `16` **INT** `14` **WIS** `14` **CHA** `12`
- **Saving Throws:** Dex `+9`, Int `+6`
- **Skills:** Acrobatics `+9`, Deception `+9`, Insight `+6`, Perception `+6`, Persuasion `+5`, Sleight of Hand `+9`, Stealth `+13`
- **Senses:** passive Perception `16`
- **Languages:** Common plus two others
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `8`

### Traits
- **Sneak Attack 1/Turn.** The operative deals an extra `21` (`6d6`) damage when it hits with a weapon attack and has advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within `5` feet of an enemy of the target that is not incapacitated.
- **Cunning Action.** The operative can Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action.
- **Evasion.** If the operative is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, it instead takes no damage on a success and half damage on a failure.
- **Uncanny Dodge.** When an attacker the operative can see hits it with an attack, the operative can use its reaction to halve the damage.
- **Fancy Footwork.** During the operative's turn, if she makes a melee attack against a creature, that creature cannot make opportunity attacks against her for the rest of the turn.
- **Rakish Audacity.** The operative adds `+1` to initiative rolls. She can use Sneak Attack against a creature if she is within `5` feet of it, no other creatures are within `5` feet of her, and she does not have disadvantage on the attack roll.
- **Unstable Blink Harness.** The operative wears a 200-year-old teleport harness keyed to her phase daggers. Magical effects that prevent teleportation also prevent her Dagger Blink.
- **Recall Blades.** At the end of each of the operative's turns, one phase dagger she has thrown teleports back to an empty sheath on her harness unless a creature is holding it or it is in an antimagic field.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The operative makes two Phase Dagger attacks or two Hand Crossbow attacks.
- **Phase Dagger.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* `+9` to hit, reach `5 ft.` or range `20/60 ft.`, one target. Hit: `8` (`1d6 + 5`) piercing damage plus `7` (`2d6`) force damage. Once per turn, add Sneak Attack if applicable. If thrown, the dagger remains in the target's space or lodged in the target until the end of the operative's turn.
- **Hand Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* `+9` to hit, range `30/120 ft.`, one target. Hit: `8` (`1d6 + 5`) piercing damage. Once per turn, add Sneak Attack if applicable.
- **Dislocator Stab (Recharge 5-6).** The operative makes one Phase Dagger attack. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC `16` Charisma saving throw or be teleported up to `20` feet to an unoccupied space the operative can see. The destination must be on solid ground or a stable surface.
- **Flash-Slit Grenade (1/Day).** Each creature of the operative's choice within `10` feet must make a DC `16` Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature is blinded until the end of its next turn. The operative can then immediately use Dagger Blink without expending a use if one of her thrown phase daggers is within range.

### Bonus Actions
- **Dagger Blink (4/Day).** The operative teleports to an unoccupied space within `5` feet of one of her thrown phase daggers within `60` feet. The operative must be able to see the dagger, or the dagger must be lodged in a creature she can see. If she appears within `5` feet of a creature that cannot see her, she has advantage on the next attack roll she makes against that creature before the end of the turn.
- **Dead Angle.** The operative moves up to `15` feet. If it ends this movement hidden or behind cover, it has advantage on its next attack before the end of the turn.
- **Feinting Step.** The operative chooses one creature within `5` feet of her. The next melee attack roll she makes against that creature before the end of the turn has advantage. She cannot use this bonus action on the same turn she uses Dagger Blink.

### Reactions
- **Uncanny Dodge.** When an attacker the operative can see hits it with an attack, the operative halves the attack's damage against it.
- **Emergency Dagger Blink (1/Day).** When the operative takes damage and one of her thrown phase daggers is within `60` feet, she teleports to an unoccupied space within `5` feet of that dagger and becomes invisible until the start of her next turn or until she makes an attack.

### Magic Item
#### Rift-Spool Harness
- **Rarity:** Very rare
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Age:** About `200` years old
- **Effect:** Grants Dagger Blink and Emergency Dagger Blink.
- **Limit:** Dagger Blink can be used `4` times per day. Emergency Dagger Blink can be used `1` time per day.
- **Fault:** Whenever the operative uses Dagger Blink, roll a `d20`. On a `1`, the teleport works, but the harness sparks violently and the operative takes `7` (`2d6`) force damage.

### Tactics
- Throw a Phase Dagger past or into a vulnerable target, then use Dagger Blink to appear beside it and apply Sneak Attack.
- In one-on-one fights, Rakish Audacity lets her apply Sneak Attack without needing advantage or allies.
- Use Feinting Step when she specifically needs advantage, such as against a high-AC target or when trying to offset disadvantage.
- Use Dislocator Stab to move a target out of protective auras, into a hazard, or away from allies.
- Emergency Dagger Blink should trigger the first time the operative takes a truly dangerous hit, but only if she has already thrown a dagger into a useful escape position.
- Clever players can counter her by grabbing a thrown dagger, moving away from it, blocking line of sight, or forcing her to spend all `4` daily blinks.
- This enemy is best in rooms with pillars, catwalks, multiple doors, or machinery that breaks line of sight.

## Ironline Veteran
*Medium humanoid, any alignment*

Fighter-style elite guard, duelist, or commander. Use when you want a direct martial threat who can stand in the open and trade blows with high-level characters.

- **Armor Class:** `19` plate armor
- **Hit Points:** `142`
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`
- **STR** `20` **DEX** `14` **CON** `18` **INT** `11` **WIS** `14` **CHA** `12`
- **Saving Throws:** Str `+9`, Con `+8`, Wis `+6`
- **Skills:** Athletics `+9`, Intimidation `+5`, Perception `+6`
- **Senses:** passive Perception `16`
- **Languages:** Common plus one other
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `9`

### Traits
- **Indomitable (2/Day).** If the veteran fails a saving throw, it can reroll the save and must use the new roll.
- **Second Wind (1/Day).** As a bonus action, the veteran regains `20` hit points.
- **Action Surge (1/Day).** On its turn, the veteran can take one additional action.
- **Sentinel Training.** When the veteran hits a creature with an opportunity attack, the creature's speed becomes `0` for the rest of the turn.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The veteran makes three weapon attacks.
- **Greatsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+9` to hit, reach `5 ft.`, one target. Hit: `12` (`2d6 + 5`) slashing damage plus `4` (`1d8`) force damage.
- **Heavy Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* `+6` to hit, range `100/400 ft.`, one target. Hit: `7` (`1d10 + 2`) piercing damage.
- **Driving Assault (Recharge 5-6).** The veteran makes two Greatsword attacks against one creature. If both attacks hit, the target must succeed on a DC `17` Strength saving throw or be pushed `15` feet and knocked prone.
- **Commanding Strike (Recharge 6).** The veteran makes one Greatsword attack. On a hit, one allied creature that can hear or see the veteran can immediately use its reaction to move up to half its speed and make one weapon attack.

### Bonus Actions
- **Second Wind (1/Day).** The veteran regains `20` hit points.
- **Brace.** Until the start of its next turn, the veteran gains a `+2` bonus to AC against melee attacks, but its speed is reduced to `0`.

### Reactions
- **Parry.** The veteran adds `3` to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the veteran must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
- **Punish Retreat.** When a creature within `5` feet of the veteran moves away without Disengaging, the veteran makes one Greatsword attack against it.

### Tactics
- Use the veteran to hold a doorway, protect a boss, or keep the party away from a control device.
- Action Surge should happen when a player is prone, restrained, or separated.
- Commanding Strike makes this enemy stronger with even one lesser ally nearby.

## Twin-Hammer Colossus
*Large construct, lawful evil*

Heavy brute or elite construct bodyguard. Use when you want a big enemy that walks into the middle of the party and makes everyone respect the reach of two giant hammers.

- **Armor Class:** `18` reinforced plating
- **Hit Points:** `168`
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`
- **STR** `22` **DEX** `10` **CON** `20` **INT** `8` **WIS** `12` **CHA** `8`
- **Saving Throws:** Str `+10`, Con `+9`, Wis `+5`
- **Skills:** Athletics `+10`, Perception `+5`
- **Damage Resistances:** force; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision `60 ft.`, passive Perception `15`
- **Languages:** understands Common and Construct Cant but rarely speaks
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `9`

### Traits
- **Twin Hammer Frame.** The colossus wields two massive force-charged hammers. Its hammer attacks count as magical.
- **Siege Body.** The colossus deals double damage to objects and structures.
- **Unstoppable Gait.** The colossus has advantage on saving throws against being pushed, knocked prone, or restrained. It can move through the space of Medium or smaller creatures, but cannot end its movement there.
- **Crushing Passage.** The first time on a turn the colossus moves through a creature's space, that creature must succeed on a DC `18` Strength saving throw or take `10` (`3d6`) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The colossus makes two Giant Hammer attacks.
- **Giant Hammer.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+9` to hit, reach `10 ft.`, one target. Hit: `11` (`2d10`) bludgeoning damage plus `3` (`1d6`) force damage.
- **Double Impact.** If the colossus hits the same creature with both Giant Hammer attacks on the same turn, the target must succeed on a DC `17` Strength saving throw or be pushed `15` feet and knocked prone.
- **Hammerquake (Recharge 5-6).** The colossus slams both hammers into the ground. Each creature of its choice within `15` feet must make a DC `17` Dexterity saving throw, taking `27` (`6d8`) thunder damage plus `10` (`3d6`) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. On a failed save, a creature is also knocked prone.
- **Force Anvil (Recharge 6).** The colossus targets one creature it can see within `30` feet. The target must make a DC `17` Strength saving throw. On a failure, the target takes `22` (`4d10`) force damage and is pulled up to `20` feet straight toward the colossus. If this pull brings the target within `10` feet of the colossus, the colossus can make one Giant Hammer attack against it.

### Bonus Actions
- **Hammer Lock.** After the colossus hits a creature with a Giant Hammer attack, it can lock that creature in place with crushing force. The target must succeed on a DC `17` Strength saving throw or its speed is reduced to `0` until the end of its next turn.

### Reactions
- **Backswing.** When a creature within `10` feet of the colossus misses it with a melee attack, the colossus makes one Giant Hammer attack against that creature.

### Tactics
- Put the colossus in wide rooms, throne approaches, hangar floors, or portal chambers where its reach and forced movement matter.
- It should use Force Anvil to pull casters or ranged attackers into danger.
- Hammerquake is best when two or more characters are close or when the party is trying to protect an objective.
- If you want it nastier, let it use Hammer Lock after opportunity attacks too.

## Orb-Crowned Colossus
*Large construct, lawful evil*

Artillery guardian with a spinning crystal orb for a head. Use when you want a construct that feels alien, precise, and dangerous at range while still being hard to ignore in melee.

- **Armor Class:** `17` reinforced plating and rotating ward plates
- **Hit Points:** `156`
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`
- **STR** `20` **DEX** `12` **CON** `19` **INT** `12` **WIS** `14` **CHA** `8`
- **Saving Throws:** Con `+8`, Int `+5`, Wis `+6`
- **Skills:** Arcana `+5`, Perception `+6`
- **Damage Resistances:** fire, force, lightning; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** blinded, charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned
- **Senses:** blindsight `30 ft.`, darkvision `120 ft.`, passive Perception `16`
- **Languages:** understands Common and Construct Cant; communicates in projected glyphs and harmonic tones
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `10`

### Traits
- **Spinning Orb Head.** The colossus cannot be blinded while its orb head is intact. A creature that starts its turn within `10` feet of the colossus and can see the orb must succeed on a DC `16` Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the start of its next turn.
- **Arcane Targeting Lattice.** The colossus ignores half cover and three-quarters cover with spell-like attacks.
- **Volatile Core.** When the colossus is reduced to `0` hit points, its orb head cracks and releases stored energy. Each creature within `10` feet must make a DC `16` Dexterity saving throw, taking `14` (`4d6`) fire damage plus `14` (`4d6`) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The colossus makes two Force Ray attacks or one Force Ray attack and one Gravity Palm attack.
- **Force Ray.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+8` to hit, range `120 ft.`, one target. Hit: `18` (`4d8`) force damage.
- **Gravity Palm.** *Melee Spell Attack:* `+8` to hit, reach `10 ft.`, one target. Hit: `15` (`3d6 + 5`) force damage, and the target must succeed on a DC `16` Strength saving throw or be pushed `10` feet away or pulled `10` feet closer, colossus's choice.
- **Fireball Eruption (Recharge 5-6).** The orb releases a compressed starburst at a point the colossus can see within `150` feet. Each creature in a `20-foot-radius` sphere centered on that point must make a DC `16` Dexterity saving throw, taking `28` (`8d6`) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. Objects in the area ignite if they are not being worn or carried.
- **Prismatic Sweep (Recharge 6).** The orb fires a sweeping beam in a `60-foot` line that is `5` feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a DC `16` Dexterity saving throw, taking `22` (`4d10`) force damage plus `11` (`2d10`) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. A creature that fails the save cannot benefit from invisibility until the end of its next turn.

### Bonus Actions
- **Target Lock.** The colossus marks one creature it can see within `120` feet until the end of its next turn. The next Force Ray attack the colossus makes against the marked target has advantage. If it hits, the target's speed is reduced by `10` feet until the end of its next turn.

### Reactions
- **Rotating Ward.** When the colossus is hit by a ranged attack or spell attack, it adds `3` to its AC against that attack, potentially causing it to miss.

### Tactics
- Start with Fireball Eruption if the party clusters.
- Use Target Lock on casters, healers, or flying characters.
- Keep it near guards or terrain that discourages melee attackers from simply surrounding it.
- Its orb head makes it feel more like a magical surveillance weapon than a normal soldier.

## Empty-Hand Colossus
*Large construct, lawful neutral or lawful evil*

Weaponless control brute. It does not carry blades or hammers because its body is the weapon: gravity plates, locking joints, sonic pressure, and command-field emitters built into its palms and chest.

- **Armor Class:** `18` smooth impact plating
- **Hit Points:** `176`
- **Speed:** `35 ft.`
- **STR** `23` **DEX** `12` **CON** `20` **INT** `10` **WIS** `14` **CHA** `10`
- **Saving Throws:** Str `+10`, Con `+9`, Wis `+6`
- **Skills:** Athletics `+10`, Insight `+6`, Perception `+6`
- **Damage Resistances:** force, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, poisoned, restrained
- **Senses:** darkvision `60 ft.`, tremorsense `30 ft.`, passive Perception `16`
- **Languages:** Common, Construct Cant
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `10`

### Traits
- **Empty-Hand Doctrine.** The colossus's unarmed strikes count as magical. It does not need free hands to grapple.
- **Anchor Field.** The ground within `10` feet of the colossus is difficult terrain for enemies. A creature that starts its turn in this area must succeed on a DC `17` Strength saving throw or its speed is reduced by `10` feet until the start of its next turn.
- **Pressure Shell.** The colossus has advantage on saving throws against spells and effects that would move it, knock it prone, or teleport it against its will.

### Actions
- **Multiattack.** The colossus makes two Kinetic Slam attacks. It can replace one attack with Null Grip.
- **Kinetic Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+10` to hit, reach `10 ft.`, one target. Hit: `16` (`2d8 + 7`) bludgeoning damage plus `9` (`2d8`) force damage.
- **Null Grip.** One creature within `10` feet must succeed on a DC `18` Strength saving throw or be grappled escape DC `18`. Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained and takes `9` (`2d8`) force damage at the start of each of its turns.
- **Repulsion Wave (Recharge 5-6).** The colossus emits a circular blast of force. Each enemy within `20` feet must make a DC `17` Strength saving throw, taking `27` (`6d8`) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. On a failed save, a creature is also pushed `20` feet away and knocked prone.
- **Gravity Pin (Recharge 6).** The colossus targets a point on the ground it can see within `60` feet. Each creature in a `15-foot-radius` area centered on that point must make a DC `17` Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes `22` (`4d10`) force damage and is restrained by crushing gravity until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and is not restrained.

### Bonus Actions
- **Reposition Target.** The colossus moves one creature it has grappled up to `15` feet to an unoccupied space it can see within reach. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.
- **Body Lock.** The colossus gains `15` temporary hit points and cannot be moved against its will until the start of its next turn.

### Reactions
- **Catch And Break.** When a creature misses the colossus with a melee attack from within `10` feet, the attacker must succeed on a DC `17` Dexterity saving throw or be grappled by the colossus.
- **Command Pulse.** When a creature the colossus can see within `30` feet starts casting a spell, the colossus forces that creature to make a DC `16` Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes `10` (`3d6`) thunder damage. This does not interrupt the spell unless the damage causes a failed concentration check or the spell otherwise requires concentration.

### Tactics
- This colossus is best as an objective defender.
- It should pin one character, shove others off the objective, and force the party to spend actions freeing allies.
- Use Gravity Pin to stop the party from rushing a console, portal, or prisoner line.
- It is scary without weapons because it feels like the room itself has decided to hold the party down.
