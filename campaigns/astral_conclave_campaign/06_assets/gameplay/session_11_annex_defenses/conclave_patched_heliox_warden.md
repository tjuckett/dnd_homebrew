# Conclave-Patched Heliox Warden

*Medium construct, lawful evil · CR 5 (`1,800 XP`)*

| AC | HP | Speed |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `18` | `102` (`12d8 + 48`) | `30 ft.` |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `18` (`+4`) | `10` (`+0`) | `18` (`+4`) | `8` (`-1`) | `12` (`+1`) | `5` (`-3`) |

- **Saves** Str `+8`, Con `+8`, Wis `+5`; **Skills** Athletics `+8`, Perception `+5`
- **Resistances** nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, slashing; **Immunities** poison, psychic; **Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned
- **Senses** darkvision `60 ft.`, passive `15`; **Languages** understands Common and Primordial

## Traits

**Override Collar.** A creature adjacent to the warden can use an action to make a DC `16` Arcana, Tinker's Tools, or Thieves' Tools check. On a success, the warden is incapacitated with speed `0` until repaired or reactivated. On a failure by `5` or more, it makes one Shock Maul attack as a reaction.

**Corridor Anchor.** Within `5 feet` of a doorway, shutter, or passage no wider than `10 feet`, the warden has advantage on saves against being moved or knocked prone. Hostile creatures treat spaces within `5 feet` of it as difficult terrain.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Two Shock Maul attacks.

**Shock Maul.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+8` to hit, reach `5 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `13` (`2d8 + 4`) bludgeoning plus `4` (`1d8`) lightning.

**Repulsion Bash.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+8` to hit, reach `5 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `10` (`1d12 + 4`) force; DC `16` Strength save or pushed up to `15 feet`. A target pushed into a wall, shutter, or creature takes `5` (`1d10`) extra bludgeoning and falls prone.

**Lock the Passage (Recharge 5-6).** Choose a `10-foot` square within `10 feet`. Until the start of the warden's next turn, it is difficult terrain for hostile creatures; the first hostile creature entering it each turn must make a DC `16` Strength save or have speed `0` for that turn.

## Reaction

**Intercept.** When a creature moves from within the warden's reach to beyond its reach, the warden makes one Repulsion Bash attack.

# Marcus, 12th-Level Tech

*Medium humanoid (human), neutral good*

Marcus is a veteran field engineer who specializes in Heliox control lattices. He is designed as a capable allied NPC: useful in a fight, but strongest when repairing, disabling, or commandeering machinery.

| AC | HP | Speed |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `18` | `99` (`12d8 + 36`) | `30 ft.` |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `10` (`+0`) | `14` (`+2`) | `16` (`+3`) | `20` (`+5`) | `14` (`+2`) | `11` (`+0`) |

- **Proficiency Bonus** `+4`; **Saves** Con `+7`, Int `+9`
- **Skills** Arcana `+13`, Investigation `+13`, Perception `+6`, Sleight of Hand `+6`
- **Tool Proficiencies** Smith's tools `+13`, thieves' tools `+10`, tinker's tools `+13`
- **Resistances** lightning; **Senses** passive `16`; **Languages** Common, Primordial

## Traits

**Battlefield Engineer.** Marcus has advantage on checks made to identify, disable, repair, or operate constructs, traps, and technological devices. A construct does not gain advantage on saving throws against Marcus's effects from Magic Resistance.

**Flash of Genius (4/Day).** When Marcus or a creature he can see within `30 feet` makes an ability check or saving throw, Marcus can use his reaction to add `+5` to the roll.

**Heliox Override.** Marcus automatically recognizes the warden's Override Collar. When he succeeds on the check to disable it, he can choose to commandeer the warden instead of incapacitating it. A commandeered warden is friendly to Marcus and his allies and obeys Marcus's spoken commands for `10 minutes`. Marcus must concentrate on this control as if concentrating on a spell. If his concentration ends, the warden is incapacitated with speed `0` until repaired or reactivated.

**Grenade Satchel (4/Day).** Marcus carries four grenades. Each grenade action expends one use of this trait, and Marcus chooses which type to throw when he uses it.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Marcus makes two Arc-Pistol attacks, or he makes one Shock Baton attack and uses Disrupting Pulse.

**Arc-Pistol.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+9` to hit, range `90 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `16` (`2d10 + 5`) lightning damage.

**Shock Baton.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* `+6` to hit, reach `5 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `6` (`1d6 + 2`) bludgeoning plus `9` (`2d8`) lightning damage, and the target can't take reactions until the start of its next turn.

**Fragmentation Grenade.** Marcus throws a grenade at a point he can see within `60 feet`. Each creature in a `15-foot-radius` sphere centered on that point must make a DC `17` Dexterity saving throw, taking `24` (`7d6`) piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. Unsecured objects in the area also take this damage.

**Arc Grenade.** Marcus throws a grenade at a point he can see within `60 feet`. Each creature in a `10-foot-radius` sphere centered on that point must make a DC `17` Constitution saving throw, taking `22` (`5d8`) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. A construct that fails is also incapacitated and has speed `0` until the start of Marcus's next turn.

**Gravitic Snare Grenade.** Marcus throws a grenade at a point he can see within `60 feet`, creating a `15-foot-radius` distortion that lasts until the end of Marcus's next turn. The area is difficult terrain. Each hostile creature in the area when it appears must succeed on a DC `17` Strength saving throw or be pulled up to `10 feet` toward its center and restrained until the effect ends. A restrained creature can use its action to repeat the save, ending the condition on itself on a success.

**Smoke-Screen Grenade.** Marcus throws a grenade at a point he can see within `60 feet`. A `20-foot-radius` sphere of violet smoke spreads around corners and is heavily obscured for `1 minute`, until a strong wind disperses it, or until Marcus dismisses it as a bonus action. Marcus and creatures protected by his Deploy Barrier can see through this smoke.

**Disrupting Pulse.** One creature or device Marcus can see within `60 feet` must make a DC `17` Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it takes `18` (`4d8`) lightning damage and has disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn. A construct takes `27` (`6d8`) lightning damage instead and is also unable to take reactions until the end of its next turn. On a success, the target takes half damage and suffers no other effect.

**Master Override (Recharge 5-6).** One construct Marcus can see within `30 feet` must succeed on a DC `17` Intelligence saving throw or be incapacitated and have speed `0` until the end of Marcus's next turn. If it fails by `5` or more, Marcus can immediately move it up to half its speed and cause it to make one weapon attack against a target of his choice.

**Field Repair (3/Day).** Marcus touches a construct or object and restores `25` (`4d8 + 7`) hit points to it. Alternatively, he ends one of the following conditions affecting a construct: blinded, deafened, incapacitated, paralyzed, or stunned.

## Bonus Action

**Deploy Barrier (3/Day).** Marcus projects a barrier around himself or one creature he can see within `30 feet`. The target gains `15` temporary hit points and half cover until the start of Marcus's next turn.

## Reaction

**Emergency Deflection.** When Marcus is hit by an attack he can see, he adds `+4` to his AC against that attack, potentially causing it to miss.

## Tactics

- Marcus prioritizes the Heliox Override over damaging the warden.
- He uses Deploy Barrier on whoever is exposed while attempting an override.
- He uses an Arc Grenade against clustered constructs, a Gravitic Snare Grenade to hold a corridor, and a Smoke-Screen Grenade to cover an override or retreat.
- Against other constructs, he opens with Master Override and follows with Disrupting Pulse.
- He saves Field Repair for an allied construct or critical annex machinery unless an objective specifically requires the warden intact.
