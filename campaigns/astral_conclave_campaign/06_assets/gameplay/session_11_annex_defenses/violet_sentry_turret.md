# Violet Sentry Turret

*Medium construct, unaligned · CR 3 (`700 XP`)*

| AC | HP | Speed |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `17` | `60` (`8d8 + 24`) | `0 ft.` |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `14` (`+2`) | `14` (`+2`) | `16` (`+3`) | `3` (`-4`) | `14` (`+2`) | `3` (`-4`) |

- **Saves** Dex `+4`; **Resistances** nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, slashing; **Immunities** poison, psychic
- **Condition Immunities** blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone
- **Senses** darkvision `120 ft.`, passive `12`; **Languages** understands Conclave command code

## Traits

**Fixed Mount.** The turret occupies a ceiling alcove or wall recess and has three-quarters cover against attacks outside its firing corridor. A creature reaching the alcove attacks it normally.

**Targeting Lattice.** A creature hit by Targeting Beam glows violet until the start of the turret's next turn. The turret has advantage on Force Bolt attacks against that target.

**Manual Override.** A creature adjacent to the housing can use an action for a DC `15` Arcana, Tinker's Tools, or Thieves' Tools check. On success, the turret is disabled for `10 minutes`; on failure, the creature takes `7` (`2d6`) lightning damage.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Two attacks, only one of which can be Targeting Beam.

**Force Bolt.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+6` to hit, range `120 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `12` (`2d8 + 3`) force.

**Targeting Beam.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+6` to hit, range `120 ft.`, one target. *Hit:* `7` (`1d8 + 3`) radiant; Targeting Lattice applies.

**Suppressive Sweep (Recharge 5-6).** `30-foot` line, `5 feet` wide; DC `14` Dexterity save, `13` (`3d8`) force on a failure or half on a success. On a failure, the creature cannot take reactions until its next turn.
