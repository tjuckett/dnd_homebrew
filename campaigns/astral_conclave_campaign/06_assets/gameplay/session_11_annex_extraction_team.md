# Session 11 Seismic Annex Extraction Team

These enemies want to destroy records and transport the captive, not win a fight to the death.

## Quick Select

| Role | Quantity | Stat card |

| Purge records / move captive | `2` | [Annex Purge Technician](session_11_annex_extraction_team/annex_purge_technician.md) |

## Fast Tactics

- Handler operates the gate; one technician advances extraction; the other burns or packs evidence.
- Escort blocks the shortest route and hides the console or captive with static.
- Extraction advances at most once per round from any source.
- Once the captive is free, surviving agents flee with portable data.
