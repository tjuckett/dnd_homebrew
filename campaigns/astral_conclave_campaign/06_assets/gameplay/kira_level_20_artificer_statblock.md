# Kira, Age-of-Technology Artificer
*Medium humanoid, neutral good*

Former [[Astral Conclave]] artificer, breach engineer, and survivor of the age of technology. Kira is not just a spellcaster with tools; she is one of the last living people who understands the old machines as native technology rather than lost magic.

- **Armor Class:** `21` antique infused half plate, repulsion shield
- **Hit Points:** `188` (`20d8 + 100`)
- **Speed:** `30 ft.`, swim `30 ft.`
- **STR** `10` **DEX** `14` **CON** `20` **INT** `20` **WIS** `14` **CHA** `12`
- **Saving Throws:** Con `+17`, Int `+17`, Wis `+14`
- **Skills:** Arcana `+11`, History `+11`, Investigation `+11`, Perception `+8`, Sleight of Hand `+8`
- **Damage Resistances:** acid, cold, lightning
- **Senses:** passive Perception `18`
- **Languages:** Common, Primordial, Deep Speech, Aquan
- **Proficiency Bonus:** `+6`
- **Challenge:** roughly CR `17`

## Traits
- **Level 20 Artificer.** Kira uses Intelligence as her spellcasting ability. Spell save DC `19`, `+11` to hit with spell attacks.
- **Tool Expertise.** Kira doubles her proficiency bonus for ability checks using tools.
- **Flash of Genius (5/Day).** When Kira or a creature she can see within `30` feet makes an ability check or saving throw, Kira can add `+5` to the roll.
- **Soul of Artifice.** Kira is attuned to six magic items and gains `+6` to all saving throws, already included above. If she is reduced to `0` hit points but not killed outright, she can end one active infusion or overdraw one antique device to drop to `1` hit point instead.
- **Arcane Firearm.** When Kira casts an artificer spell through her tuning rod, one damage roll of that spell deals an extra `4` (`1d8`) force damage.
- **Ancient Technical Fluency.** Kira has advantage on checks to identify, repair, sabotage, or safely overload pre-war machines, Conclave devices, leyline equipment, planar engines, and construct-control systems.
- **Two-Century Gear Decay.** The first time each day Kira uses an antique magic item for a major effect, roll a `d20`. On a `1`, the item works but gains a visible fault: smoke, harmonic scream, cracked crystal, frozen relay, or a one-round shutdown. Kira can repair one fault during a short rest with tinker's tools.

## Spellcasting
Kira has the following artificer spells prepared.

- **Cantrips:** `fire bolt`, `guidance`, `mending`, `ray of frost`, `shocking grasp`
- **1st level, 4 slots:** `absorb elements`, `cure wounds`, `detect magic`, `faerie fire`, `shield`
- **2nd level, 3 slots:** `aid`, `lesser restoration`, `mirror image`, `see invisibility`, `web`
- **3rd level, 3 slots:** `counterspell`, `dispel magic`, `fly`, `revivify`, `water breathing`
- **4th level, 3 slots:** `fabricate`, `freedom of movement`, `Otiluke's resilient sphere`, `summon construct`
- **5th level, 2 slots:** `Bigby's hand`, `greater restoration`, `wall of force`

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** Kira makes two attacks with her Arcane Sidearm or Elemental Tuning Rod in any combination.
- **Arcane Sidearm.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* `+11` to hit, range `90/300 ft.`, one target. Hit: `16` (`2d10 + 5`) force damage.
- **Elemental Tuning Rod.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+11` to hit, range `120 ft.`, one target. Hit: `23` (`4d8 + 5`) force damage, and the target cannot take reactions until the start of Kira's next turn. Elementals and constructs take an extra `9` (`2d8`) lightning damage.
- **Recalibrate Breach Harmonics (Recharge 5-6).** Kira emits tuned anti-planar force in a `20-foot-radius` sphere centered on a point she can see within `120` feet. Each creature of her choice in the area must make a DC `19` Constitution saving throw, taking `36` (`8d8`) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. On a failed save, a creature also cannot teleport or benefit from extradimensional movement until the end of Kira's next turn. Elementals and constructs make this save with disadvantage.
- **Emergency Reconstruction (1/Day).** Kira targets a construct, object, machine, or vehicle section she can touch. The target regains `70` hit points, or one disabled nonmagical subsystem begins functioning again for `1 hour`.

## Bonus Actions
- **Eldritch Cannon.** Kira commands a Small eldritch cannon she can see within `60` feet. The cannon has AC `18`, `30` hit points, and immunity to poison and psychic damage. Choose one:
  - **Force Ballista.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* `+11` to hit, range `120 ft.`, one target. Hit: `18` (`3d8 + 5`) force damage, and the target is pushed `5` feet away from the cannon.
  - **Protector.** Kira and each creature of her choice within `10` feet of the cannon gains `12` (`2d8 + 3`) temporary hit points.
- **Overclocked Actuator (3/Day).** Kira takes the Dash, Disengage, or Use an Object action.

## Reactions
- **Repulsion Shield.** When a creature Kira can see hits her with a melee attack, she pushes that creature up to `15` feet away.
- **Countermeasure Pulse (3/Day).** When Kira sees a creature within `60` feet cast a spell, activate a device, or trigger a magical machine, she can force the creature to make a DC `19` Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the spell or device still resolves, but one target of Kira's choice has advantage on its saving throw against it or resistance to its damage.

## Antique Magic Items
These are not shiny modern treasures. They are 200-year-old age-of-technology devices: powerful, irreplaceable, and slightly tired.

### Kira's Elemental Tuning Rod
- **Rarity:** Legendary
- **Attunement:** Kira only, or a creature trained by Kira
- **Function:** Arcane firearm, spell focus, leyline tuner, and planar stabilizer.
- **Effects:**
  - Grants `+2` to spell attack rolls and artificer spell save DCs, already included in Kira's stats.
  - Can detect unstable planar resonance within `300` feet.
  - Lets Kira use Recalibrate Breach Harmonics.
- **Age Fault:** If overused, the rod begins harmonizing with nearby Conclave devices, making Kira easier to locate.

### Repulsion Shield, Mark III
- **Rarity:** Very rare
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Function:** Collapsible wrist shield with old force-projector ribs.
- **Effects:**
  - Grants `+2 AC`, included in Kira's AC.
  - Lets Kira use Repulsion Shield as a reaction.
  - Once per day, Kira can cast `Otiluke's resilient sphere` without spending a spell slot.
- **Age Fault:** The shield emits visible blue-white static whenever it blocks a heavy hit.

### Infused Half Plate Of The Drowned Lab
- **Rarity:** Very rare
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Function:** Pressure-sealed pre-war armor from Kira's underwater station.
- **Effects:**
  - Functions as `+2 half plate`.
  - Grants swim speed `30 ft.`
  - Grants resistance to acid and cold.
  - Kira can breathe underwater while wearing it.
- **Age Fault:** The seals hiss under sudden fire or thunder damage.

### Nullflare Sidearm
- **Rarity:** Very rare
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Function:** Compact pre-war arc pistol built to disrupt planar creatures and construct shielding.
- **Effects:**
  - Kira's Arcane Sidearm attack deals force damage.
  - Once per day on a hit, Kira can suppress the target's teleportation and planar travel for `1 minute`; DC `19` Charisma save negates.
- **Age Fault:** The sidearm has no replacement cells. On a natural `1`, it cannot fire again until Kira spends an action clearing the chamber.

### Ward Emitter Spool
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** Not required
- **Function:** Kira's spell-storing item.
- **Effects:**
  - Stores `web`.
  - Can cast `web` up to `10` times using Kira's spell save DC `19`.
- **Age Fault:** The projected webbing looks like luminous wireframe strands and briefly reveals Kira's old Conclave calibration signature.

### Breachwright's Tool Harness
- **Rarity:** Legendary
- **Attunement:** Required
- **Function:** A walking lab of folding tools, drone sockets, memory slates, and self-heating solder arrays.
- **Effects:**
  - Grants advantage on tinker's tools, smith's tools, thieves' tools, and arcana checks involving technology.
  - Once per day, Kira can cast `fabricate` without spending a spell slot.
  - Once per day, Kira can stabilize a failing machine, portal, vehicle, or construct-control device for `10 minutes`.
- **Age Fault:** Every repair consumes rare components Kira cannot easily replace.

## Running Kira
- Kira should feel like a walking emergency solution, but not an infinite one.
- She is strongest when solving machines, shielding allies, and preventing planar or control-tech disasters.
- In combat, she opens with control or defense, then uses her sidearm and tuning rod while the cannon protects allies.
- She should avoid wasteful fights. Every shot sounds expensive because it is.
- If the party asks why her devices look strange, the answer is simple: they are not prototypes anymore. They are antiques from a world that knew how to build miracles and then died badly.
