# Wintervale Assault

## Objective

Take [[Wintervale]] from [[Astral Conclave]] control before the Convergence, which is three weeks away as of Session 13.

## Confirmed Allies

- Approximately ten earth elementals from [[Stonehearth]] joined the initial assault and continued clearing the Conclave base after the party moved to the airship.
- Warforged support is possible but has not been confirmed.

## Unknowns

- Conclave troop strength, leadership, and defensive preparations
- The condition and allegiance of Wintervale's civilians
- The function, connections, and defenses of the town's portal circle

## Current Status

During Session 14, the party attacked and cleared the initial area of the Conclave's main Wintervale base. The earth elementals remained to clear the rest while the party moved to seize a Conclave airship as it docked. The state of the base and the outcome of the airship takeover are unresolved.
