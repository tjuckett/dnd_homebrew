# Echoes of Dapplefern: The First Race

Detailed quest: [[Echoes of Dapplefern]]

## Purpose

The First Race is a fey pursuit through the [[Gloamfold Chase]]. It is not a boss fight. The party must carry the **Hearth-Ember** from the ruined echo of Moonpaw's village to the Dawnroot Bell before dawn while [[The Thorn-Crowned Stag]] hunts them at mortal scale.

Winning releases [[Dapplefern Refuge]] from the Stag's forest-realm and restores every true path out. It does not free Moonpaw's wife and child; it proves they are alive and establishes the later Final Hunt.

The Stag runs the First Race only after the shorter Witness Run in [[Echoes of Dapplefern]] convinces it that Moonpaw is the runner described by the Tabaxi survivors.

## Core Rules to State Aloud

The Stag must declare these terms before neutral witnesses. Let the fey player challenge vague wording.

- The race begins at the third bell after moonrise and ends when the first direct dawn-light touches the Dawnroot Bell.
- The party wins if the Hearth-Ember is placed in the Dawnroot Bell's cradle and every willing racer reaches the bell.
- The Stag wins if the ember goes out, is willingly given away, or every racer is captured.
- The Stag hunts in its **mortal chase form**, one of many shapes it can wear. It cannot use legendary power, teleport, charm, or a killing blow.
- It may use terrain, lesser fey, false trails, and direct capture. It must give every obstacle a real counterplay option.
- A captured racer becomes a temporary pendant on the Stag's thorn crown. They remain conscious and can speak or act in limited ways from within the pendant.
- The Stag may not harm Dapplefern, pursue a non-racer into the basin, or alter the finish after the first bell.

If the Stag breaks a declared term, the race ends in the party's favor. It will not do this deliberately; the whole point is a story worth honoring.

## At-the-Table Engine

Use three visible tracks. Announce changes so the race feels fair.

| Track | Starts at | What it means |
|---|---:|---|
| **Dawn Clock** | `0/8` | Advance by 1 after each terrain zone, major delay, or failed group decision. At 8, dawn comes. |
| **Stag's Lead** | `3` zones behind | Reduce by 1 when the party is delayed, takes a loud shortcut, or fails a danger. Increase by 1 when it loses the trail or the party makes a clever reversal. At 0, the Stag enters the current zone. |
| **Sport** | `0/3` | Gain 1 when the party displays speed, cleverness, or selfless teamwork in a memorable way. At 3, the Stag is fully entertained and will honor a close finish generously. |

### Capture Instead of Death

When the Stag reaches a character, run a short capture challenge rather than an instant removal: two successes before two failures using movement, magic, a rescue, or terrain. On failure, the character becomes a pendant.

While pendant-bound, a character can once per zone offer one brief sensory clue, distract a lesser fey, or grant advantage to a rescuer who can hear them. A free racer can recover a pendant by taking it from the Stag, making the Stag drop it, or reaching the Dawnroot Bell. No player should sit out for long.

### Level-Appropriate Stag Form

For a level-10 party, use the Stag's chosen mortal shape as a mobile recurring threat, not a damage engine. The shapeshifter's true form is not a combat target:

- AC and saving throws comparable to a tough CR 9-10 creature.
- One meaningful attack or grapple per round, never a devastating multiattack routine.
- Excellent speed, climbing, and tracking; poor performance against clever barriers, prepared ambushes, and formally invoked rules.
- It withdraws after a capture attempt, a dramatic terrain reversal, or an impressive use of the environment. It wants the race to continue.

## Map Overview

Draw the race as eight connected zones. Each zone should be one encounter map, skill challenge, or fast tactical scene. Paths occasionally branch, but all reconnect before Zone 8.

```text
Start: Ruined Hearth
        |
1. Lantern Ashes -- 2. Splitroot Drop -- 3. Mothglass Fen
                                      |             |
                              4A. High Canopy   4B. Underroot Flood
                                      \             /
                                   5. Fang Cairn
                                         |
                                   6. Thorn Maze
                                         |
                                   7. Mirror Run
                                         |
                                   8. Dawnroot Bell
```

The party chooses either 4A or 4B. Use the unchosen route as a consequence, shortcut, or rescue option later.

## Zone Details

### Start: The Ruined Hearth

**Map:** A broken village square under permanent violet dusk: collapsed homes, a dry well, a half-burned cooking circle, and root walls where the forest has grown through memory. The Hearth-Ember floats inside a stone brazier.

**Scene:** The Stag introduces the race. Moonpaw sees one amber pendant brighten with his wife and child inside; give him a short, player-led exchange before the first bell.

**Obstacle:** The ember cannot be carried in an ordinary container. It needs a willing bearer, and it dims whenever that bearer abandons a companion in immediate danger.

**Choices:**

- Choose a bearer and appoint a backup.
- Ask the Stag one question about the course; it answers truthfully but poetically.
- Spend time searching the village echo for a keepsake. Gain a family clue, but advance the Dawn Clock once.

**Stag behavior:** It starts three zones behind and watches. No combat here.

### Zone 1: Lantern Ashes

**Map:** Narrow lanes of ash-covered roots, hanging paper lanterns, and knee-high ghost grass. Every lantern shows a different possible path.

**Obstacle:** Most lanterns lead in circles. A correct path has no flame, only its reflection in wet ash.

**Run it as:** Group Investigation, Survival, Arcana, or fey-culture check, DC 15. Two successes before two failures crosses cleanly.

**Failure:** Advance the Dawn Clock and reduce the Stag's Lead by 1. The party still exits; do not make them lost indefinitely.

**Encounter option:** `2` ash-masked route spirits attempt to persuade a separated character that the others abandoned them. They are social hazards, not mandatory combatants.

**Sport opportunity:** A player deliberately takes the unlit path despite the obvious temptation, or uses an honest personal memory to expose a false lantern.

### Zone 2: Splitroot Drop

**Map:** A 60-foot ravine split by giant roots, with hanging vines, a broken rope bridge, and a rain-swollen creek below. Place three vertical layers: canopy lip, bridge level, creek bed.

**Obstacle:** The safe-looking bridge breaks under the second person. The roots are reliable but slick; the creek carries anyone who falls toward the Underroot Flood route.

**Solutions:**

- Climb or leap the roots: Athletics or Acrobatics, DC 16.
- Build a crossing from rope, magic, or salvaged bridge planks.
- Let the creek take the party to Zone 4B, losing no extra time if planned, but risking separation.
- Use flight or teleportation creatively; the Stag cannot teleport after them, but it can take higher ground on the next zone.

**Failure:** A racer is separated or the ember-bearer must choose between speed and a rescue. Rescuing them earns Sport; abandoning them dims the ember and advances the Dawn Clock.

**Stag behavior:** If its Lead reaches 1, its antlers appear above the ravine rim and it creates a falling-root hazard rather than attacking.

### Zone 3: Mothglass Fen

**Map:** Waist-deep black water, silver reeds, cypress knees, and sheets of reflective water that show the wrong sky. Use irregular islands and narrow boardwalk remnants.

**Obstacle:** Velvet moths form the faces and voices of loved ones. A character who follows a voice alone gains a Hunt mark; three marks trigger a capture challenge.

**Run it as:** Each character chooses a route: boardwalk (slow but safe), reed swim (fast but Constitution/Survival DC 15), or island-hop (Acrobatics DC 16). The group needs three total successes.

**Encounter option:** A **Mothswarm** blinds or obscures rather than dealing heavy damage. Fire disperses it but risks the ember; wind, music, light, or a true spoken memory work better.

**Fey player moment:** They can identify the moths as invitations, not commands, and teach the party to answer only with a chosen race-name.

**Failure:** A character takes a Hunt mark, the Dawn Clock advances, or the party arrives at Zone 4 with the Stag one zone closer.

### Zone 4A: High Canopy Run

**Map:** Swaying boughs 80 feet above the forest floor, braided vine rails, giant leaves slick with rain, and gaps between trunks. The canopy reveals moonlight and makes the Stag visible below as a moving shadow.

**Best for:** Fast, mobile characters and groups that trust each other.

**Obstacle:** The Stag's lesser fey, **Briar Hares**, chew vine rails and carry away dropped items. They want mischief, not death.

**Run it as:** Three rounds of movement and choices. Each round, choose: sprint, secure the party, protect the ember, or chase a hare with a key item. Require DC 16 Athletics/Acrobatics/Animal Handling/appropriate magic for risky moves.

**Failure:** Fall to a lower bough (lose one round, not lethal) or drop a useful item. If the ember falls, another character can catch it with a reaction or clever spell.

**Sport opportunity:** A character turns the rail collapse into a swing, catches another racer, or tricks the hares into cutting the Stag's own route. Increase the Stag's Lead by 1 on a particularly good reversal.

### Zone 4B: Underroot Flood

**Map:** Half-submerged root tunnels, glowing fungus, sudden vertical shafts, and fast-moving brown water. Keep sightlines short and sound loud.

**Best for:** Careful groups, aquatic magic, and players who like rescue pressure.

**Obstacle:** A trapped Dapplefern scout echo calls for help from a flooded pocket. It is not a real current resident, but the ember reacts as if the choice is real.

**Choices:**

- Rescue the echo: lose one Dawn Clock step but gain 1 Sport and a root-map shortcut to Zone 5.
- Bypass it: no immediate penalty, but the Stag taunts the party and gains a free clue to the ember bearer’s fear.
- Determine it is an illusion: Insight or Arcana DC 16. Success lets the party free the real clue hidden within the echo without losing time.

**Encounter option:** `1-2` root-bound drowning shades grapple and pull; use low damage and forced movement rather than a lethal underwater fight.

### Zone 5: Fang Cairn

**Map:** A collapsed raider waystation around a standing stone, rusted hooks, old cargo rings, and thorn vines writing over the walls. This is the midpoint and an ideal short-rest-like breath only if the party earns it.

**Obstacle:** The original bargain is etched into the witness-stone. Reading it gives the party a useful race advantage but lets the Stag close in.

**Rewards for engaging it:** Choose one.

- Learn the Stag's current mortal form weakness: it cannot cross running water without touching ground.
- Speak Moonpaw's wife or child's name safely at the next zone without triggering a mark.
- Learn a shortcut through the Thorn Maze, reducing one required success there.

**Cost:** Advance the Dawn Clock or reduce the Stag's Lead by 1. Make the choice explicit.

**Stag behavior:** The Stag appears at the far end of the cairn, talks rather than attacks, and offers an accurate but painful truth. If players parley cleverly, award Sport; if they attack, it defends long enough to escape and the race continues.

### Zone 6: Thorn Maze

**Map:** Walls of blackthorn 15 feet high, tiny moonlit gaps, low tunnels, and thorn patches shaped like running deer. Mark three false exits and one true path.

**Obstacle:** The maze changes only when someone lies. Every deliberate lie causes the nearest path to close; every hard truth opens a route for one round.

**Run it as:** Three choices. Each player can offer a hard truth, solve with Survival/Investigation DC 17, cut a route at a resource cost, or use magic. Two group successes reach Zone 7.

**Trap:** Thorn grasp restrains rather than damages heavily. A restrained racer hears the Stag approaching and must be freed or risk a capture challenge.

**Fey player moment:** They can invoke the rule that a maze cannot reject a truth freely given, automatically opening one exit once during this zone.

### Zone 7: Mirror Run

**Map:** A broad shallow river under a cloudless false dawn. The water reflects alternate choices: a party that left someone behind, a Dapplefern burning, Moonpaw reunited with family but alone. Stepping on reflections creates solid glass paths for one round.

**Obstacle:** The fastest route is made of tempting false outcomes. It will hold only if the party recognizes the reflection as a possibility, not a promise.

**Run it as:** Each player states one thing their character refuses to trade for an easy victory, then makes a DC 16 Wisdom save, Insight check, or relevant roleplay choice. Three successes create a stable route.

**Failure:** A character is separated into an alternate reflection. They can be retrieved with a rope, dispel effect, true name, or by refusing the illusion's offer. Do not advance the clock more than once here.

**Stag behavior:** At Lead 0, it enters the river behind them in mortal form. This is the best place for a brief chase combat: grapple, shove, obstacles, and pendant captures—not hit-point attrition.

### Zone 8: Dawnroot Bell

**Map:** A steep root-covered hill rising from the river to a single giant white-barked tree. At the top, a bronze bell hangs in a cradle of living wood. The first real sunlight is visible behind the horizon.

**Final obstacle:** The bell is 40 feet above the last root shelf. The ember bearer must reach it while the others hold, misdirect, or outrun the Stag. The Stag gets one final capture attempt if it has Lead 0.

**Victory:** Place the ember in the cradle and ring the bell. Describe the sound as every root in Dapplefern answering at once. The Stag stops immediately, bows, and honors the truce.

**Close finish:** If the party reaches the bell on Dawn Clock 8 with Sport 3, let the Stag call it a photo finish and grant victory. It values a legendary story over a technicality.

## Encounter Menu

Use at most one significant combat encounter and two noncombat hazards. The hunt fails if it becomes a normal dungeon crawl.

| Tone | Add | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Eerie | Mothswarm at the Fen | Temptation, obscured vision, and name-traps |
| Vertical | Briar Hares in the canopy | Item theft and unstable movement |
| Rescue | Root-bound shades in the flood | Forced movement and cooperation |
| Tactical | Mortal Stag at Mirror Run | Capture pressure and terrain use |
| Social | Stag at Fang Cairn | Bargain language, painful truth, and sportsmanship |

## Terrain and Map-Building Notes

- Favor multiple elevations, moving water, narrow bridges, root tunnels, and sightline breaks.
- Put a visible objective in every map: an exit glow, bell, branch, bridge, or safe shelf.
- Give each zone at least three approaches: movement skill, magic/tool use, and a social or environmental trick.
- Avoid dead ends. A failed route costs time or position but always reconnects.
- Use the Stag's shadow, hoofbeats, moths, or antler silhouette before it physically arrives. Players should always know how close it is.

## Resolution

When the bell rings, the Thorn-Crowned Stag returns to its towering true shape. It is pleased, not redeemed. It releases Dapplefern from the forest-realm, confirms Moonpaw's wife and child live in the Gloamfold, and offers the Final Hunt after the Astral Conclave arc.

Read or paraphrase:

> "You ran for more than your own breath. That is rare. Take your refuge, runners. Take your roads and your dawn. But the oldest prize waits where the paths have no end, and one day, Moonpaw, you will come and race for home."

Do not make this a forced future quest. It is a binding invitation and a real promise, not a demand.
