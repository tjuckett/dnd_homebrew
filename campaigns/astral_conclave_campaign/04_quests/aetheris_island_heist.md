# Aetheris Island Heist Preparation

## Purpose

[[Aetheris Island]] and its [[Northern Base]] are ready to become the campaign's next major arc: a multi-session infiltration and heist against the Astral Conclave's northern command, Astral Heart infrastructure, and comet-capture project.

This is not a fair frontal assault. The party's objective is to enter with preparation, accomplish a limited set of high-value goals, and leave before the full automaton garrison can respond.

## Current Campaign Connection

- The Conclave's Heliox operation has been broken, exposing its control and elemental-resonance research.
- The rescued genasi and Eldara's resonance trammel provide proof that the Conclave's elemental-control work is part of a larger program.
- The Conclave has already shipped humanoid essence hearts north, likely toward Aetheris Island's heavy-array and observatory infrastructure.
- Kira has lost her lab but is secretly rebuilding a supervised workshop in Heliox. The city's resources give her a path to prepare countermeasures for a northern operation.
- [[Stonehearth]] is the immediate post-Heliox destination. It should provide political context, elemental support, or a concrete northern lead—not require the party to complete every elemental storyline before acting.

## Recommended Trigger

Send the party north when they gain at least two of the following:

1. **A precise target:** annex records, the trammel, or Stonehearth intelligence confirms that a specific Astral Heart shipment, elemental-interface subject, comet-array component, or captured citizen group is at the Northern Base.
2. **A workable entry plan:** sky-dock access, stolen command codes, a smuggler route, a maintenance tunnel, or a short aerial insertion window.
3. **One prepared advantage:** Kira's automaton-disabling pulse, an elemental faction distraction, an insider, or a countermeasure for the elemental-detection wards.

This lets the party choose to go north while still making the preparation matter. The Conclave's escalation means waiting too long has a cost, but the campaign should not demand perfect readiness.

## What The Island Actually Is

Run Aetheris Island as a cold, fortified northern island with a jagged Frostspire ridge at its center. The **Northern Base** is the Conclave's town-sized military and industrial installation spread between the sheltered harbor, lower fortress, and ridge-top observatory.

| Zone | Function | First impression |
|---|---|---|
| **Storm Harbor** | Supply piers, inspection sheds, sea wall, hidden service culverts | Ice, cranes, armed dock crews, and cargo that never appears on public manifests |
| **Outer Fortress** | Barracks, armory, wall towers, automaton patrol depot | A disciplined military town built to survive a siege |
| **Sky Dock** | Airships, astral skimmers, flight crews, fuel stores | The fastest entry and exit point, but watched by Captain Frostwing |
| **Command and Relay Center** | War rooms, records, internal communications | The place to steal plans or create a false emergency |
| **Ridge Observatory** | Comet tracking, celestial lenses, array calibration | A cliff-built machine pointing at the sky rather than the sea |
| **Heart Array Vault** | Astral Heart cradles, heavy-array controls, essence-routing hardware | The true prize and most dangerous place to stay |

## Primary Mission Options

Do not require all of these in one operation. Present them as competing objectives.

| Objective | Why it matters | What success changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Steal or disable an Astral Heart** | Removes a major power source and gives Kira a possible way to power or counter the larger comet system | The Conclave loses capacity; the island goes into emergency response |
| **Rescue prisoners** | Could include portal captives, genasi companions, or elemental-interface subjects | Adds witnesses and allies, but slows extraction |
| **Sabotage the comet-capture array** | Delays the Conclave's endgame without needing to occupy the island | Creates a dangerous celestial/planar backlash if done carelessly |
| **Steal observatory data** | Reveals comet timing, the larger network, and Malrec's next move | Gives the party a strategic advantage without taking the Heart |
| **Extract an insider** | Orion, a coerced engineer, or a disillusioned flight officer can reveal routes and operations | Creates a living source of intelligence and a future target for Conclave retaliation |

## Key Figures

| NPC | Domain | Default response |
|---|---|---|
| [[Commander Orlan]] | Fortress and garrison | Locks down the base, prioritizes holding territory over chasing every intruder |
| [[Captain Alrinia Frostwing]] | Sky Dock and air operations | Hunts escape routes; may respect competence and preserve crews over ideology |
| [[Chief Engineer Eryndor]] | Observatory and heavy array | Protects data and the comet project, even at human cost |
| [[Vesper "Pinkshade" Arlin]] | Counter-infiltration and cleanup | Identifies the real objective, isolates the party, and retreats if the operation is lost |
| [[Orion]] | Possible prisoner, fugitive, or covert saboteur | Provides insight on ward gaps, comet timing, and Kira's older systems |

## Defenses And Countermeasures

- **Automaton-heavy garrison:** the main reason a frontal fight is untenable.
- **Elemental-detection wards:** a major complication for the genasi and any elemental allies. They can be spoofed, overloaded, or used to create a decoy alert.
- **Circle-trigger defenses:** stored spells activate for an hour when elementals approach; they are predictable only with reconnaissance or stolen schedules.
- **Shield layers and watch towers:** protect the obvious gates and sky routes, but require power relays that can be targeted.
- **Sentinel golems:** reserve defenders for the Heart Array Vault and observatory, not routine patrols.
- **Rapid response relay:** shutting down or feeding false data to the command center is the best way to prevent every zone from responding at once.

## Kira's Preparation: The Blackout Pulse

Kira can use recovered Heliox materials and her covert repair bay to build a limited **Blackout Pulse**: a nonlethal electromagnetic/arcane disruption that disables or confuses much of the island's automaton force for a short window.

- It does **not** affect living Conclave personnel, golems with hardened cores, or the Heart Array itself.
- It should not be used near allied warforged without protection; immunity to the Conclave signal is not immunity to an electromagnetic pulse.
- The pulse buys an insertion-and-extraction window, not a full conquest. Use roughly `6` rounds during a tactical breach or `10` minutes during a stealth sequence.
- Building it requires a rare stabilizer, a power source, and safe calibration. The resonance trammel, Stonehearth's grounding rites, or a salvaged array component can each solve one part of that problem.

This makes Kira useful without making her the solution to the entire operation.

## Approaches

| Route | Need | Strength | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sky Dock infiltration** | Flight cover, forged manifest, or a stolen skimmer | Fastest entry and exit | Frostwing controls the response |
| **Storm Harbor smuggling route** | A contact, submerged approach, or false cargo | Gets close to depot and lower systems | Inspections and cold-water hazards |
| **Ridge ascent** | Climbing, weather magic, or an elemental guide | Reaches observatory with less traffic | Severe exposure and detection wards |
| **Service culvert** | Old map, Kira's insight, or an insider | Bypasses the outer wall | Tight, trapped, and near industrial machinery |
| **Deliberate diversion** | Elemental or political support | Pulls troops away from the true target | Can escalate into real civilian or elemental harm |

## Escalation Clock

| Step | Base response |
|---:|---|
| `0` | Routine watches; party remains unknown. |
| `1` | Local anomaly; patrols investigate but normal operations continue. |
| `2` | Zone lockdown; command center coordinates nearby defenses. |
| `3` | Vesper identifies an infiltration and begins cutting exits or false-routing the party. |
| `4` | Commander Orlan activates hardened defenses; sky dock and Heart Vault are sealed. |
| `5` | The base preserves the Astral Heart/comet work over personnel safety; the party must extract or accept a costly final objective. |

Advance only for loud failures, major delays, or visible sabotage. Strong preparation should skip or reverse a step.

## High-Value Discoveries

Ensure the party can leave with at least two:

- The true comet arrival window and the condition required to activate the capture array.
- Where the humanoid essence hearts came from and how many remain.
- The destination of transferred Heliox citizens or genasi captives.
- Proof that Malrec is concentrating assets for a larger operation.
- A vulnerability in the Astral Heart, observatory array, or island shield.
- Evidence of a Conclave internal fracture that can be exploited later.

## Outcomes

- **Clean heist:** one major objective succeeds and the party escapes before full lockdown.
- **Costly success:** the party gains the Heart, data, or prisoners but leaves Vesper/Orlan with evidence of their methods and allies.
- **Partial success:** the party destroys or delays the array but cannot secure its records or captives.
- **Capture without campaign stall:** the Conclave takes the party or one ally into the base, turning the next segment into an internal escape/rescue rather than ending the arc.

## GM Guardrails

- The Northern Base is an operation, not a single boss room. Let players choose their entry, objective, and exit.
- Do not use every defense at once; each zone should be a problem with at least two solutions.
- The rescued genasi and Eldara's trammel should provide leverage or stakes, not make the new player responsible for the entire mission.
- Kira's Blackout Pulse helps with automata but does not neutralize people, hardened golems, or the Heart Array.
- Aetheris Island should feel like a major escalation: it reveals the Conclave's scale and moves the campaign closer to the comet endgame.
