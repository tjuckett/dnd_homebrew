# Current State

## Player Party
- The campaign currently has 6 player characters.
- The established party includes a fighter, druid, rogue/barbarian, monk, and artificer. A newly freed player character joined during Session 11; their details have not yet been recorded.

## Latest Known Situation
- The party used the Central Spire's broadcast system to make all warforged permanently immune to the Astral Conclave's control signal. The process has left them inactive for approximately eight hours, though the party does not yet know that duration.
- Heliox's population was incapacitated across the city immediately after the signal.
- Six stations used for the takeover signal were destroyed by fire. A group of roughly six to twenty humans was seen running toward the Central Spire at the same time; their identity and intent are unknown.
- Stony used his wrist device to stop a newly forming portal.
- The party interrupted Astral Conclave technicians attempting to repair the main portal at the base of the spire for their escape.
- The party pursued and freed a Conclave-held asset in the sub-basement. The asset is the campaign's new player character, who fought beside them against the remaining local Conclave forces.
- The party is currently in the Central Spire sub-basement, depleted after the rescue and deciding what to do next.
- The players have uncovered evidence that the Astral Conclave moved beyond elemental harvesting into humanoid essence extraction.
- The Null Bloom Facility remains a central source of intelligence, danger, and moral horror.
- Alden Wren knows how to overload the modified containment device, but doing so likely kills him.
- Bloomwell's five-floor design, worked-stone shell, and internal teleport matrix make it harder to assault directly than a normal hidden lab.
- Surviving tabaxi captives may still be inside the facility, including fugitives in the ducts and at least one psi-resistant detainee.
- The Conclave has already shipped weaker humanoid essence hearts north for unknown integration into larger systems.
- Tension between practical Conclave operators and ideological zealots is increasing under the strain of unstable research.
- Kira is a known expert on Conclave breach technology and helped the party reach the Central Spire's broadcast system.
- The workshop boundary that helped keep elementals out of humanoid lands appears to depend on trapped elemental power and may be weakening as those reserves are drained.
- Northern Conclave infrastructure increasingly looks designed for active comet capture, not simple observation, suggesting the harvest phase may be closer than assumed.
- The players have reached [[Asmar]], where construct civilization is split between [[Calyxis Forge]] and [[Heliox Prime]] under a fragile cold truce.
- The [[Astral Conclave]] is poised to target Asmar's mountain crystal supply and any Sovereign-era infrastructure connected to the [[Elemental Crucible]].
- One construct player character may be the lost Sovereign of Accord, but their memory remains fractured.
- The party has moved from the upper spire to its sub-basement and has openly engaged the local Conclave cell.
- The main portal at the spire was not functional when Conclave technicians attempted to repair it for their escape. Its wider history and any previous victims remain unresolved.
- Stony's wrist device successfully stopped a newly forming portal during the crisis.
- One of Heliox's original six control hubs was destroyed by Stony's feedback method, killing roughly half the citizens affected through that part of the network.
- The six stations used to broadcast the takeover signal have been destroyed by fire; the responsible party and the effect on the control system are unknown.
