# Session 09 - July 23, 2026

## Summary
- Continuing their descent from the upper spire, the party acted on what they had learned behind the throne room: the Astral Conclave had grafted its control network onto Heliox's legitimate civic systems and was funneling citizens toward the portal beneath the spire.
- The party confronted the active portal beneath the Central Spire, determined to stop the Astral Conclave's operation.
- They discovered that three portal-anchor devices were keeping the portal open. The devices also controlled nearby citizens, turning innocent Heliox into unwilling defenders.
- The party fought through the controlled citizens and defenders to reach the anchors.
- They disabled two of the three devices, but the remaining anchor kept the portal active.
- [[Malrec]], Kira's former protege and a major Conclave threat, entered the fight. The party recognized they were outmatched and retreated to [[Kira]]'s underwater base.
- In a desperate attempt to end the fight, a party member tried to leave Malrec halfway through the portal so that the closing portal would cut him in half. The plan failed, pulling Malrec fully through instead.
- Malrec nearly killed the party. Stony used a teleportation bracelet to move him into another room, buying the party a single round to escape.
- Everyone escaped, but Kira's underwater base was lost in the process.

## Portal Assault
- The assault followed the party's earlier discovery that the control signal did not originate from a single machine in the spire. Instead, the Conclave had exploited Heliox's existing directive infrastructure and synchronized it around the portal operation.
- The portal could not be closed simply by attacking one point: three separate anchor devices sustained it.
- Each device also broadcast a control signal to citizens in the surrounding area.
- This made the battle especially dangerous. The party had to fight their way through people who were being controlled rather than acting by choice.
- Disabling two anchors weakened the portal operation but did not shut it down. One remaining device was enough to keep the portal open.

## Malrec's Arrival And Escape
- High Curator [[Malrec Voss]], Kira's former protege and leader of the Astral Conclave, arrived in person. His arrival changed the encounter from a difficult objective battle into an unwinnable direct confrontation.
- The party withdrew to Kira's underwater refuge rather than remain trapped in a fight they could not win.
- The attempt to use the portal as a weapon against Malrec backfired, bringing him fully into the base.
- Stony's teleportation bracelet created the opening the party needed, relocating Malrec to another room for one crucial round.
- The party escaped during that window, abandoning the base to Malrec and the Conclave.

## Current Situation
- The portal remains active because its final anchor device was not disabled.
- The Conclave's portal network and control graft still threaten Heliox and its citizens.
- Kira's underwater base and its submarine have been compromised or lost.
- The party survived a direct encounter with Malrec, but now knows he is far beyond their ability to defeat in a straightforward fight.

## Continuity Notes
- The party had entered the Central Spire from above in Session 08 and preserved their stealth long enough to reach the portal operation.
- The Conclave's control network was a foreign graft inside Heliox's real Civic Directive Lattice; Heliox itself is not simply a Conclave-controlled city.
- Three portal-anchor devices maintained the portal during the assault; two were disabled and one remained active.
- The anchors also controlled nearby citizens, making them unwilling combatants.
- Malrec Voss is Kira's former protege, the leader of the Astral Conclave, and an immediate high-level threat.
- Stony's teleportation bracelet saved the party by relocating Malrec long enough for everyone to escape.
- The party's retreat cost them Kira's underwater base.
