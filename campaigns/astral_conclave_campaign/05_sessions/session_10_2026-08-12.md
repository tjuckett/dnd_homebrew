# Session 10 - August 12, 2026

## Summary
- The party returned to Kira's underwater base and found it completely stripped and wrecked. The submarine had been destroyed as well.
- Realizing that the site could not be salvaged, they abandoned it and teleported back to [[Asmar]].
- Kira determined that code from the Sovereign could be used to make every warforged immune to the Conclave's control signal.
- The solution required reaching the broadcast source, so the party infiltrated the Central Spire again.
- They descended through several floors and found the broadcast room in the upper levels of the tower.
- Four powerful elementals guarded the room. The [[Astral Conclave]] controlled them through a device in the room above.
- The party reached the control device and used it to reboot and recode the warforged, making them immune to the signal.
- The four elemental guardians were a Cinderstorm Primordial, an Abyssal Tide Sovereign, a Sky-Razor Tempest, and a Worldroot Cataclysm.
- One elemental was sent away to the Feather.
- The Sovereign was not sacrificed, despite the risk involved in using the code.
- With the immediate crisis over, the Sovereign chose to leave the party and lead the warforged into their future.

## Return To The Spire
- The loss of Kira's base ended any attempt to regroup or recover resources there.
- Back in Asmar, Kira identified the broadcast system as the decisive target rather than the remaining portal-anchor device alone.
- The party returned to the Central Spire and infiltrated its upper levels to reach the signal's source.

## The Broadcast Chamber
- The broadcast room was protected by four high-powered elementals.
- The elementals were major fire, water, air, and earth threats: the Cinderstorm Primordial, Abyssal Tide Sovereign, Sky-Razor Tempest, and Worldroot Cataclysm.
- A separate device in the room above was controlling those elementals on behalf of the Astral Conclave.
- By gaining access to that device, the party turned the Conclave's control infrastructure into a way to protect the warforged instead.
- The recoding made the warforged immune to the control signal rather than merely interrupting it temporarily.

## Current Situation
- The warforged are immune to the Astral Conclave's control signal.
- The immediate broadcast threat has been neutralized.
- Kira's underwater base and submarine remain destroyed or unsalvageable.
- The Sovereign has left the party to guide the warforged independently.

## Outcome
- The party prevented the Sovereign's possible sacrifice.
- The warforged have gained lasting protection from Conclave control.
- One elemental was sent to the Feather.
- The party has lost its underwater refuge but achieved a major victory for warforged autonomy.

## Continuity Notes
- The code used to protect the warforged came from the Sovereign player character.
- The Sovereign's connection to pre-partition executive authority had already been identified in Heliox records, despite their fractured memory.
- The party had to physically reach the broadcast source in the Central Spire to apply the recoding.
- The four elementals were controlled remotely through a device in a room above the broadcast chamber.
- The Sovereign's departure is voluntary: they leave to lead the warforged into the future.
