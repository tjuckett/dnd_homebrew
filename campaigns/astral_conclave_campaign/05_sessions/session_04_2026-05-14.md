# Session 04 - May 14, 2026

## Summary
- Instead of fully investigating the prepared Sandglass Relay site, the party chose to descend into the cave or fissure beside the first relay site.
- They discovered that the cave had been recently revealed by the activity at [[Sandglass Relay]].
- As they entered and explored the cavern, they found a nest of sand scorpions.
- After some searching, the sand scorpions began attacking.
- The party retreated back to [[The Calyxis Reclaimer]] and set it up to fire on the sand scorpions as they emerged from the hole.
- They killed several scorpions with the Reclaimer's weapons, but the swarm pressure built until they began to get overwhelmed.
- They started the Reclaimer moving, but the sand scorpion queen emerged and attacked the train.
- The rest of the sand scorpions are dead. Only the queen remains.
- During the relay-site exploration, Stony recovered a device from the relay that can retune their scanner.
- Stony has the retuning device, but he has not figured out how to use it yet.

## Current Combat State
- The sand scorpion queen is on the last train car of [[The Calyxis Reclaimer]].
- She has killed many of the NPCs in the last car and is moving forward through the train.
- The queen has barely been hurt.
- She has burned `1` Legendary Resistance.
- Stony's weapon is jammed until he can unjam it.
- The party is still in combat with the queen at the start of Session 05.

## Outcome
- The Sandglass Relay investigation is still largely unresolved.
- The party already has one important piece of Sandglass hardware: Stony's relay retuning device, which can retune their scanner once understood.
- The cave was not an unrelated dungeon; it was exposed by the relay activity and the disturbed basalt beneath the site.
- The queen fight has turned the Reclaimer itself into the immediate battlefield.
- The party's decision to use the Reclaimer solved the lesser scorpion threat but escalated the danger to the train, the attached NPCs, and any remaining personnel in the rear cars.

## Current Situation
- Session 05 should open in the middle of the fight.
- The immediate focus is survival, protecting the Reclaimer, and stopping the queen before she reaches more important cars or personnel.
- After the queen is dealt with, the table can return to the Sandglass Relay investigation and the unused prep from Session 04:
  - recover evidence from the half-detached relay core,
  - figure out Stony's relay retuning device and use it to stabilize or retune the command-bolt detector,
  - decide whether to mount the Sandglass receiving antenna on the Reclaimer,
  - choose whether to chase Cinderhook Relay, continue toward Heliox, return to Calyxis, or investigate the sleeper-agent device.

- The scorpion nest gives a natural explanation for why the relay crew may not have fully recovered every piece of damaged hardware below the site.
