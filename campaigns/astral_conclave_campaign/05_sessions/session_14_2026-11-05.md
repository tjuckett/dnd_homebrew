# Session 14 - November 5, 2026

## Summary

The party brought roughly ten of Stonehearth's earth elementals to Wintervale and attacked the Astral Conclave's main base. They cleared the initial base area, then either pushed the remaining Conclave forces out or forced them to abandon it while the earth elementals finished clearing the rest of the site.

With the base no longer holding, the party moved to the Conclave airship as it docked at Wintervale. The session ended with them attempting to seize the ship.

## Key Outcomes

- The assault on Wintervale began with approximately ten earth elementals from Stonehearth.
- The party cleared the Astral Conclave's initial base area in Wintervale.
- The earth elementals continued clearing the base after the party moved on.
- A Conclave airship was docking at Wintervale.
- The party began an attempt to take over the airship; its outcome remains unresolved.

## Open Questions

- Has the Conclave's main Wintervale base been fully secured, and what losses or assets remain there?
- How many earth elementals remain able and willing to fight after the base clearance?
- Can the party capture the docked airship before it departs or calls reinforcements?
- Who commands the airship, what is it carrying, and where was it bound?
- What Conclave forces, civilian conditions, and portal assets remain elsewhere in Wintervale?
