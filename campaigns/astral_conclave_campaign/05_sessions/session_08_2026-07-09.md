# Session 08 - July 9, 2026

## Summary
- The party landed at the top of the spire in [[Heliox Prime]].
- From there, they began moving downward through the structure rather than approaching from the city streets.
- They descended through roughly `20` flights in a restricted section of the spire.
- The party found a security desk staffed by a single individual.
- They silently took out the person at the desk before an alarm could be raised.
- They continued deeper into the spire by following a spiral staircase down toward the throne room area.

## Restricted Spire Descent
- The upper spire was a restricted area, but the party managed to move through it quietly.
- Their approach avoided a wider fight or immediate citywide response.
- The descent established that the spire has layered security, but the party was able to bypass or neutralize the first meaningful checkpoint.
- The security desk encounter was handled silently, preserving the party's stealth.

## Throne Room Approach
- The spiral staircase led the party down toward the throne room area.
- The party did not enter the throne room directly.
- Instead, they sneaked past guards and moved into the hallway and chamber behind the throne room.
- This kept them close to the center of power while avoiding a frontal confrontation.

## Chamber Behind The Throne Room
- In the hallway or chamber behind the throne room, the party encountered two heavily built guards.
- The party used a zone of silence to keep the fight contained.
- They took out both guards without alerting the wider spire.
- These guards were much tougher than the earlier security desk contact and suggest that the party is now near a much more important area.

## Population Funnel And Open Portal
- The party discovered that the populace of [[Heliox Prime]] is being funneled into the spire through its base.
- A portal is currently open within or beneath the spire.
- The party determined that no device inside the Central Spire is emitting the population-control signal.
- The most likely explanations are that the five remaining external control devices overlap their coverage at the spire or that an unknown control mechanism is involved.
- The party does not yet know where the portal leads or exactly what is happening to the citizens sent through it.
- They suspect the [[Astral Conclave]] is abandoning its operation in Heliox rather than prolonging the conflict.
- Their working theory is that the Conclave has decided to harvest the city's population while withdrawing instead of attempting to transport Heliox's infrastructure or maintain its cover.
- The party has not yet confirmed whether the citizens are being killed, stripped for power cores, processed elsewhere, or transported intact.

## Current Situation
- The party is still inside the spire.
- They are positioned behind or near the throne room area.
- The two heavy guards have been defeated inside the zone of silence.
- The party is about to enter the next room.
- They have preserved stealth so far, but they are now deep enough that any mistake could trigger a major response.
- At the base of the spire, Heliox's populace is being directed inside while an unknown portal remains active.
- The party believes they may be witnessing an Astral Conclave evacuation and mass-harvest operation already in progress.

## Continuity Notes
- Session 08 was primarily an infiltration session inside the spire.
- The party entered from above, not from the streets below.
- They passed through a restricted section, neutralized a security desk, descended a spiral staircase, avoided throne room guards, and eliminated two heavy guards behind the throne room.
- They also learned that citizens are being funneled into the base of the spire toward an open portal.
- They confirmed that the spire itself does not contain the control-signal emitter.
- They suspect the Astral Conclave is cutting its losses and harvesting the population as it withdraws, though the portal's destination and the citizens' exact fate remain unknown.
- The next session should begin at the door to the next room, immediately after the silent fight.
- The immediate tension is whether the party can keep stealth while reaching whatever lies behind the throne room quickly enough to stop or understand the operation below.
