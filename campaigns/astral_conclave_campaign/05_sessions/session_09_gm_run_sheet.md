## The Night In One Sentence

The party discovers the Conclave's graft behind Veyrix's throne, obtains a locked device that can shut down controlled Heliox locally, descends under a visible harvest clock, disables two different portal-anchor systems, and uses a wrist device to close or pursue through the exposed portal.

| `3` | Run the Sovereign scan: probable authority, failed memory continuity, deliberate severance. |

## Starting Position

- Five level-`10` characters.
- Level `5` of the [[Central Spire]], immediately outside the Continuity Audit Chamber behind the throne room.
- The party entered from above and remains apparently undetected.
- A silence effect covered the defeat of two heavy guards.
- The populace is being funneled into an open portal at the spire's base.
- The party already knows there is no central-spire control emitter.
- Five external control devices remain; destroying them individually is too slow and risks repeating the lethal warehouse feedback.

## Before Play: Make Three Decisions

1. **Who is in or enters the Audit Chamber?**
   - Default: [[Nemeris-4]], an archive custodian who becomes a tense temporary ally.
   - Political version: [[Arch-Calculant Veyrix]].
   - Fast-action version: an [[Astral Conclave Relay Handler]] arrives to purge evidence.
2. **What happened to the two heavy guards?** Dead, unconscious, or repairable.
3. **How much is known about the portal destination?** At minimum, leave recoverable destination data so closing the portal does not erase the campaign's route to those already taken.

## Scene 1: Silence Ends

### Opening Beat

Read or paraphrase:

> The silence breaks. The deep pulse of the spire returns all at once—machinery below, a distant marching rhythm, and the faint thrum of something much larger cycling beneath your feet. Beyond the next door, a calm mechanical voice says, “Variance persists. Run the sovereign comparison again.”

The door notices the construct PC before anyone touches it:

```text
EXECUTIVE RESONANCE DETECTED.
SOVEREIGN AUTHORITY: UNCONFIRMED.
MEMORY CONTINUITY: FAILED.
ESCALATION TO ARCHIVE CUSTODIAN REQUIRED.
```

### How To Run It

- Give the party a moment to listen, inspect, spoof, or enter.
- Do not spring an ambush; their successful stealth earned a safe opening.
- Opening the chamber advances the pressure clock from `0` to `1` when the sovereign scan begins.

## Scene 2: The Continuity Audit Chamber

### What They See

Read or paraphrase:

> A dark circular chamber opens behind the throne. Brass calculation engines ring the walls. Above the central console hangs a model of Heliox made from amber light: eight city rings, thousands of dim citizen-signatures, and five bright signals entering from distant districts. A thin violet pattern is braided through the amber network. Every populated route bends toward the base of the spire, where a pulsing arch consumes one light after another. Beside the console, a waist-high three-vaned machine rests in a locked charging cradle.

### The Five Facts They Must Learn

Do not hide these behind rolls:

1. **No control signal originates inside the spire.**
2. **Five outside signals converge here.** Their overlapping timing is strongest at the portal intake.
3. **The violet layer is foreign.** It is an [[Astral Conclave]] graft inside legitimate Heliox civic infrastructure.
4. **The portal is active now.** A decreasing counter tracks citizens being processed or transferred.
5. **The three-vaned device is useful.** It can locally override network-controlled Heliox with a locked shutdown command.

Checks can reveal additional detail, not gate the solution:

- **Arcana/Investigation:** The portal's `Null-Key Shroud` is using the five signals as a rotating timing and authorization reference.
- **Tinker's Tools:** The projector is portable, charged, and hardware-locked against command editing.
- **Insight/History:** The amber system is authentic Heliox governance; the violet graft is exploiting it rather than replacing it.
- **Construct PC:** The room recognizes a pre-partition executive resonance connected to the lost Sovereign of Accord.

### Physical Finds

- Local Directive Projector in its charging cradle.
- Live portal-intake counter and map of Levels `3`, `2`, and `1`.
- Fivefold convergence diagram showing no sixth emitter.
- Partial list of Conclave-contaminated relays.
- Portal destination fragments and transfer logs.
- Sealed Sovereign archive index or route to the memory vault.
- Concealed service-lift access linking Levels `5`, `4`, `3`, `2`, and `1`.

## Scene 3: The Sovereign Scan

The scan should reveal evidence, not settle the PC's identity completely:

- Their resonance matches pre-partition Sovereign authority.
- Command receptors were deliberately removed, burned out, or rerouted.
- Their memory loss resembles an engineered severance, not ordinary damage.
- Archive term: `Null Sovereign Pattern`.
- Plain-language term: `Root Refusal`.

Good lines:

- `Authority match: probable. Continuity match: failed.`
- `The chassis remembers permission that the mind no longer possesses.`
- If Veyrix appears: `Authority without memory is a weapon without a hand. I will not give it a city.`

Keep this scene short if the players are focused on rescuing the populace. Offer the memory vault as a future route rather than forcing a long detour.

## Scene 4: Demonstrate The Projector

### What The Interface Says

```text
PROXIMAL PRIORITY ENGINE
LOCAL COVERAGE: 60 FEET
SUBJECT FILTER: NETWORK-RECEPTIVE CONSTRUCTS
DIRECTIVE: EMERGENCY DORMANCY / WRITE-SEALED
BEHAVIOR: RELEASE / DISARM / SHUT DOWN IN PLACE
DIRECTIVE LATCH: 10 MINUTES
STATUS: AWAITING DEPLOYMENT AUTHORITY
```

### Locked Command

> Cease all assigned tasks. Release all restrained creatures. Set down held objects and weapons without damaging them. Disengage motive, tool, and communication systems. Assume a stable posture and enter safe dormancy in your current space. Do not move, attack, restrain, operate, repair, communicate, or resume until the safety interval ends.

### Make Its Use Obvious

Show a stored test or live model:

- Controlled figures march toward the portal.
- The amber field opens.
- Affected figures release what they hold and go dark in place.
- Red Conclave figures remain active.
- One dormant figure is dragged outside the radius and remains shut down.

Say plainly:

- Wrist devices can authenticate deployment but cannot rewrite the command.
- It cannot make Heliox attack the Conclave or damage an objective.
- It affects controlled Heliox, not loyal Conclave personnel.
- Placement and timing are the player choice.

### Device Rules

- Deploy: `1` action.
- Authenticate: `1` action, or complete this now in the Audit Chamber.
- Radius: `60 feet` while stationary; `20 feet` while carried or pushed.
- Full combat charge: `8 rounds`.
- Shutdown latch on affected subjects: `10 minutes`.
- AC `18`; `50` hit points; resistant to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; immune to poison and psychic damage.
- An affected Heliox immediately releases grapples, ends concentration, becomes incapacitated with speed `0`, and leaves initiative.
- Leave dormant tokens on the map as inert obstacles or ordinary physical cover.

Recommended reveal line:

> The machine cannot free anyone. But for sixty feet and a handful of minutes, it can make the violence go quiet.

## Scene 5: Present The Choice

Show the party all of this at once:

- Citizens are crossing the portal now.
- The wrist devices appear to connect, but their commands are being accepted by a decoy layer.
- The projector provides a workable local answer.
- The memory vault and deeper archive are accessible, but every investigation scene costs time.

Likely choices:

- **Descend immediately:** Faster rescue, less information, more dangerous placement.
- **Spend one scene preparing:** Authenticate the projector, learn the shutdown sequence, and identify the safest target route; advance the pressure clock once.
- **Pursue the memory vault:** Strong character revelation, but advance the pressure clock for each major scene and accelerate intake.

Do not make them guess the intended tactic. Tell them a direct fight against the full controlled force is likely fatal.

## Pressure Clock Before Combat

| Step | Event |
|---:|---|
| `0` | Party remains undetected behind the throne. |
| `1` | Audit Chamber recognizes sovereign resonance. |
| `2` | Hidden Conclave process begins copying the scan. |
| `3` | Missing guards or failed check-ins are noticed. |
| `4` | Conclave begins purging archive evidence. |
| `5` | Another citizen group crosses; intake accelerates. |
| `6` | Heliox seals radial routes; Conclave personnel begin withdrawing. |
| `7` | Veyr-Kas orders final synchronization, harvest, and portal collapse. |

Advance it for prolonged investigation, failed stealth, loud combat, or indecision. Reward strong preparation by revealing routes or slowing a later complication.

## Descent To The Portal

Recommended route:

1. Level `5`: Audit Chamber and projector cradle.
2. Level `4`: Withdrawal stores; optional supplies, evidence, or fleeing Conclave personnel.
3. Level `3`: Three control stations and portal gallery.
4. Level `2`: Open portal and three physical anchors.
5. Level `1`: Citizen intake concourse and uncontrolled reinforcements.

The concealed service lift is fastest and carries the projector. Using it may alert the Level `3` gallery unless the party spoofs its destination signal.

## The Portal Puzzle

### Explain This Visually

- There are `3` anchor systems: West, East, and Upper.
- Each system has:
  - one physical anchor,
  - and one matching control station.
- The portal needs any `2` functioning systems; the third is a failsafe.
- The party must disable `2 different systems`.
- Disabling a station and its matching physical anchor still removes only `1` system.

### Two Ways To Disable A System

- **Control station:** Phase key or DC `17` Arcana, Investigation, or Tinker's Tools to place it in standby.
- **Physical anchor:** Damage it or earn `2` sabotage successes with DC `17` Arcana or Tinker's Tools.

### What Happens

1. First system offline: load transfers to the other two and hazards intensify.
2. Second different system offline: `Null-Key Shroud` loses quorum.
3. Last-Lock Capacitor keeps the Shroud active through one final enemy round.
4. A wrist device displays `EMERGENCY CLOSURE AVAILABLE`.
5. One character maintains the wrist connection for one uninterrupted round.
6. Portal closes safely if the connection is not broken.

## Choosing The Two Encounters

Let the party choose from the audit display. Use the [Portal Encounter Cheat Sheet](../06_assets/gameplay/heliox_portal_encounter_cheat_sheet.md) for rosters.

Good pairings:

- Safest for civilians: West Station + East Station.
- Fastest physical route: West Anchor + East Anchor.
- Most cinematic: Upper Anchor + East Station.
- Best stealth route: West Station + Upper Station.
- Most dangerous: East Anchor + Upper Station.

After the first system goes offline:

- Transfer its load to the remaining anchors.
- Lock some routes and show reinforcements moving.
- Add `1` [[Heliox Sever-Axe]] to the second target; it shuts down in the projector field.
- For a level-`11` party, also add `1` [[Astral Conclave Veil Escort]] unless they are badly depleted.
- Do not permit a consequence-free short rest; the intake finishes while they rest.

## Running Each Fight

### Before Initiative

- Mark controlled Heliox and loyal Conclave with visibly different tokens.
- Show which enemies the projector can shut down.
- Point out a useful deployment location.
- Make clear that the full roster is deliberately overwhelming.

### First Round

- If the party delays deployment, let the controlled Heliox demonstrate grapples, blocking, and forced movement.
- Loyal Conclave casters provide the lethal pressure.
- Do not focus every attack on one PC before the projector can be used.

### When The Field Opens

- Every affected Heliox immediately releases grapples and restraints.
- End their concentration.
- Remove them from initiative.
- Leave their tokens where they shut down.
- Continue with the loyal Conclave defenders.

### Crowd Handling

Do not roll hundreds of citizen turns. Use zones:

- **Pressing Crowd:** Difficult terrain; risk of grapple or forced movement outside the field.
- **Command Surge:** An uncontrolled lane advances on a clock step.
- **Hostage Screen:** Missed ranged attacks through a dense lane may hit a civilian.
- **Dormancy Line:** Anyone entering the active field shuts down, creating growing inert terrain.

### Eight-Round Field Clock

| Round | Event |
|---:|---|
| `1` | Field activates; controlled Heliox in range shut down. |
| `2` | Conclave shifts loyal troops toward the projector. |
| `3` | Casters attack around dormant citizens; start casualty pressure if exposed. |
| `4` | Loyal troops prioritize ranged attacks against the projector. |
| `5` | Core overheats; radius contracts to `45 feet` unless vented with an action. |
| `6` | Veyr-Kas authorizes lethal force and accelerates intake. |
| `7` | Radius contracts to `30 feet`; previous shutdowns remain latched. |
| `8` | Core fails unless sustained with a powerful charge, spell slot, or suitable item. |

## If The Party Splits

- Alternate between groups on one initiative order.
- Do not use two complete deadly rosters.
- At each location keep:
  - the highest-CR controlled Heliox,
  - and one loyal support defender.
- Remove the other base defenders.
- Do not add the pursuit package unless one group finishes early and reinforcements can logically reach the other.

## Last-Lock Finale

When the second system goes offline:

1. Announce that the Shroud is failing but a capacitor catches it for one final round.
2. Loyal defenders stop protecting individual anchors and focus on the wrist-device user.
3. Let another PC disable or destroy Last-Lock early if they reach the Upper Station.
4. After the extra round, display `EMERGENCY CLOSURE AVAILABLE`.
5. Require one uninterrupted round of wrist-device contact.
6. On success, describe the portal collapsing inward and the five outside signals drifting out of synchronization.

## Endings

### Portal Victory

- Everyone not already transferred is saved from immediate intake.
- The five remote signals remain active but lose coherent timing locally.
- Dormant citizens remain safely shut down until the ten-minute latch expires.
- Captives already transported remain the next rescue objective.

### Through The Portal

- The party follows the captives or is forced through.
- The projector can travel with them.
- End as the destination becomes visible and the portal seals behind them.

### Costly Or Partial Victory

- Portal closes, but destination records are purged.
- One citizen group is transferred before closure.
- An anchor collapse damages the spire or kills citizens.
- The Conclave escapes with the phase key or Last-Lock data.

### Failure Without A TPK

- Drive the party through the portal.
- Force a service-route retreat with evidence but without stopping intake.
- Let Veyrix or a Heliox system break synchronization long enough for escape, creating political debt.

## Information Rewards

Make sure the party leaves with at least `3`:

- Proof of the Conclave graft inside the Civic Directive Lattice.
- Proof the five external devices converge at the portal.
- Portal destination or transfer records.
- The terms `Null Sovereign Pattern` and `Root Refusal`.
- Evidence the construct PC's memory severance was deliberate.
- Veyr-Kas's name or directive signature.
- Route to the memory vault or deeper Crucible records.
- Evidence that Veyrix could become an ally of necessity.

## Final GM Reminders

- Reward the stealth achieved in Session 8.
- Do not hide the projector's essential function behind a roll.
- The projector shuts controlled Heliox down; it never turns them into allies.
- The six locations are choices, not six required encounters.
- Two different anchor systems are enough.
- The portal does not close automatically; a wrist-device user must complete the final round.
- Keep Veyrix morally gray and Heliox's Preservation Doctrine distinct from the Conclave graft.
- Do not require a third full fight after two systems are offline.
