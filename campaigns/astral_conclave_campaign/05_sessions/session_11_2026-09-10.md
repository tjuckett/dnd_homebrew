# Session 11 - September 10, 2026

## Summary

- The party began roughly three-quarters of the way up the Central Spire, immediately after using the broadcast system to send a reboot signal to the warforged.
- The signal successfully made the warforged permanently immune to the Astral Conclave's control signal, but rendered them inoperative during an approximately eight-hour reboot. The players do not yet know the reboot duration.
- Taking refuge in an exterior-facing room, the party saw Heliox's population incapacitated across the city.
- They also saw a group of roughly six to twenty humans running toward the spire and watched the six broadcast stations used for the takeover signal erupt in flames. The party inferred that the fires were intended to stop the reboot signal.
- The party detected a portal beginning to form. Stony used his wrist device to stop its creation.
- Descending to the base of the spire, the party found Conclave technicians attempting to repair the main portal so they could escape. The party stopped them and learned that the Conclave was attempting to extract an unidentified "asset."
- The party pursued the asset through the basement and sub-basement, overcoming further Astral Conclave personnel attempting to flee.
- In the deepest chamber, the party found the asset while badly depleted. They managed to wake the captive during the battle.
- The asset was the campaign's new player character. Together, the party and the newly freed character defeated the remaining Astral Conclave forces in the chamber.
- The party is now together in the sub-basement, deciding what to do next.

## Sequence of Events

### The Reboot Signal

After the fight with the elemental guardians and the use of the spire's broadcast equipment, the party transmitted a reboot signal to all warforged. The signal succeeded: it permanently made them immune to the Astral Conclave's control signal. Every warforged went out of commission during the reboot, which lasts about eight hours but has not yet been established in character.

From an exterior-facing room high in the spire, the party saw the wider consequences: Heliox's population was unconscious or otherwise incapacitated, while a human group, estimated between six and twenty people, ran toward the Central Spire. At the same time, all six stations used to broadcast the takeover signal went up in flames. The party concluded that someone was destroying the stations to prevent or end the reboot signal.

### Portal Interruption

The party detected the formation of another portal. Stony used his wrist device to prevent it from opening.

### The Descent and the Asset

At the base of the spire, Conclave technicians were attempting to repair the main portal for an escape. The party stopped them and learned of an important Conclave asset being moved out of the city.

The party followed the lead through the spire's basement and sub-basement. They encountered and stopped additional Astral Conclave members trying to escape before reaching the room holding the asset.

### The Rescue

The final confrontation left the party nearly spent, but they woke the captive in the middle of the battle. The asset proved to be the new player character. With the captive fighting alongside them, the group defeated the remaining Astral Conclave forces.

## End State

- All warforged are permanently immune to the Astral Conclave's control signal. They are rebooting and unavailable for roughly eight hours; the party does not know the duration yet.
- Heliox's population remains incapacitated as last observed from the spire.
- The six takeover-signal broadcast stations have been destroyed by fire.
- Stony prevented a new portal from being created.
- The main portal at the spire base was being repaired for a Conclave escape attempt; the party interrupted that work.
- The new player character has been freed from Conclave custody and joined the party.
- The immediate Conclave forces in the asset chamber have been defeated.
- The party remains in the Central Spire's sub-basement and must decide its next move.

## Open Questions

- Who were the humans running toward the spire, and what was their objective?
- Who destroyed the six broadcast stations, and did they act to end the reboot or to conceal evidence?
- What is the exact condition of Heliox's civilian population?
- Can the Conclave repair the main portal or mount another extraction attempt?
- What did the Astral Conclave intend to do with the newly freed character, and what information do they carry?
