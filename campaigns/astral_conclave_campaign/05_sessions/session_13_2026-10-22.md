# Session 13 - October 22, 2026

## Summary

The party followed the [[Astral Conclave]] into the mountains and found a secret base occupying the crumbling ruins of a prewar outpost. They investigated the site despite its decay and the heavily booby-trapped main building. Some traps were disabled, others were triggered, and the party survived the exploration.

Within the outpost, they found a portal circle resembling the portal systems encountered in other cities, including Wintervale. Activating it alerted the Astral Conclave to their position. The Conclave activated the circle from its side and sent a bomb through. The party was not caught by surprise; after the Conclave blocked the portal, they warped the bomb back through, teleported to the other side, left it there, and returned.

The party then visited [[Stonehearth]] for an update on the rod they had left with its leader to power the Primal Engine, which is intended to open a portal to the elemental planes. The energy draw incapacitated the leader, who is now recovering. The rest of the earth elementals remain willing to help.

The party learned that only three weeks remain until the Convergence. They concluded that they must begin striking Conclave positions and assembling allies immediately. Their next objective is Wintervale: they intend to take the town from the Astral Conclave. Stonehearth has agreed to send twenty earth elementals to join the attack, and the warforged may also participate.

## Key Outcomes

- The party discovered and survived exploration of a trapped, decaying prewar mountain outpost used by the Astral Conclave.
- A portal circle at the outpost is active enough to alert the Conclave and permit hostile transit.
- The party returned a Conclave bomb through the portal and carried it to the far side before escaping.
- The rod's attempted use to power the Primal Engine incapacitated Stonehearth's leader; the engine's status is unresolved.
- Stonehearth committed twenty earth elementals to the planned assault on Wintervale.
- The party has three weeks before the Convergence and ended the session planning the Wintervale assault.

## Open Questions

- What lies on the far side of the outpost portal, and what damage did the returned bomb cause?
- Can the Primal Engine be safely powered while Stonehearth's leader recovers?
- What forces, civilians, defenses, and Conclave assets are present in Wintervale?
- Will the warforged commit forces to the assault?
- What will occur when the Convergence arrives in three weeks?
