# Session 15 Quick Notes - Wintervale Air Dock

Detailed prep: [Session 15 Brainstorming](session_15_2026-11-12_brainstorming.md)

## Current Scene

The party is fighting the [[Astral Conclave Sky-Captain]], [[Astral Conclave Relay Handler]], [[Astral Conclave Null Cantor]], and crew aboard the docked Conclave airship.

The Conclave intended to load a prototype **Aetheric Planar Anchor** from the nearby **Northshore Transit Warehouse** and fly it to the [[Northern Base]] on [[Aetheris Island]]. The party intervened before loading was complete.

## The Planar Anchor

- The Anchor is still in the warehouse, on a heavy loading sled beneath an open crane door.
- Its hoist chains are attached, but its weight has **not** left the floor.
- A thick copper-and-crystal signal cable runs from the Anchor to the Relay Handler's console aboard the airship.
- It is a black-iron ring machine built around a smoky geode core; it hums near portals and earth elementals.
- It cannot go through a portal. Its resonance disrupts planar transit, and attempting to portal it would cause catastrophic feedback at both ends.
- The airship is present specifically to transport the Anchor physically to the Northern Base.

## Mid-Fight Twist: Turn the Earth Elementals

When the earth elementals begin overwhelming the Conclave or when the party has clear control of the deck, the Relay Handler plugs the **Geode Command Engine** into the signal cable.

> A low note rolls out from the warehouse. Pale light crawls along the cable and through the dock. The earth elementals stop mid-stride, dust shaking from their joints, then turn toward the party.

### Range and Effect

- The Anchor amplifies the command signal to **300 feet** from the warehouse; this covers the whole dock fight.
- Each allied earth elemental in range makes a **DC 16 Charisma save**.
- An elemental with a personal bond to the party, one directly aided during the battle, or one within 30 feet of a party character has advantage.
- On a failure, it is commandeered until the Geode Command Engine is disabled. It follows: **“Contain the intruders. Do not destroy the dock.”**
- On a success, it resists the pulse but loses its reaction until its next turn.
- A commandeered elemental repeats the save at the end of each turn. A party member can use an action to appeal to or ground one, granting advantage or an immediate new save.

Run the ten elementals as **two groups of five**. Their jobs are to block boarding routes, grapple or shove characters toward safe containment spaces, guard the warehouse, and shield the Anchor—not to murder the party.

## What the Conclave Wants

1. Use the earth elementals to pin the party and defend the warehouse.
2. Activate the Anchor, locking Wintervale's portal circle and interfering with teleportation in the dock zone.
3. Either finish loading the Anchor and escape, or overload it rather than allow capture.

The Sky-Captain uses the chaos to prepare launch or escape. The Null Cantor prevents magical sabotage. The Relay Handler must choose between maintaining the elemental control signal, activating the Anchor, and fleeing.

## Player-Facing Objectives

| Objective | How to resolve it |
|---|---|
| Free the elementals | Disable the Geode Command Engine at the Relay Handler's console: AC 15, 45 HP, or two DC 16 Arcana/Nature/tinker’s tools/thieves’ tools successes; *dispel magic* DC 16 also works. |
| Shut down the Anchor | Reach the warehouse core: two DC 16 Arcana/Nature/tinker’s tools/thieves’ tools successes, *dispel magic* DC 16, or remove/break the geode core. |
| Stop the ship | Take the helm, secure two true mooring chains, or disable the lift array. |
| Stop a rupture overload | If ordered by the captain, the Anchor creates a visible three-round overload clock. Disable it, remove the core, or have freed elementals ground it. |

While the Anchor is active, teleportation and portal travel into or out of the dock zone require a **DC 16 spellcasting ability check**. On a failure, the magic is wasted and the creature remains where it started.

## End Result / Lead

If the party captures the Anchor, its marks and records identify the [[Northern Base]] as the source of prototype anti-portal and elemental-command technology. The airship's flight log offers a route to Aetheris Island and a possible disguise or infiltration method.
