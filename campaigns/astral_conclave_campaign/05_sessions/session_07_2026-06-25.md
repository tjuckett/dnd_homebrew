# Session 07 - June 25, 2026

## Summary
- The party reached [[Heliox Prime]] and began investigating the city from above.
- By flying over Heliox and scanning the city, they discovered `6` major devices or hubs capable of broadcasting the control signal through the city.
- These devices appear to be part of the system that can control or override Heliox citizens.
- The party chose not to enter openly.
- Instead, they waited until night, scaled the city wall, and infiltrated Heliox quietly.
- Their goal was to find one of the signal hubs and learn whether it could be disabled.

## Entering Heliox
- The party approached Heliox as an infiltration problem rather than a public confrontation.
- They entered at night and avoided the official gates and intake procedures.
- The scan from above gave them enough information to identify at least one hub location inside the city.
- They moved toward a warehouse that contained one of the control devices.

## The Warehouse Hub
- The warehouse was not as heavily guarded as expected.
- The party successfully got inside and located the device.
- A battle broke out around the warehouse hub.
- The fight was dangerous, but the site was not defended like a fortress.
- The device was active enough for Stony to interface with it and test an idea.

## Stony's Feedback Device
- Stony figured out how to create a device or tuning method that could send a feedback signal through the control network.
- The theory was that a strong enough feedback pulse might disrupt the control signal across the connected hubs.
- The danger was unclear:
  - it might simply overload or destroy the control devices,
  - it might break the control effect safely,
  - or it might harm the citizens currently tied into the network.
- Stony did not know which outcome would happen before trying it.
- The party had to be within `30` feet of one of the hubs to send the feedback wave into the network.

## The Feedback Wave
- Stony managed to activate the warehouse device and send the feedback wave through it.
- The feedback worked in the narrow technical sense:
  - the warehouse control hub was destroyed,
  - and the control signal attached to that hub was disrupted.
- The cost was catastrophic.
- The feedback killed roughly `50%` of the citizens affected through that part of the control network.

## Outcome
- One of the `6` control hubs in Heliox has been destroyed.
- The party has confirmed that the hub network is real and that the citizens are directly vulnerable through it.
- Stony's feedback method is not a viable general solution unless the party is willing to cause mass civilian deaths.
- Destroying the remaining hubs the same way would amount to genocide.
- The party now needs another way to disable, isolate, or rewrite the control system without killing the people connected to it.

## Current Situation
- Heliox still contains `5` remaining known control hubs.
- The party has proven that physical access to a hub can affect the whole network, but crude feedback is lethal.
- The Astral Conclave or compromised Heliox authorities are likely to notice that one hub has gone dark.
- The party needs to move before the citywide response locks down around them.
- Their next problem is no longer finding the control network; it is finding a humane way to sever it.

## Continuity Notes
- The city scan established `6` control hubs total.
- The warehouse hub was destroyed during Session 07.
- Stony's feedback device can disrupt the network from within `30` feet of a hub, but the first test killed about half of the affected citizens.
- This confirms that the citizens are not just being commanded from outside; they are dangerously integrated into the signal architecture.
- Any future solution should focus on decoupling, shielding, rewriting, or safely powering down the control layer before destroying more hubs.
