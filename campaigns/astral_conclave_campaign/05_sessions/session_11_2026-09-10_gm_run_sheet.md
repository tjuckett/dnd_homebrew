# Session 11 GM Run Sheet

Detailed prep: [Session 11 Brainstorming](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md)

## The Night In One Sentence

Heliox wakes free, its hidden resistance takes charge, and the party races beneath the spire to stop the Conclave from purging its elemental-control research and extracting an earth-genasi captive.

## Starting Position

- The warforged are immune to the Conclave signal but have no memory of actions taken while controlled.
- The shared code leaves them aware of and inclined to trust the Sovereign; they remain independent, not a hive mind.
- Kira's base is lost. Malrec escaped. The local Conclave cell is still inside the spire.
- Full context: [Established Starting Position](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#established-starting-position).

## At-The-Table Sequence

| Beat | Run this |
|---:|---|
| `1` | The control signal dies; people wake with missing time and Conclave posts begin collapsing. |
| `2` | The Continuity Cell activates the civic console; the Sovereign rallies warforged to protect civilians and take Conclave agents alive. |
| `3` | Conclave survivors retreat beneath the spire. Civilian pursuit is stopped by automated defenses. |
| `4` | The party runs the Seismic Annex defense gauntlet under the extraction countdown. |
| `5` | In the annex, stop the purge, prevent the breach-gate extraction, and free the earth genasi. |
| `6` | Brief Heliox recovery montage: Assembly begins, annex sealed, prisoners and records secured. |
| `7` | Travel to Stonehearth. End on Eldara's audience at the Heartstone Altar. |

## Immediate Leadership

The **Continuity Cell** leads Heliox because it uncovered the graft before the takeover and was imprisoned or isolated for it. It is not the Sovereign's government.

| NPC | Immediate job |
|---|---|
| Veyrix | Civil coordination and public confirmation that the graft was foreign |
| Nemeris-4 | Restore records and preserve evidence |
| Dhor-8 | Direct loyal guards and identify the under-spire route |
| Tavar-9 | Reopen intake/transit and organize volunteers |
| District witness | Validate the response publicly and represent civilians |

- The **Civic Continuity Console** opens routes, broadcasts, records, medical access, and defense controls. It cannot compel anyone.
- The Sovereign's role: warforged liaison. His call: `Protect the wounded. Secure Conclave agents alive where you can. Hold the city for its people. You are free to choose how.`
- Detailed prep: [Immediate Civil Leadership](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#immediate-civil-leadership-the-continuity-cell) and [Sovereign's Call](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#the-sovereigns-call-to-the-warforged).

### Kira's Role

- Kira stays in a sealed, supervised, covert Heliox industrial bay—not as a new ruler or a free agent. The Continuity Cell quietly gives her materials to begin rebuilding a lab.
- She stabilizes the recoding, seals the breach gate, and decodes annex records.
- Send a diagnostic prism with the party to Stonehearth; it helps Crystalmender assess Eldara's resonance trammel.
- Kira does **not** accompany the first visit. Her extraction history makes that Eldara's invitation to extend, not the party's assumption.
- The secrecy is temporary protection, not a judgment that warforged distrust is unreasonable. If exposed, make it an accountability scene.
- Detail: [Kira After The Loss Of Her Lab](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#kira-after-the-loss-of-her-lab).

## Opening Read-Aloud

> The violet rhythm dies. Across the spire, tools fall still in hands that no longer remember choosing to hold them. Amber light returns to the city displays: **CIVIC CONTINUITY QUORUM RECOGNIZED. EMERGENCY ACCESS REQUESTED.**

Make clear: citizens under control have no memory of that period; the city is free but frightened; Conclave agents are still active.

## The Conclave Retreat

The local cell retreats to the **Seismic Annex**, a concealed black-site vault beneath the former portal chamber. Its objective: destroy evidence, retain the genasi, and transport him to the main Conclave base.

Freed guards follow but are forced back by shutters, turrets, and guard robots. The party must go now.

```text
SEISMIC ANNEX / CONCLAVE-SEALED
SUBJECT: EARTHWARD
VITAL SIGNS: PRESENT
EXTRACTION PROTOCOL: ACTIVE
```

- Detail: [Conclave Retreat and Countdown](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#the-astral-conclaves-defeat-escape-and-capture).

## Annex Defense Gauntlet

| Layer | Fast options |
|---|---|
| Lockdown shutters | Force, pick, disable actuator, or use maintenance crawl |
| Sentry corridor | Destroy, blind, hack, cover, or bypass targeting relay |
| Guard-robot bay | Fight, remove override collars, shut down, or bypass |
| Seismic seal | Disable two pylons, ground circuit, or force through |

- Use `2` [Conclave-Patched Heliox Wardens](../06_assets/gameplay/session_11_annex_defenses/conclave_patched_heliox_warden.md) and `2` [Violet Sentry Turrets](../06_assets/gameplay/session_11_annex_defenses/violet_sentry_turret.md). Collar or manual-override removal can shut them down.
- No endless reinforcements; the clock is the pressure.
- Detail: [Defense Gauntlet](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#defense-gauntlet).

## Extraction Countdown

Start at `1` on entering the route. Advance for major delay or costly bypass. In the final chamber, the extraction advances only when the handler or a technician spends an action operating it (maximum once per round). Announce each step.

| Step | Event |
|---:|---|
| `1` | Annex seals and outer defenses activate. |
| `2` | Incinerator begins destroying records. |
| `3` | Captive moves onto extraction cradle. |
| `4` | Breach gate charges. |
| `5` | Gate can transport captive at end of next Conclave turn. |
| `6` | Extraction activates. Give one final player-facing interruption chance. |

The primary victory is freeing the captive. Evidence and prisoners are additional wins.

## Annex Finale

- Enemies: [Annex Relay Handler](../06_assets/gameplay/session_11_annex_extraction_team/annex_relay_handler.md), [Annex Veil Escort](../06_assets/gameplay/session_11_annex_extraction_team/annex_veil_escort.md), and `2` [Annex Purge Technicians](../06_assets/gameplay/session_11_annex_extraction_team/annex_purge_technician.md).
- Priorities: technicians purge/extract; handler protects breach key; escort blocks and then escapes.
- Equipment is in a nearby evidence locker—return it quickly.
- If the captive is freed, remaining agents flee with portable data rather than fight to the death.
- Detailed setup and outcomes: [Discovery Scene](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#discovery-scene) and [Player-Agency Guardrails](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#practical-starting-conditions).

## Aftermath: Keep It Brief

- Seal the gate; establish the Civic Recovery Assembly; begin the missing-person count.
- Do not punish people for actions during the memory gap.
- Expose only one clear collaborator tonight, if useful: **Warden Ilex-7**. Evidence shows he selected citizens before the signal reached his district.
- Detailed prep: [Hidden Conclave Collaborators](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#hidden-conclave-collaborators).

## Final Scene: Stonehearth

Montage the journey; do not add a travel encounter unless the table asks for one.

Kira remains in her covert Heliox repair bay, rebuilding with city resources. The party carries her diagnostic prism and her warning that the trammel is Conclave extraction technology built from calibration principles she once knew.

1. Granitefist challenges the party at the entrance.
2. Crystalmender examines the genasi and Conclave residue.
3. Eldara receives them at the Heartstone Altar. A Conclave resonance trammel is visibly draining amber energy from her stone runes. She asks: `Did you come to open the way home, to make peace here, or to ask us to fight another war for you?`

End on Eldara examining the evidence as the trammel pulses. It is collecting the same kind of resonance data used in the annex; removing it carelessly risks a backlash through Eldara or the Heartstone Altar.

- Detailed scene: [Stonehearth Arrival](session_11_2026-09-10_brainstorming.md#stonehearth-arrival-scene).

## GM Reminders

- The liberation is real; do not restore citywide control.
- Control victims have missing memories. Evidence, not suspicion, distinguishes them from collaborators.
- The Continuity Cell leads Heliox; the Sovereign rallies warforged without ruling the city.
- The new genasi is a survivor and a full PC: do not decide their pact, history, or companions' fate.
