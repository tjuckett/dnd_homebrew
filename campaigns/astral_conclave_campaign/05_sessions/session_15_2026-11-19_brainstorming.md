# Session 15 Brainstorming - Wintervale Secured

> **GM preparation, not established canon.** This session begins after the Session 14 victory: the party captured the Conclave airship. Its job is to make Wintervale's liberation real, resolve the remaining Conclave force without a second siege, and give the ship a cost and future.

## Session Promise

The party won the decisive battle. Do not take the airship away by fiat or have every remaining Conclave soldier fight to the death. The last command cell wants to preserve personnel, erase intelligence, and recover one useful asset. The party determines whether Wintervale is secured through mercy, force, or both.

By the end, establish who governs Wintervale; the fate of remaining troops and collaborators; the portal's status; the airship's condition and crew; and the lead to the [[Northern Base]] on [[Aetheris Island]].

## Established Starting State

- The party captured the Conclave airship after finishing the dock combat.
- Roughly ten [[Stonehearth]] earth elementals helped assault the main Conclave base and continued clearing it after the party moved to the dock.
- Wintervale's remaining civilians, portal infrastructure, Conclave personnel, cargo, and the airship's condition are unresolved.

## Opening: Victory Is Not Yet a Government

> The airship's engines settle into a low, reluctant hum beneath your command. Across the north dock, the last alarm fades into snow and smoke. Then the first runners arrive: an elemental carrying a cracked Conclave banner, a dockworker asking who gives orders now, and a breathless scout warning that armed figures are moving toward the airship from the town hall.

The elementals report: the base is broken; most guards fled, surrendered, or threw down weapons; a **relay lieutenant** and four to six disciplined troops escaped toward the old town hall with a portable relay case; technicians and coerced workers are barricaded but frightened; the undercroft portal remains active; and one elemental is injured or trapped beneath a collapsed wall. This is not an army - the local garrison is defeated, but its remaining command cell can still make a clean victory costly.

## Stabilize the City

Present all three requests at once. The party can delegate, solve one personally, or accept a cost for delay. Do not make all three combats.

| Problem | Best helpers | Success | Cost if ignored |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trapped elemental** beside records and prisoners' effects | Elementals, [[Brenn Ironfist]] | Alliance secured; records recovered | It survives but cannot help today |
| **Frightened town** emerging from curfew | [[Ithilwen Moonshadow]], Celdric | Public accepts a provisional watch | Rumors and looting complicate the finale |
| **Warehouse and Anchor** | [[Torel Greenthumb]], Brenn | Cargo/evidence preserved; technicians cooperate | Panic damages minor cargo or destroys one record |

Each good result grants one **Wintervale asset**: loyal elemental help, town volunteers and supplies, or intact technical records.

### Provisional Council

Stage a brief decision at the dock or Temple of Eternal Winter. Brenn organizes defenses, repairs, and dock crews; Ithilwen organizes shelter, healing, and a public accounting of coercion; Torel secures Conclave equipment and studies the airship; Celdric coordinates the temporary watch. Ask the party to endorse this council, appoint another trusted local, or retain direct authority briefly. They do not need to rule the town.

## Remaining Astral Conclave Forces

| Group | Number / attitude | Default action | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary dock guards | 6–10, demoralized | Hide weapons, surrender, or flee in pairs | Disarm and detain; do not require a manhunt |
| Technicians and coerced workers | 4–6, frightened | Keep equipment from failing and protect themselves | Amnesty for nonviolent cooperation earns records, repairs, and witnesses |
| Relay lieutenant's cell | Lieutenant + 4 elite troops, committed | Call extraction and sabotage the captured ship | Session's principal opposition |
| Off-site Conclave patrol | 4–6, uncertain | Converges only after a signal to assess events | Can be bluffed, scattered, or fought if needed |

The Conclave order is **preserve personnel and deny sensitive intelligence**, not retake Wintervale at any price. Treat coerced workers and conscripts individually; deliberate abusers face witnesses and local justice.

## Main Encounter: The Last Signal

Yes: run one meaningful encounter as the surviving cell converges on the airship—but not another battle for the city. The lieutenant activates the portable relay case in the town-hall undercroft, opening a brief extraction route through compromised dock hardware. Elite troops approach through the warehouse lane for the ship's lower-deck navigation console.

Show the blue-white town-hall pulse and the flashing lower-deck signal before initiative. The party chooses whether to hold the ship, cut the relay, or split up.

| Objective | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Protect the flight log | The lieutenant needs three actions at the navigation console to purge it. An adjacent creature can stop/reverse the purge with a DC 15 Arcana, Investigation, or relevant tools check. |
| Stop extraction | Disable the relay case with two DC 15 Arcana/thieves' tools/tinker's tools successes, destroy it (AC 15, 35 HP), or seize the lieutenant. |
| Keep the ship safe | Each round a saboteur spends at engine access advances a three-step **Engine Fire** clock. At 3, the ship is grounded and needs major repair; it is not destroyed. |
| Protect civilians | Direct volunteers or elementals to clear civilians as a narrative benefit, or spend an action personally. |

Use the **Relay Lieutenant** as an [[Astral Conclave Relay Handler]] with 75 hit points and a reaction to move 15 feet after an ally within 30 feet is hit. Add four [[Astral Conclave Veil Escort]]-type troops. Add the patrol only after two full relay rounds or a loud, obvious party mistake.

- The lieutenant wants the navigation log and an exit, not a duel.
- Two escorts screen boarding; two target engine access or cover retreat.
- The patrol calls for proof of authority at the dock edge. A captured officer, visible control of the ship, or a strong Deception/Persuasion/Intimidation play can make it surrender or leave.
- When the lieutenant falls or the relay is disabled, the rest retreat or surrender. Do not run a mop-up fight.

The party has high ground and ship systems, but must avoid damaging its new asset. The earth elementals remain on stone ground, block streets, and protect civilians. A partial win costs one asset—damaged engines, purged records, a fleeing lieutenant, or a shaken town—never the whole victory.

## Aetheris Suppression Field: Kira's Sabotaged Design

The Northern Base did not merely turn off its portal. A **contained anti-teleportation field** covers all of [[Aetheris Island]], generated by three linked suppression towers around its perimeter. Kira helped design the field before leaving the Conclave's work, then quietly sabotaged its shared calibration logic. The Conclave believes it is a stable island defense; Kira knows that disabling any one tower collapses the field's triangulation long enough to create an entry window.

The field rejects any attempt to teleport or use a portal **into** Aetheris Island. The destination sigil flares, then folds shut; the traveler remains at the departure point and does not lose the spell slot or portal charge. It does not stop ordinary teleportation elsewhere, teleportation within the island once a creature is there, or physical travel through the perimeter. The captured airship can therefore fly in, but it becomes the party's only reliable way to reach the island until a tower is disabled.

| Tower | Position | Defense / opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| **Stormbreak Tower** | Northwest sea cliff | Exposed to wind and reachable by a daring airship drop; guarded by a small sky-watch crew. |
| **Frostspire Tower** | High ridge above the observatory | Hard climb and severe weather; closest to the field's master calibration. |
| **Harbor Tower** | East seawall above Storm Harbor | Best access for a disguised airship or cargo landing; busiest and most socially guarded. |

Disabling one tower requires reaching its control chamber and either destroying the suppression core (AC 17, 60 HP; resistant to nonmagical weapon damage), completing two DC 16 Arcana/tinker's tools checks, or applying Kira's sabotaged calibration sequence. On success, the field fails for **one hour** before the Conclave can reroute power through the remaining two towers. That window allows teleportation into the island and creates a noticeable but localized alert; it is an opportunity, not a quiet permanent bypass.

Torel can recognize the system from recovered parts, but only Kira, a captured Conclave engineer, or the airship's records can identify the sabotage as intentional. Let the party learn that a single tower is enough to open the field before they arrive, so the choice is whether to exploit the airship cover, strike a tower first, or use the field failure as a diversion.

## Portal Decision

After the Last Signal, put the town-hall circle's future to the party.

| Choice | Benefit | Complication |
|---|---|---|
| **Seal it** | No immediate Conclave transit | Loses a route for signals and misinformation |
| **Guard and study it** | Can trace signals and learn about the island's suppression network | Requires watches; carries infiltration risk |
| **Use it once** | Send misinformation, demand terms, or trace the lieutenant | Clearly tells the Conclave Wintervale is lost; the suppression field still blocks entry |

The town hall and airship both need people to run them. This is a practical reason to recruit locals, elementals, or a defector.

## The Captured Airship and Next Lead

The vessel can fly and defend itself, but needs a pilot/helmsman, engineer, and three or four crew hands for safe long travel. Brenn can organize repairs, Torel can stabilize unfamiliar systems, and a surrendered technician or defector can provide key knowledge. Its transponder and route codes offer one plausible approach to the Northern Base before the Conclave replaces them.

The cargo hold contains flight records, a partial manifest, and evidence that the **Aetheric Planar Anchor** is the comet-capture array's missing stabilizer. Without it, the Conclave can still channel the comet's energy, but it will take significantly longer and carries a much higher chance of a dangerous surge, failed capture, or damage to the array. Confirm one actionable fact: the base expects the ship in **four days**. The captured route is now more valuable because Aetheris's suppression field blocks teleportation into the island. The party can fly under the ship's identity, send a false report, or use the delay to prepare another route.

## End State: Wintervale Is Free

> Before nightfall, Wintervale's old Conclave signal is dark. The captured airship hangs over a dock guarded by stone and lanternlight. At the Temple steps, names are read—not as curfew lists, but as people returning home.

On main-objective success, Wintervale has a provisional council; the organized garrison has surrendered, scattered, or been defeated; the party has set a policy for workers, prisoners, and collaborators; the portal is sealed, guarded, or deliberately used; and the party owns an airship with a four-day Northern Base lead. The next session can leave Wintervale deliberately rather than abandon an unresolved occupation.
