# Session 14 GM Run Sheet - The Crystal Run

Detailed prep: [Session 14 Brainstorming](session_14_2026-11-05_brainstorming.md)

## The Night in One Sentence

The party raids the docked Conclave airship at Wintervale's north-side air dock before it can depart with unstable bulk crystals, while Celdric and twenty earth elementals hold the wider battlefield together.

## Starting Position

- The party has **three weeks** until the Convergence.
- [[Stonehearth]] supplied twenty earth elementals; use them as four five-elemental battlefield teams, not individual combatants.
- **Celdric** is inside Wintervale scouting and will join the fight.
- The ocean lies north and west; regular docks sit northwest; the larger air dock is immediately north of town beside them.
- The airship is docked at a two-story open stone structure with a central column, an upper boarding deck, and a lower boarding deck. It has a balloon and is readying for departure.
- The Conclave can send only prepared small objects through its cargo portal. It needs the airship for bulk crystals, people, equipment, and lift-crystal recharge.
- Two visible ward nodes form an **Aetheric Quarantine Lattice**, preventing the party from forcing enemies through the ship's portal controls without first defeating or spoofing the defense.
- A Sky-Captain commands the airship and its deck batteries. Disabling both batteries also drops the Sky-Captain's command ward.
- The airship uses a visible Power Triad: Aegis, Arsenal, or Lift. The Sky-Captain chooses one at initiative 20 and cannot select the same mode twice in a row; the party can seize the console and force the next mode.

## Before Play: Choose Four Scout Facts and One Uncertainty

Give these through Celdric before initiative. Pick four.

- Two of the four mooring chains are the real launch locks.
- The crane is also a shore-power link for the ship.
- A locked warehouse contains the final cargo or possible prisoners.
- A relay beacon will call reinforcements or contact the portal network.
- The commander has emergency-transit capability.
- A lower-hold maintenance route enters from below the waterfront.
- Aerial scouts create a brief attack window.

**Uncertainty:** the most protected crate could be a weapon, prisoner transport, or decoy.

## Opening Read-Aloud

> Salt wind cuts across Wintervale's northern shore. Beyond the regular docks, the Conclave airship hangs against its mooring chains beside the two-tiered stone airdock, balloon taut and breathing with pale light. Below, a crane eases a sealed crystal housing toward the lower deck. Tiny packets wink out one by one through a small cargo aperture in the central column. Then Celdric's signal reaches you: *They are almost ready to fly.*

## Session Flow

| Beat | Run this |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Celdric delivers the scout briefing. Let the players pick their approach and assign the four earth-elemental teams. |
| 2 | Resolve one opening move: lower-hold infiltration, upper-deck gantry assault, false cargo delivery, magic/flight, or an elemental diversion. |
| 3 | Start the dock battle. Show the Launch, Crystal Denial, and Emergency Extraction clocks. |
| 4 | Run the Conclave's logistical response: launch first, extraction second, records/cargo denial only when necessary. |
| 5 | Resolve the airship's fate: captured, grounded, escaped damaged, or destroyed. |
| 6 | Give one concrete intelligence reward and show the three-week Convergence clock still moving. |

## Allied Assignments

| Team | Default job | What it achieves |
|---|---|---|
| Road Team | Seal south/east streets | Blocks town reinforcements |
| Yard Team | Hold the waterfront yard and warehouse | Protects civilians/cargo and prevents encirclement |
| Beacon Team | Isolate the relay beacon | Delays portal/signal reinforcements |
| Reserve Team | Go where the party directs | Brace crane, breach guard post, stabilize collapse, or cover retreat |

Each team acts on one shared initiative. Its turn is one battlefield task: hold, block, escort, breach, or stabilize.

## Enemy Intent

The Conclave is moving three bulk-crystal housings while sending refined crystal wafers through a small cargo portal. Its priorities are:

1. launch the airship;
2. preserve the calibration records and enough crystal feedstock to matter;
3. extract personnel if launch fails;
4. deny a housing only if it cannot be saved.

They do not want to destroy Wintervale or the air dock. They need both.

## Round Guidance

| Round | Conclave action |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Escorts screen boarding routes. Technicians advance Launch. Magister controls the upper deck; Cantor holds counterspell or uses *slow*. |
| 2 | Ship fires a deck-battery action. Technicians retract gangplank / advance Launch or prime one housing. Handler begins Extraction if threatened. |
| 3+ | If launch works, depart. If stopped, open extraction. If both fail, bargain, surrender, or burn only the records. |

Never use forced movement to drop a character without clear warning and a catch, net, rigging, or meaningful save. The danger is pressure and height, not a cheap instant death.

## Dock Controls

| Objective | Player-facing solution |
|---|---|
| Prevent launch | Secure two true mooring locks, seize the helm, or disrupt the lift array |
| Stop reinforcement | Disable or occupy the relay beacon |
| Save cargo | Stabilize a primed housing with an action and an appropriate Arcana/tool/spell solution |
| Stop extraction | Incapacitate the Handler, break concentration, or seize the relay focus |
| Bypass portal defense | Disable both ward nodes (AC 15, 25 HP each) or spoof a seal at the console (DC 15 Arcana/thieves' tools/tinker's tools) |
| Stop ship artillery | Disable both upper-deck batteries (AC 16, 40 HP each; DC 16 Arcana/thieves' tools/tinker's tools) |
| Seize power routing | Lower-deck central console; DC 16 Arcana/thieves' tools/tinker's tools chooses and locks next round's mode |
| Board safely | Lower deck = safer but slower; upper deck = faster but exposed |

Use DC 15 for a straightforward control, tool, or Arcana attempt; lower it to DC 13 if Celdric supplied exactly the needed fact. Reward inventive spells and class features without demanding a fixed solution.

## Captured Commander / Loot Leads

Give one, not all, unless the party secures multiple objectives.

- Flight plan with the next safe refueling site or Conclave relay.
- Calibration ledger showing the shipment feeds a larger Conclave operation tied to the Convergence.
- Intact bulk-crystal housing as evidence, resource, or bargaining chip.
- A prisoner or coerced dockworker who knows local Conclave routines.
- A portal-key component that makes a later infiltration easier.

## End States

| Result | Immediate consequence |
|---|---|
| Capture ship | Major asset, but it needs a crew, repairs, and a secure berth. |
| Ground ship | Town and cargo secured; important Conclave personnel may escape. |
| Ship escapes damaged | Party gains a pursuit lead, rescued people, or stolen records. |
| Ship destroyed | Conclave loses the asset; Wintervale loses dock infrastructure and some intelligence. |

## GM Reminders

- Let Celdric contribute once decisively, then keep him as an abstract ally rather than a sixth PC.
- Let the earth elementals own the perimeter; keep the player characters on the airship objective.
- Advance only one enemy clock per Conclave round.
- The Conclave agents retreat, bargain, or surrender when the mission is untenable. They do not fight to the last hit point by default.
- The Aetheric Quarantine Lattice blocks only forced use of the ship's portal infrastructure. It does not negate the party's ordinary teleport spells.
- In Arsenal mode, the ship takes one Deck System Action on initiative count 20 while the Sky-Captain can direct it. Use Aether Broadside or Gravitic Suppression.
- Put a physical marker on **Aegis**, **Arsenal**, or **Lift**. Announce the intended next mode at the end of the prior Conclave turn. The Captain cannot choose the same mode twice in a row.
- The Magister and Cantor are elite casters for this encounter. The Magister's *chain lightning* is a one-time panic button; use it against a meaningful cluster, not as surprise punishment. Use the Cantor's *wall of force* to divide choices or protect an objective, not to remove a player from the fight.
- Do not disclose the shipment's final destination unless the party earns that answer.
