## Characters To Consider
Use, rename, or ignore these depending on where Session 09 ended.

### Ilyra Vossane
- Role: Astral Conclave defector or reluctant logistics officer.
- Use if:
  - the party needs a living person who knows where transferred citizens were sent,
  - or someone has to explain the Conclave's extraction chain from the inside.
- Demeanor:
  - precise, frightened, bitterly practical.
- Knows:
  - portal destination labels,
  - transfer categories,
  - which cargo was citizens and which was equipment,
  - and the name of one higher Conclave handler.
- Wants:
  - protection from Veyr-Kas,
  - proof that defecting will actually matter,
  - or safe passage out of Heliox.
- Complication:
  - she helped move people before she understood they were being harvested.

### Tavar-9
- Role: Heliox intake supervisor, recently freed from control or partially resistant.
- Use if:
  - the party needs a Heliox witness who was inside the intake process,
  - or a moral face for the citizens nearly taken.
- Demeanor:
  - orderly, shaken, ashamed of what their hands did under command.
- Knows:
  - how citizens were sorted,
  - which intake lanes led to the portal,
  - and which official orders were real Heliox procedure versus Conclave graft.
- Wants:
  - to account for every missing citizen,
  - and to testify before someone who can still act.
- Complication:
  - other Heliox citizens may blame Tavar for obeying the intake orders.

### Nemeris-4
- Role: archive custodian and continuity records specialist.
- Use if:
  - the party needs access to records about the construct PC,
  - the `Root Refusal`/`Null Sovereign Pattern`,
  - or the route to a memory vault.
- Demeanor:
  - cautious, meticulous, quietly brave when evidence is at stake.
- Knows:
  - pre-partition executive resonance records,
  - which archives were purged,
  - and what backup indices might have survived.
- Wants:
  - preservation of truthful records,
  - and proof before making accusations against Heliox leadership.
- Complication:
  - their access credentials may now be revoked or marked compromised.

### Merrow-Glass
- Role: Conclave portal technician left behind, injured, or trapped.
- Use if:
  - the party needs a hostile prisoner,
  - quick answers under pressure,
  - or a way to understand the portal machinery.
- Demeanor:
  - arrogant until cornered, then very survival-oriented.
- Knows:
  - emergency portal closure procedures,
  - where a collapsed portal tries to reconnect,

- Wants:
  - not to be handed over to Heliox,

- Complication:
  - they may carry a delayed purge phrase or tracking crystal.

## Possible Session Shapes
### If The Party Closed The Portal
- Deal with the immediate Heliox aftermath.
- Account for missing citizens.
- Protect evidence from Conclave cleanup.
- Decide whether to pursue the captives, the memory vault, or Veyr-Kas.

### If The Party Went Through The Portal
- Open in the destination facility.
- Establish where the captives are being taken.
- Let the party decide between escape, sabotage, and rescue.
- Introduce a Conclave character who can explain the extraction site.

### If The Portal Stayed Open
- Make Session 10 a desperate containment and rescue session.
- Heliox is unstable, citizens are still at risk, and the party must choose what can still be saved.
