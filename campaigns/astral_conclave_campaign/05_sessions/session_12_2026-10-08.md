# Session 12 - October 8, 2026

## Summary

In the aftermath of the rescue beneath the Central Spire, the party carried the incapacitated Sovereign in a bag of holding while the warforged reboot ran its course. The Sovereign remained incapacitated for eight hours.

Immediately after freeing the Conclave-held asset, the party saw someone dragging an object across the floor, triggering it, and fleeing. Their investigation revealed a massive bomb intended to level Heliox. Stony disabled the device before it could detonate.

The party returned to the solar farm to retrieve their train, then pursued the bomb team's trail into the desert. At a rock outcropping, they determined the fugitives had entered a portal and escaped; its destination remains unknown.

The party then crossed the desert and returned to Calyxis. There, they found that the town had also been incapacitated, but was now immune to the Astral Conclave's control just as Heliox is.

After discussion of the party's artifacts, the group was permitted to keep two of them. Their identities and capabilities have not yet been recorded.

The Sovereign chose to remain behind to lead their people. They pledged that their people would aid the fight against the Astral Conclave when they were able. The active adventuring party then chose to enter the mountains and follow a trail left by the Astral Conclave.

## Key Outcomes

- A city-level bomb in Heliox was found and disabled by Stony.
- The bomb team's escape route led through a portal at a desert rock outcropping.
- Calyxis was found incapacitated but newly immune to Conclave control.
- The party retained two artifacts.
- The Sovereign left the adventuring party to lead their people and became a prospective ally against the Astral Conclave.
- The party ended the session heading into the mountains on an Astral Conclave trail.

## Open Questions

- Where did the bomb team go through the desert portal?
- What caused Calyxis's incapacitation, and how extensive is its new immunity?
- Which two artifacts did the party retain, and what can they do?
- Where does the Astral Conclave's mountain trail lead?
