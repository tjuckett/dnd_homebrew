### Clock Counters

- **Launch:** hold two true mooring locks, take helm, or disable lift array.

## Aetheric Quarantine Lattice

**Purpose:** The Conclave's response to the party using portals to forcibly remove or destroy people. It protects the airship's cargo aperture and deck-side transit controls only.

- **Effect:** A creature forced into a protected portal is shunted to the nearest unoccupied space on the same side. It does not cross and takes no shunt damage. A person-sized portal cannot be opened through the protected controls without a Conclave seal.
- **Does not block:** normal movement, attacks, the party's personal teleports, or unrelated portals elsewhere.
- **Ward nodes (two):** AC 15; 25 HP; immune poison and psychic; resistant to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing.
- **Disable:** adjacent action; DC 15 Arcana, thieves' tools, or tinker's tools. Stony or use of Celdric's exact scout fact grants advantage. Both nodes disabled = lattice down.
- **Spoof:** DC 15 check at the transit console allows one deliberate creature transit for one round.
- **Enemy repair:** Relay Handler can spend an action at the central-column console to reactivate one disabled node.

## Aether Deck Batteries (Ship System)

Two batteries are recessed into the airship's upper-deck rails. They can fire only while the **Arsenal** Power Triad mode is active.

**Each battery:** AC 16; 40 HP; immune poison and psychic; resistant to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing. Adjacent action to disable: DC 16 Arcana, thieves' tools, or tinker's tools. A technician can reactivate one with an action. If both are disabled, the Sky-Captain's Airship Command Ward fails.

| Deck System Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Aether Broadside** | Point within 120 ft.; Conclave-chosen creatures in 15-ft. radius make Dex save. Two batteries: DC 16, 27 (6d8) force/lightning, half on success. One battery: DC 15, 18 (4d8), half on success. Failure also pushes 10 ft. |
| **Gravitic Suppression** | One 20-ft. square on gantry/deck becomes difficult terrain until initiative 20 next round. Hostile creatures beginning there make DC 15 Str save or speed becomes 0 until start of next turn. |

## Power Triad

Place a marker on the active mode. At **initiative count 20**, the Sky-Captain chooses the mode; it cannot choose the same mode twice in a row. Announce its intended next mode at the end of the preceding Conclave turn.

| Active mode | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Aegis** | Quarantine Lattice functions. Sky-Captain gains +2 AC until next initiative 20. No Deck System Action; no Lift advance from ship power. |
| **Arsenal** | Ship takes one Deck System Action. Quarantine Lattice is suppressed; no Aegis AC bonus. |
| **Lift** | Advance Launch one segment. Quarantine Lattice is suppressed; no Deck System Action. |

**Override console:** Lower deck, central column. Adjacent action; DC 16 Arcana, thieves' tools, or tinker's tools. Success chooses and locks the next initiative-20 mode; Sky-Captain cannot override it. Stony has advantage. Failure alerts the Sky-Captain but causes no damage.

## Conclave Round Card

**Round 1:** Veil Screen at gangplank. Technicians work Launch. Magister controls upper deck. Cantor reserves *counterspell* or casts *slow*.

**Round 2:** Advance Launch or prime one housing. Handler begins Extraction if threatened. Magister uses Vector Step to pull a key ally away from grapples.

**Round 3+:** Launch if possible. If grounded, Extraction. If neither works, offer withdrawal / surrender or burn records only.

## Crystal-Housing Surge

When primed, visibly describe static/frost, rising hum, and metal tugging toward the housing. On the next enemy turn, if not stabilized, it releases a **15-foot contained force/lightning surge** and leaves crystal-fragment difficult terrain. It is not a town-destroying explosion.

## Safety / Fairness

- Forced movement goes toward deck center, cargo net, or rigging unless the player knowingly takes a railing risk.
- The balloon does not instantly explode or drop the ship; cutting rigging slows launch and creates a controlled descent problem.
- If the party is struggling: Celdric disables the relay, one escort peels off, or the commander prioritizes escape over Launch.
- If the party is dominating: the first Launch segment completes, the Handler moves a key to the upper deck, or 2-3 ordinary guards arrive after a relay signal.
