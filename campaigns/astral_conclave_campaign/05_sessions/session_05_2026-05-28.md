# Session 05 - May 28, 2026

## Summary
- The party killed the sand scorpion queen aboard [[The Calyxis Reclaimer]].
- After the fight, they rested.
- They returned to the scorpion lair beneath [[Sandglass Relay]].
- The lair still contained `4` adult sand scorpions and roughly `20` scorpion young.
- The party avoided another fight by holding out the queen's severed head and intimidating the remaining scorpions.
- The remaining scorpions did not attack as long as the party did not attack them.
- Inside the lair, the party found corpses that had been dragged down from the relay site and eaten by the scorpions.
- They recovered tuned crystals from a bag among the dragged-down corpses.

## Crystal Discoveries
- The crystals are tuned to different command-relay frequencies or destinations.
- One crystal is tuned to the second relay site, likely [[Cinderhook Relay]].
- The Cinderhook crystal currently has no ping. The party does not yet know this is because Cinderhook has already been cleaned out.
- One crystal is tuned to the leyline charging site, [[Mirrordune Relay]] or the Mirrordune charging station.
- One crystal is tuned toward the main city, [[Heliox Prime]], but the signal is faint.

## Casualties
- The party lost `15` of their soldiers.
- `4` soldiers survived.
- Almost all accompanying NPCs were killed.
- The only named NPC survivors are:
  - [[Selix]],
  - [[Varyn]],
  - and the captain.

## Outcome
- The queen is dead, but the scorpion lair is not fully cleared.
- The remaining scorpions are a danger if provoked or if the party returns without the queen's head or another strong intimidation tactic.
- The party has recovered practical lead objects instead of only abstract clues: three tuned crystals that point toward Cinderhook, Mirrordune, and Heliox.
- The Cinderhook silence creates a mystery and a pressure clock.
- Mirrordune appears to be the strongest active lead.
- Heliox is present as a faint, ominous destination rather than the immediate loudest signal.
