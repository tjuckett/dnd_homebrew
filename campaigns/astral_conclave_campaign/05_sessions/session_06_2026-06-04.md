# Session 06 - June 4, 2026

## Summary
- The party followed the Mirrordune signal instead of going to the silent [[Cinderhook Relay]].
- At [[Mirrordune Relay]] or Mirrordune Station, workers were still present and were actively filling crystals.
- The site was a trap. The workers expected the party to attack and planned to detonate the site once the party committed.
- Instead of dismounting to investigate carefully, the party drove [[The Calyxis Reclaimer]] straight through the sand toward the bunker.
- The Reclaimer clipped the bunker several times as it forced its way in.
- The party eventually struck the leyline charger itself.
- That impact triggered the detonation, but the blast became far larger than intended because it kept siphoning energy directly from the leyline instead of only consuming the charged crystals.

## The Mirrordune Blast
- The explosion happened in two stages:
  - an initial disintegration radius that simply erased almost everything inside it,
  - followed by a much larger outer blast radius that heavily damaged anything it did not fully annihilate.
- The party survived by sheltering inside the Reclaimer's drive compartment.
- The drive-compartment doors were melted shut by the blast, trapping the party inside for a time.
- Once they forced the doors open, Mirrordune was gone.
- A giant crater remained, with effectively nothing left to salvage from the actual site.

## Reclaimer Damage
- [[The Calyxis Reclaimer]] was catastrophically damaged in the blast.
- The vehicle has `3` major parts or sections.
- One forward section had been separated some distance away from the rest of the vehicle.
- Roughly half of that separated section was inside the disintegration radius and was destroyed beyond use.
- The destroyed section cannot simply be repaired; it has to be replaced, rebuilt, or worked around.
- The remaining Reclaimer sections survived, but only barely, and need salvage work before they can be properly reconnected and moved safely.

## Aftermath
- There was no meaningful salvage left in the crater itself.
- The party brought in [[Kira]] in disguise.
- Kira helped them salvage what remained of the Reclaimer.
- With Kira's help, the party can now hook the surviving Reclaimer sections back together and replace or bypass the part they lost.
- While the party was carrying out repairs and salvage, a drone arrived to observe or investigate the aftermath.
- The party shot the drone down.
- This suggests that someone closer than the city proper was already watching Mirrordune or responding quickly to the blast.
- Session 06 was consumed almost entirely by the Mirrordune approach, detonation, survival, entrapment, escape, and immediate salvage aftermath.

## Outcome
- [[Mirrordune Relay]] is gone.
- The party did not recover normal site evidence from Mirrordune because the leyline-fed detonation erased almost everything.
- The lack of salvage is itself a clue:
  - the Conclave was willing to destroy the entire charging site,
  - the trap was meant to kill the party absolutely,
  - and the energy routing behind Mirrordune was larger and more dangerous than a simple relay cache.
- The drone's arrival is another clue:
  - Heliox-side personnel or a nearby scout element reacted fast enough to investigate the blast zone,
  - the party may already be marked as active survivors rather than assumed dead,
  - and there may be a field team, outpost, or forward observer between Mirrordune and [[Heliox Prime]].
- The Reclaimer survived only in damaged pieces.
- Kira is now physically involved in helping the party continue.
- [[Heliox Prime]] is the most likely next destination, though the party has not fully committed yet.

## Current Situation
- Session 07 should begin after the salvage operation at or near the Mirrordune crater and the damaged Reclaimer.
- The immediate practical questions are:
  - how mobile the Reclaimer now is,
  - what Kira learned from the damage,
  - whether the party goes directly to [[Heliox Prime]],
  - and whether they approach Heliox openly, quietly, or in broken condition.

## Continuity Notes
- The Mirrordune site no longer functions as an investigation scene; it is now a destroyed crater.
- The party's strongest remaining leads are the surviving crystals, the relay/scanner hardware they already carried forward, Kira's technical observations, and the broader Heliox connection.
- The shot-down drone means Heliox-linked scouts or observers may become the party's first contact before the city itself does.
- The surviving expedition remains tiny and traumatized after the Sandglass losses and the Mirrordune blast.
- Captain Talis, [[Selix]], and [[Varyn]] remain important voices in deciding whether to push on, pull back, or enter Heliox carefully.
